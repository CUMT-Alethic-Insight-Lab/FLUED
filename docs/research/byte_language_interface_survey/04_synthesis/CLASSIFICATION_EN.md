# Classifying byte-language interfaces: problems, strategies and technique families

## 1. Classification contract and central interpretation

The 296 canonical records describe a field that is broader than learned byte segmentation. They include lexical representation, full-resolution sequence models, recurrent state, conditional computation, encoder compression, causal generation, adaptation, probability conversion, multilingual evaluation, diagnostic controls and resource accounting. The useful common question is **which part of the language interface a technique changes, and which difficulty that change addresses**. A small input alphabet alone settles neither the internal compute unit nor the output distribution.

This classification has three connected layers:

1. **Problem hierarchy:** six editorial branches containing the existing twelve C01–C12 overlapping families. The complete canonical memberships cover all 296 BIP records.
2. **Solution-strategy routes:** thirteen compact home routes, with every record assigned once for navigation. A home route identifies the principal technical object or evaluative intervention; it does not make the records independent scientific questions.
3. **Used/borrowed technique families:** the mechanisms explained within those routes, with cross-links for methods serving several purposes. “Borrowed” means an identifiable AI or statistical technique used in the cited work; genealogical borrowing is asserted only where the source establishes it. Deterministic standards and resource audits are explicitly non-neural categories.

These are **manual, evidence-informed thematic labels**, not algorithmic clustering. No embeddings, distance metric, learned partition or statistical cluster discovery is involved. The home-route partition is a reading convenience; problem labels and substantive technique relationships remain multi-label. For example, a circuit draft belongs to structured prediction, speculative decoding, probability accounting and systems cost, while a learned boundary can simultaneously concern aggregation, gradient estimation, causal timing and language allocation.

The documentary scope is 141 source entities, 140 contributing entities, 296 records and 92 distinct scoped FULL sources. The source entities include standards, one official introduction and a screened proposal; they are not 141 native-byte language models. The records comprise 283 original source-backed extractions, three audit extractions and ten field-addendum subfindings. [COUNTS](../02_inventory/COUNTS.md), [AUTHOR_HANDOFF](../02_inventory/AUTHOR_HANDOFF.md) and [CLAIM_RULES](../05_review/CLAIM_RULES.md) govern those distinctions. The detailed problem → one-sentence solution → technique inventory remains [DETAILED_INVENTORY](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md); this essay organizes it without reproducing its full rows.

## 4. Strategy and technique interpretation

### 4.1 Encoding: remove a coverage bottleneck, not all lexical structure

An alphabet remedy changes which strings can enter the system, how they are coded or how many model symbols they occupy. Byte fallback, code-point hashing, byte-BPE, morphology-derived codes and script-dependent transcoding solve different versions of that problem. Their commonality is representation engineering, not a shared generative architecture.

The [GPT-2 narrow extraction, BIW-0141](../03_reading/FIELD_ADDENDUM/gpt2_official_report_2019.md), official report p.4 §§2.2–2.3, specifies a 256-byte base followed by frequency merges and category/space constraints. Its LM predicts 50,257 merged symbols. [CANINE, BIW-0004](../03_reading/R01/BIW-0004.md), §3.1, instead hashes Unicode code points into multiple learned tables. It avoids a training-derived input vocabulary but accepts collisions, and its downstream task heads do not create an open byte generator. BPE and hash embeddings are actual source-used techniques; “both give coverage” does not establish identical preservation or probability contracts.

The route also contains deliberate non-AI instruments: UTF-8 legality, normalization equivalence and grapheme boundaries. A legal sequence can contain the wrong scalar; canonically equivalent strings can differ in original bytes; a grapheme can span multiple code points. [BIP-0112/0217/0218](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0112) specify these distinctions rather than adding three empirical failures.

MYTE, [BIW-0104/BIP-0221](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0221), uses Morfessor resources and morphology-derived code bytes, not raw UTF-8. Appendix Table 8 reports MyT5 low-resource/overall MT means 21.1/21.8 versus ByT5 21.9/23.1; main Table 4's low-resource summary instead favors MyT5 20.4 versus 20.1. The population/aggregation relationship remains unresolved. A shorter or more balanced encoding is therefore a claim about allocation whose utility must be tested, not an automatic fairness or quality result.

### 4.2 Local composition: recover useful lexical features without confusing the sequence clock

CNNs detect short patterns; max pooling selects strong features; highway gates regulate transformation; recurrent composers preserve ordered dependencies; n-gram hashes add lexical capacity. These methods address the statistical inefficiency of learning every spelling variant independently. They may leave the backbone clock at words, subwords or bytes.

[Character-Aware LM, BIW-0055](../03_reading/R02/BIW-0055.md), Model/Fig.1, composes externally segmented words from character CNNs and highway layers, then predicts words with an LSTM. It is not byte autoregression. Table 7 makes the gate's role tangible: large PTB perplexity is 84.6 without highway, 78.9 with two highways and 92.6 with an ordinary one-layer MLP. The authors suggest optimization difficulty; the experiment does not prove that explanation. The local composer also slows training relative to word lookup, approximately 1,500 versus 3,000 word tokens/s in the reported large PTB setup.

[Local Byte Fusion, BIP-0070](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0070), adds multiscale local features while retaining byte sequence length. [T-FREE, BIW-0127](../03_reading/R10/BIW-0127.md), §§3.1–3.2/Appendix A, represents rule-split word-like units by sparse hashed trigram sets and decodes through candidate scoring. Its binary-set representation need not be injective even before hash collisions; the analyst example `aaaa`/`aaaaa` shares the same distinct padded trigram set under the stated rule. This is a representational limitation, not a measured software collision. New-word input hashing does not permit unrestricted novel-word output from a finite dictionary.

### 4.3 Grouping: learn or impose shorter representations, with explicit content and rate pressure

Pooling, deletion, merging and soft assignment can all reduce expensive positions, but they retain different information. A boundary identifies an address or grouping decision; it does not tell us whether content was concatenated, averaged, attended over, selected from an end state or discarded.

[Charformer, BIW-0005](../03_reading/R01/BIW-0005.md), §2.1, uses soft candidate-width mixtures followed by fixed-stride reduction. [MANTa, BIW-0006](../03_reading/R01/BIW-0006.md), §3.1, predicts Bernoulli frontiers, approximates cumulative membership and composes blocks with depthwise convolution/max pooling. The former's soft alternatives are not an exact latent partition; the latter's Gaussian is not exact marginalization over every segmentation. MANTa's hard retained-length cap supplies essential compression pressure: §6.1 reports degeneration toward one block per byte without it.

[DTP, BIW-0007](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B05/W0007.md), §§3–5, distinguishes unigram-label imitation, own-model entropy peaks, Gumbel task gradients and whitespace rules over character corpora. Its English whitespace result can match learned methods while Finnish differs, and realized shortening rates differ. [MrT5, BIP-0066–0069](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0066), instead learns soft deletion before hard inference removal; softmax1 and a controller address distinct gate-learning issues. Encoder shortening does not shorten the autoregressive target.

Token Merging's feature-matching and size-weighted aggregation, [BIP-0160/0161](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0160), are adjacent vision evidence. They inform aggregation and multiplicity accounting, not a demonstrated causal byte segmentation method. The remaining limitation across this route is not one universal “compression loss”: it depends on content operator, visibility, rate, task and restoration path.

### 4.4 Hierarchical allocation: choose expensive updates and make their timing legal

Fixed patches amortize trunk work predictably. Observed-byte rules avoid boundary training. Separate entropy teachers allocate computation from predicted uncertainty. Contextual routers use task-trained states; score-function methods optimize sampled decisions; utility labels estimate a larger model's benefit. These are distinct sources of decision information.

[MEGABYTE, BIW-0008](../03_reading/R03/BIW-0008.md), §2.2, concatenates fixed byte patches and uses separate global-patch/local-byte shifts. [BLT, BIW-0010](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B05/W0010.md), conference §§2–4/Appendix H, uses a separately trained causal entropy model, byte n-grams and cross-attention aggregation. [H-Net, BIW-0011](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B05/W0011.md), §2/Appendix E.1, selects contextual boundary states and uses smoothing/straight-through gradients. Its selection is not DTP's explicit mean pooling. Neither entropy peaks nor a cosine router should be described as a semantic-word oracle.

Rate pressure is separate from decision gradients. H-Net's ratio loss is not a hard per-example quota. REINFORCE, [BIW-0078/BIP-0164/0165](../03_reading/R09/BIW-0078.md), provides a score-function formulation, but practical reward discounting introduces bias while reducing variance. Straight-through estimators optimize a surrogate; invoking reinforcement learning does not make every practical gradient unbiased.

Causality concerns all operands. [Patching Leaks, BIW-0074](../03_reading/R03/BIW-0074.md), Tables 1–3, detects future information from convolution and overlapping candidate means even under causal attention. Its leak-free padding arm still fails translation. Hourglass's [BIP-0104](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0104) must retain the shifted residual: only the shortened branch lacks current-group history; the immediately previous symbol remains available through the residual. Scratchpad patching, [BIW-0076](../03_reading/R05/BIW-0076.md), §3, then separates transient refresh from persistent patch count. These are linked availability problems, not synonymous boundary errors.

### 4.5 Sequence state and execution: alternatives to reducing text positions

This family is essential to a field-centered account because byte sequence length need not be addressed by learned segmentation. Multiplicative recurrence changes how an observed symbol updates state; selective state-space models control writes and retention; dense attention exposes stored positions; sparse/hash attention reduces interactions; segment recurrence reuses history; fixed latent arrays move deep work to another set of states.

[BIW-0071](../03_reading/R09/BIW-0071.md), §2, scales a plain 40-layer causal byte Transformer. Its processed-lm1b likelihood shows that tokenization is not an intrinsic prerequisite for competitive results in that setting. It does not establish equal compute or untouched-raw-text coverage: the underlying corpus already replaced rare words and shuffled sentences. [Sparse Transformer, BIW-0118](../03_reading/R09/BIW-0118.md), §7.2/Table 2, retains positions; fixed sparsity obtains 0.99 BPB versus dense 1.00, whereas strided sparsity worsens to 1.13 despite a smaller Time/Iter value. Pattern and task matter.

[Reformer, BIW-0139](../03_reading/FIELD_ADDENDUM/arxiv_2001.04451v2.md), §§2–3, separates LSH attention, reversible activation reconstruction and feed-forward chunk execution. Reversible blocks reduce depth-dependent training storage, not inference KV by implication; execution chunks are not semantic segments. Larger models fitting into memory is useful evidence even where equally large full-attention controls could not be trained.

[ACT, BIW-0138](../03_reading/FIELD_ADDENDUM/arxiv_1603.08983v6.md), §§2/3.5, adds repeated transitions inside one byte. Its task loss plus ponder penalty learns halting while holding discrete stopping counts fixed for differentiation. Wikipedia errors are training-set-only and fairly similar with/without ACT. Its effort traces are an antecedent to uncertainty-versus-utility discussion, not proof that segmentation or extra transitions improve held-out byte quality. State size, update frequency, within-position depth and stored accessible history remain independent axes.

### 4.6 Output and decoding: coherence, amortization and acceptance are separate jobs

A future window can be modeled as local autoregressive conditionals, independent marginals, a dependent circuit or a denoising block. These alternatives differ in expressivity and sampling cost. Auxiliary future losses can improve representation without specifying a coherent joint or a parallel sampler.

MEGABYTE's small local decoder preserves dependencies among patch bytes at serial local cost. [LCA, BIW-0015](../03_reading/R05/BIW-0015.md), §3, uses segment-aware prediction and a common head projection, while confidence-threshold emission is a changed policy unless separately verified. [AU-Net, BIW-0077](../03_reading/R10/BIW-0077.md), uses routing and shifts to induce different predictive horizons under one next-byte objective; this is not independent word-target supervision. Its regex preprocessing still needs actual prefix-stability evidence for an online-causality claim.

[MtPC, BIW-0079](../03_reading/R05/BIW-0079.md), §3/Appendices B–C, supplies normalized dependent drafts through mixture, HMM and tree-circuit factorizations. Expressivity has a price: the reported 16-byte rank-32 tree projection is about 730M parameters versus 21M for a factorized projection. More acceptance can still slow decoding: on EvaByte/L40S sampling, rank 128 accepts 6.02 bytes versus rank 32's 5.88 but yields 182.3 versus 191.0 bytes/s (Table 1; batch one, prefill excluded and EOS suppressed).

Exact greedy agreement, exact stochastic target-law preservation and approximate threshold/top-k acceptance are different claims. MtPC's ordinary Algorithm 3 specifies residual correction; **its optimized cached Algorithm 2's stochastic exactness remains unverified**, as [CLAIM_RULES §3](../05_review/CLAIM_RULES.md#3-mtpc-exact-source-level-qualification-not-a-software-verdict) explains. That source-level question is not a released-code bug or a reason to discard measured throughput. [Block diffusion, BIW-0089](../03_reading/R05/BIW-0089.md) offers another generative contract, not ordinary chain NLL merely because its loss can be expressed per byte.

### 4.7 Probability interface: correct the event, not merely the units on the axis

This family contains both algorithms and methodological distinctions. A canonical token path, completed-string marginal, arbitrary byte-prefix event, marked byte-plus-boundary process and denoising conditional are not interchangeable. A byte denominator expresses the scale of a score; it cannot change the event receiving probability.

[BIW-0080](../03_reading/R04/BIW-0080.md), §§2–4, treats segmentations as latent paths for completed-text likelihood and uses importance-sampling machinery. [BIW-0088](../03_reading/R04/BIW-0088.md), §4/Appendix A, sums compatible token-prefix covers for raw-byte-prefix conditionals. These methods address related but distinct questions. The latter's exactness depends on lossless canonical-support assumptions; zeroing and renormalizing noncanonical neural mass changes the original neural distribution. Incremental cover reuse reduces repeated work but retains tokenizer, vocabulary aggregation and cover/cache costs.

[BIW-0132](../03_reading/R04/BIW-0132.md) diagnoses conditioning on a canonical token prefix instead of the raw-string prefix; [BIW-0133](../03_reading/R04/BIW-0133.md) estimates vocabulary-inclusion effects near a rank cutoff with regression discontinuity. The former is a probability-event problem; the latter is a statistical treatment-effect problem under local design assumptions. They are not two measurements of one estimand.

For identical scored events, `NLL_nats/(original_bytes × ln 2)` is valid accounting. It is also legitimate to report a transparently labeled canonical-path score normalized by bytes without claiming a text marginal. TokSuite's [BIP-0286](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0286) is different: the printed CE/log₂V wording is not general BPB conversion, and actual Figure 6 computation remains unknown. That figure cannot certify equality, while separately supported option ranking and text controls remain usable.

### 4.8 Sharing and geometry: distinguish hard equality, transformed reuse and gradient effects

Tying reduces independent parameters; normalization changes logits; an adapter changes representational freedom; path-gradient analysis diagnoses learning. None determines the others. The compact alphabet of bytes also changes the dimensions relevant to classical vocabulary-subspace arguments.

[Press and Wolf, BIW-0035](../03_reading/R08/BIW-0035.md), distinguishes input/output tying, output-side projection and three-way encoder/decoder lexical sharing. [Inan et al., BIW-0036](../03_reading/R08/BIW-0036.md), §§3–6, separately tests embedding-derived soft targets, matrix reuse and their combination. Its conditional shared-column-space argument does not itself prove exact table equality or test a 256-byte alphabet. A broad rule that “tying improves byte models” would exceed these word-LM experiments.

[BIW-0091](../03_reading/R08/BIW-0091.md), §2.2, illustrates why formulas matter: `wᵢᵀh/||wᵢ||` retains hidden magnitude and is not full cosine normalization; asymmetric squared-norm output scaling differs again. Pseudo-inverse tying, nGPT and mixture-of-softmaxes remain scoped A/P background where no FULL exists. Their inclusion is a technique lead, not a decisive byte result.

The [sharing register](../02_inventory/TAXONOMY.md#version--and-axis-specific-sharing-register-current) keeps MEGABYTE local byte input/output, LCA head/head, Reformer Q/K and ELMo cross-direction sharing separate. ELMo's designated PARTIAL §3.1 establishes shared representations between directions and shared softmax between directions, with separate directional LSTMs—not input-composer/word-softmax equality. Paper specifications, static configurations and runtime aliases are different evidence levels. BLT/H-Net unspecified axes remain unknown.

### 4.9 Objectives and gradient control: a learning signal must have a named job

Auxiliary losses can expose predictive structure, constrain rate, stabilize depth, align spaces or preserve latent use. A training objective should therefore be classified by the information and gradient it supplies, not merely by the word “auxiliary.”

The temporary interventions in [BIW-0108](../03_reading/R08/BIW-0108.md), §§5.1–5.6, separate capacity, grouped exposure, boundary hints, positional distances, normalization and subword targets within a byte-resolution model. But the four-byte stage changes input grouping and suppresses three quarters of target positions as well as exposing more text. The temporary next-subword head remains worse near the end of the large-model run, with transition/head confounds; it is not a universal negative on multi-byte prediction. Offline boundary starts and repeated RoPE also need prefix-consistency evidence before deployment claims.

[Gradient surgery, BIP-0200](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0200), and [gradient-similarity gating, BIP-0201](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0201), are background techniques for conflicting objectives, not already-demonstrated repairs of every byte router. Their projections/gates act on shared updates; the benefit must still be evaluated on the main task. The sentence-VAE records [BIP-0202–0205](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0202) concern stochastic posterior/prior collapse and inference lag. They should not rename every deterministic residual bypass “posterior collapse.” GQ-VAE's quantized codes and reconstruction fallback address another content/preservation problem. This route is deliberately heterogeneous in objectives but precise about their mathematical objects.

### 4.10 Pretrained adaptation: preserve function across a changed interface

Static spelling-to-vector mimicry can cheaply reuse a lexical space, but matching one vector need not reproduce its effect inside the host. The main strategy progression is **type geometry → contextual behavior → prediction law → full byte deployment**. These are separable training stages, not guaranteed consequences of one another.

[Char2Subword, BIW-0122](../03_reading/R06/BIW-0122.md), fits cosine/L2/neighborhood/token-identity targets and then trains through a frozen mBERT. [Bolmo, BIW-0097](../03_reading/R06/BIW-0097.md), §3.2.1, matches representations after four frozen layers and distills patch probabilities while learning byte-plus-boundary output. Its frozen host still incurs backward-to-input work, and its retained suffix table makes the parameterization hybrid. Section 6.2's stage-two-only control is not catastrophic; alignment helps, especially at 1B, but additional global computation narrows the difference.

[BIW-0098](../03_reading/R06/BIW-0098.md) distinguishes byte-input/token-output distillation from the later byte decoder. [BIW-0099](../03_reading/R06/BIW-0099.md) maps different teachers into byte supervision but tests whether instruction behavior actually transfers. [BIW-0103](../03_reading/R06/BIW-0103.md) separates approximate prefix targets and marked end-of-token targets; marked-history exactness is not an unmarked marginal. Its source listing does not show preprocessing, which is not proof that EOT expansion was omitted.

[Zero-Shot Tokenizer Transfer, BIW-0126](../03_reading/R06/BIW-0126.md), amortizes embedding prediction with a hypernetwork, using warmup/overlap anchors to stabilize learning. ELMo's narrow [BIP-0293](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0293) is an earlier frozen contextual-transfer route, not byte conversion. Across these methods, inherited pretraining, teacher inference, conversion data, synthetic task supervision and unfreezing permissions remain parts of the resource contract.

### 4.11 Task utility and diagnostics: distinguish recoverable information from usable behavior

The evaluation layer asks whether the interface benefits a task, population or corruption setting—not just whether it exposes symbols. Probes, supervised interventions, natural prompting, legal decoding and exact copying have different endpoints.

[SpellingBee, BIW-0110](../03_reading/R10/BIW-0110.md), Table 1, trains a nonlinear character decoder on frozen embeddings with type holdouts and filtering. It finds recoverable orthography, but the host is not performing that decoding itself. Its separate spelling-initialization intervention produces little masked-LM loss separation in the reported run (§5). [Strawberry, BIW-0113](../03_reading/R10/BIW-0113.md) studies controlled character tasks and an added character pathway; [CharBench, BIW-0114](../03_reading/R07/BIW-0114.md) separates counting and positioning. These cannot be merged into one “character-awareness” score.

[Synthetic and Natural Noise, BIW-0109](../03_reading/R07/BIW-0109.md), Tables 6–7, shows the trade-off between order-invariant means, character CNNs and mixed-noise training. The invariance is exact for scrambling but destroys distinctions and clean quality; mixed training trades specialist performance for broader coverage. The natural-noise process is edit-lexicon substitution, not an untouched noisy parallel corpus. The source concerns character/word and subword systems, not a native UTF-8 byte frontier.

Multilingual controls disentangle tokenizer-data imbalance from model-data imbalance, [BIP-0226](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0226), and benchmark local annotation opportunity, [BIP-0227](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0227). The dialect study's [BIW-0134](../03_reading/R10/BIW-0134.md) correlations are task/metric dependent, not a tokenizer intervention. Its later positive Llama sentence can refer to information parity; the [latest correction](../05_review/CLAIM_RULES.md#7-new-r10-clarification-do-not-manufacture-a-dialect-tp-prose-contradiction) does not support a definite tokenization-parity sign contradiction. Legal scalars, correct scalars, task answers and fair allocation remain distinct outcomes.

### 4.12 Budget and selection evidence: choose the comparison before declaring the winner

This route contains empirical study design, statistics and systems accounting rather than an additional neural architecture. Equal positions control a different resource from equal original text; equal total parameters can redistribute capacity away from vocabulary tables; equal training FLOPs does not guarantee equal latency or memory.

The [ByT5 card, BIW-0024](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B08/W0351.md), §§3/6/Tables 9–10, combines equal nominal positions with much less original text exposure than mT5 and task-dependent inference cost. Table 10's XNLI is not NER, and slow sentence-pair processing with short labels cannot be attributed solely to long generated answers. Encoder-heavy depth allocation is a separate architectural intervention.

[Compute Optimal Tokenization, BIW-0047](../03_reading/R05/BIW-0047.md), studies bytes, compression and trunk size with fitted FLOP frontiers. Its fitted trunk parameter convention is not resident whole-model memory. [BIW-0028/BIP-0050](../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0050) preserves a byte same-parameter advantage alongside a subword same-training-FLOP advantage on a **3:1 Gated DeltaNet/Gated Softmax Attention hybrid**. “Flat” there means no explicit local–global–local hierarchy; it does not mean a pure attention Transformer. Its fitted N includes output but excludes input/hash tables.

[BIW-0049](../03_reading/R08/BIW-0049.md) uses actual statistical learning tools—pairwise prediction, SVM calibration and ranking—to estimate tokenizer utility from smaller-scale evidence. [BIW-0131](../03_reading/R08/BIW-0131.md), Tables 2/6/11–14, supplies a counterexample to compression sufficiency: minimum-token segmentation need not maximize task accuracy. Equal model-token training still leaves original-text exposure unequal. Proxies should be classified as predictive tools with transfer limits, not as substitutes for matched experiments.

### 4.13 Segmental structure: marginalize latent lexical units rather than equate boundaries with compute gates

Description-length segmentation, latent segment generation and posterior MAP boundaries address lexical discovery. A computational router may define useful update positions without recovering lexical words; a lexical segmental model may recover word-like units without an efficient streaming deployment. This is why the segmental route remains distinct from grouping and hierarchy.

[BIW-0043](../03_reading/R02/BIW-0043.md) uses non-neural substring frequencies, empirical coding lengths and dynamic programming; EM and hierarchical chunking are proposed directions, not implemented neural modules. [Segmental Neural LM, BIW-0042](../03_reading/R03/BIW-0042.md), §§2–4, mixes a character-spelling LSTM with a learned lexical-memory distribution and marginalizes permitted segmentations under a conditional semi-Markov assumption. An expectation-semiring penalty supplies a length prior. Its optional VGG19 image attention adds external supervision and pretrained computation.

The same source carefully separates marginal language likelihood from full-sentence MAP segmentation. On BR-phono, Table 1 reports F1 79.3 for the complete model versus 42.5 without memory and penalty, while Appendix F evaluates boundaries on the union of train/validation/test. Character types unseen in training are excluded from reported samples. These conditions support a substantive structure-learning result, not open-byte coverage or pure held-out segmentation. Oversegmentation of multidigit dates/numbers remains a concrete negative. The other segmental reserves retain A/P depth rather than inheriting this source's FULL status.

## 5. Cross-family technique links and duplicate handling

The most useful technique index is relational. **CNNs** serve word composition, ordered soft-block pooling and local causal context; **attention** serves global retrieval, latent aggregation and output querying; **dynamic programming** serves coding segmentation, latent marginalization, shortest paths and probability estimation. Their mathematical role must be stated each time. **Distillation** can align vectors, contextual states, token logits, byte targets or sequence-level translation modes. **Gating** can transform local features, halt within a byte, select trunk updates, delete positions or modify auxiliary gradients. Reusing the technique name does not make the solved problem identical.

## 6. What this organization leaves open

The classification supports a long survey without a universal winner or a single preferred architecture. It places historical character systems, encoders, seq2seq denoisers, causal byte chains, byte-complete subwords and lexical hybrids in relation without converting their outputs into one common object. Every substantive comparison should preserve **input/internal/output/supervision units; visible information; probability or task event; and the actually matched resource**.

Remaining research opportunities follow from those intersections: rate controllers whose utility survives language and domain shifts; order/content preservation under learned grouping; prefix-stable preprocessing for online byte predictors; coherent dependent drafts with implementation-linked correction contracts; functional checkpoint conversion that preserves both general and character-sensitive tasks; multilingual evaluations separating byte length from knowledge and annotation opportunity; and serving studies measuring complete scalars, exact strings, prefill, batching and tail latency at matched quality. These are open questions or thinly covered intersections, not measured universal failures.

The scoped corpus is sufficient for a serious selected survey but not an exhaustive history of compression or a common-resource leaderboard. Unknown tensor sharing remains unknown; intentional ELMo/GPT-2 partials remain narrow; ACT's training-only byte evidence and Reformer's unmatched largest controls stay attached to their claims. The field's diversity is explanatory evidence: tokenization moves several constraints together, while many successful techniques work by separating those constraints again.

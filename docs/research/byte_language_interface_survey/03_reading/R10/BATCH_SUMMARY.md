## Corrections and high-value synthesis points

1. **T-FREE output/collision:** The paper's §3.1/Fig. 1 bijectivity assertion is not generally implied by its binary set-of-trigrams encoder. Even with collision-free trigram identifiers, padded `aaaa` and `aaaaa` have the same distinct trigrams; the paper itself admits repeated trigrams are accumulated only once. This analytical example is not a test of released code. Input hashing of a new word is different from generating a new word outside the candidate dictionary. The paper reports both 500k + prompt candidates (§3.2) and 80k English + 20k German (§4.1), so attach the candidate regime to each claim.
2. **Dialect audit T8:** Full Appendix F Table 8 and Figure 5 directly confirm DI TP correlations +0.638 Phi, +0.4836 mBERT, −0.380 Llama; Phi DI IP −0.413. TC and EQA directions differ. The paper's §5.2 describes Llama DI correlation as positive, conflicting with its figure/table. Table 8 does not report effective paired n or significance. Displayed Latin-like glyphs of byte sequences alone do not establish corrupted or non-reversible text, and missing standalone characters do not equal inability to encode.
3. **Character claims:** SpellingBee reads information out with a separately trained supervised probe; Strawberry supplies explicit characters to a task-supervised model, with full current/past-token visibility but no future-token visibility. Neither is a direct next-byte LM. Their apparently different results concern different learning signals and tasks; neither settles whether byte models uniformly outperform subword models.
4. **Do not misread figures:** Strawberry's Figure 8 rescales training steps by `sqrt(|V| × k)`; plain extraction omits the square root. Its Figure 6 real-Wikipedia-sentence character-task exact match stays only a few percent despite a relative improvement. AU-Net Table 2 equal-data models differ in parameter count and FLOPs; its Appendix E Table 7 character results include unfavorable subtasks and a lower word-task average. Both limitations matter more than a general headline.
5. **Budget and causality:** AU-Net has rule-derived rather than learned segmentation, with prefix-stability required but not proved for the supplied regex; padding/truncation is present. H100 bytes/s are training throughput, not matched-quality cached decoding. T-FREE's equal-step comparisons do not equalize raw-text exposure or total compute; sparse candidate scoring adds inference work. Across all five, unreported serving, EOS, exact masking or Unicode-normalization details remain unknown; do not trigger another FULL solely for absent details.

## Cross-source synthesis for existing problem records

- **Granularity and output contracts:** ACT's Wikipedia task consumes/predicts individual bytes; GPT-2 starts with bytes but trains and predicts merged subword symbols; ELMo composes characters within word positions and predicts words. A byte-complete alphabet, raw-byte output and word-local character input are not equivalent. [BIP-0005](../../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0005) supplies a related input/target distinction, not a claim these papers use CANINE's objective. Distinguish prefix-event probability from a selected tokenization path.
- **Compute placement:** ACT adapts how many state transitions occur before advancing one byte; Reformer reduces connectivity/activation memory while retaining positions. Neither is evidence for learned shorter segmentation. ACT's within-byte effort and qualitative entropy comparisons inform, but are not the same as, boundary-utility questions such as [BIP-0054](../../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0054) and [BIP-0157](../../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0157). Reformer supplies a distinct intervention relevant to [BIP-0236](../../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0236) and [BIP-0237](../../02_inventory/DETAILED_INVENTORY.md#BIP-0237), not a new proof that sparse theoretical complexity produces serving gains.
- **Meaning of sharing:** ELMo shares representation parameters between its two LM directions and softmax parameters between directions, while LSTMs differ. Reformer shares attention query/key projection in LSH and reconstructs activations with reversible blocks; neither establishes lexical input/output tying. Invertibility of a paired residual mapping is not parameter sharing between a generative encoder and decoder.
- **Evidence asymmetry:** ACT's byte-model error is explicitly **training set**, and its selected punctuation/ID traces are qualitative. Reformer reports enwik8-64K and synthetic-copy/hash controls, but its largest runs lack equally large full-attention baselines; its plotted attention speed is not incremental serving. ELMo's inspected table supports a named task comparison, not byte generation. GPT-2 §2.2 offers a preliminary unquantified byte-LM comparison, not a matched-compute byte-versus-BPE result.

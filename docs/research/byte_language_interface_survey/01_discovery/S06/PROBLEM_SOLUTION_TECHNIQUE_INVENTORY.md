# S06 detailed problem–solution–technique inventory

2026-11-08. **48 meaningful records across 29 works.** Independent field inventory; it does not rank papers by compatibility with FLUED, source revisiting, or an earlier proposal. Positive and negative findings are retained.

## How to use the records

`WORKS.md` supplies each work's exact title/stable version, era/family, **input / internal / output / supervision units**, learning signal, causality, and source/card path. Those fields are part of every row below by ID; a row's more specific statement overrides the work-level summary. This normalized layout avoids repeating full bibliographic descriptions 48 times without omitting the required fields.

Depth: **RF** means the actual old FULL card was read, within its stated original-paper scope; **P** means targeted PARTIAL original-paper reading; **A** means abstract/identity only. No new FULL reading or implementation reproduction was done. Section/table locations on RF rows are locations explicitly documented by the inspected card; they are not falsely presented as fresh original-page inspection. Sections on P rows were actually inspected this round. Abstract-only rows do not manufacture page numbers.

The **techniques** column names AI/modeling/optimization techniques actually used or explicitly described by the authors and their role, not our search tools. Analyst cluster labels are conceptual organization only; a generic relation to another technique is not attributed as author borrowing unless stated. A systems technique such as checkpointing is labeled as systems, not disguised as an AI mechanism. **Unknown** means unverified or unreported here, not absent.

Clusters: **D** direct byte backbones; **E** encoder shortening; **G** local/global execution; **O** optimization/training signal; **B** budget/scaling methodology; **S** serving/cache/implementation; **L** language-unit fairness; **P** parallel output. Multi-label assignments are manual thematic grouping, not embedding-based clustering.

## Cross-record comparison rules for the next inventory owner

1. **Three independent training contracts:** same raw text (N03 main arm), same mechanism FLOPs (W0009/W0011/W0512), same parameter count (W0351 or W0356 arm). None implies the others. Track repeated versus unique bytes, window packing, truncation and EOS policy when available.
2. **Complete parameter ledger:** byte input/output tables, hash embeddings, local encoder, global trunk, local decoder, cross-attention, router, auxiliary training heads, entropy teacher, and draft model. Record deployment total, training-only total and active per-byte compute separately. W0512's fitted N is expressly not this total.
3. **Complete cost ledger:** tokenizer/encoding/pretokenization; boundary teacher training and corpus scoring; main-model forward/backward/optimizer; logit dumping/storage; padding/packing; prefill; byte/block decode; draft/verification; total peak memory; persistent cache; bandwidth; hardware/precision/batch. Unavailable cells stay unknown.
4. **Evidence types must not collapse:** measured time/iteration (N05), measured task inference (W0351/W0360/W0511), measured speculative byte generation (W0352), mechanismFLOPs (W0009/W0010/W0011/W0512), persistenttrunk-cache accounting (N03), estimatedweightsloaded (W0014), simulatedacceptedpositions (W0356), abstract-onlythroughputclaim (W0015), asymptoticforecast (N04).
5. **Context cannot be one scalar:** original bytes, Unicode code points, subword positions, persistent patch positions, local receptive field and stale-state interval are different. Count semantics-matched content separately for multilingual comparisons.
6. **Measured byte-native SSM evidence is MambaByte.** S4's inspected60×languagegenerationbenchmark is WikiText-103tokens,notbytes; do not quote it as an additional byte-system success. Broader attention/SSM/memory work needs a direct interface experiment to enter this stream.
7. **Technical borrowing versus analogy:** columns above attribute only explicitly employed techniques. Our D/E/G/O/B/S/L/P labels are analyst categories. For example, calling SP “cache-decoupled adaptive computation” is our grouping; its exact mask, entropyhead and transient-state mechanism are the author evidence. No undocumented claim that authors used generic memory retrieval, information-bottleneck training or reinforcement learning.

## Main unresolved coverage

- The selected mechanisms lack a shared modern GPU serving harness covering prefill, output-byte throughput, request latency quantiles, batch concurrency, complete cache/peak memory and teacher/draft residency at matched quality. This survey must preserve per-paper settings rather than manufacture a leaderboard.
- Historical convolutional byte/character routes (ByteNet), modern local-attention implementations (EvaByte), and hierarchical fixed-patch costs (MEGABYTE) need their own primary-method verification before claiming backbone coverage exhaustive. Listed as follow-up, not counted unseen works.
- Original full evidence for W0004/W0005/W0006/W0008/W0015/W0512/W0515 and recovery N01–N04 remains uneven. Prioritize handoff rather than assigning FULL from secondhand descriptions.

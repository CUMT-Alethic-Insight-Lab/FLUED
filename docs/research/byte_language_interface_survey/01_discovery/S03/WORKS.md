## Scope limits that matter for synthesis

- Decoder causality is a tensor-dependency property, not evidence that all generated units are conditionally dependent. LCA's earlier-position attention acts on duplicated past-segment latents, not on future sampled bytes.
- No new full-read count: **0**. Reused old FULL cards: **10**, covering 10 of the 12 W entities; remaining original visits are A/P/O/S.
- No real serving reproduction was run. Published speed must retain decoding policy, verifier identity, hardware, batch, prefix/output lengths and cache settings.
- Streaming source contracts are sparse: this fragment has causality, incremental cache and UTF-8 framing evidence, but no unified time-to-first-complete-scalar or burst-emission benchmark.

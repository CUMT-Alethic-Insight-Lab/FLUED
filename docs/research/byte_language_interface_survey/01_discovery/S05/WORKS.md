# S05 works — multilinguality, robustness, and evaluation

2026-11-08. Discovery/inventory, not a new full-reading batch. Field-centered: inclusion does not depend on FLUED, source revisiting, or the earlier multigranularity proposal.

## Counts and evidence convention

**35 independent works: 32 research papers + 3 normative specifications.** Of the papers, **16 already have W IDs in the old catalog**, **3 were already discovered in its scope-recovery audit**, and **13 are fresh academic discoveries relative to both that catalog and the inspected recovery audits**. Three specifications are newly added normative references, not research papers. Thus 19 entities lack old W IDs, but only 13 are fresh academic discoveries. Do not add recovery reuse to fresh discovery.

**15 actual old evidence cards were read in full as cards**, retaining their claimed original FULL status and their stated limitations; this is not 15 new full paper reads. CANINE is catalog/abstract reuse without a card. Fresh original-paper FULL reads: **0**. Some original methods were selectively inspected below; missing tables, figures, appendices and budgets remain missing.

Depth codes: **RC** = actual old FULL card read, original paper not reread here; **A** = original abstract and identity/version page checked; **P** = selected original method/results text, not FULL; **N** = selected normative passages. Source locations attached to RC records are inherited from the card, explicitly not a fresh original inspection. Unknown author/version fields are not reconstructed from arXiv submitter names.

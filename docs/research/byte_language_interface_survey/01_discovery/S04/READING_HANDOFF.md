## What can already be synthesized without pretending new FULL readings

1. **Small vocabularies change parameter allocation, not just tokenization.** ByT5 reallocates table parameters and uses encoder-heavy depth; its fixed-position comparison is not fixed original text or fixed compute. A table-size calculation cannot tell how much regularization tying supplies.
2. **Tying is a vector of choices.** Encoder/decoder input sharing, decoder input/output sharing, layer sharing, geometric transformations and initialization transfer are distinct. One old ByT5-Large operative configuration supports input-table sharing together with untied output softmax. It does not support a universal claim about all ByT5 sizes, every implementation, or all byte models.
3. **Geometry and sharing should not be synonymous.** The inspected normalization source's “cosine” head divides by output-row norm but retains hidden norm; nGPT instead normalizes hidden states and introduces vocabulary-specific scales. Neither supplies a byte-head factorial. Pseudo-inverse tying's orthonormal-column condition requires enough vocabulary rows for its hidden dimension.
4. **Direct byte literature already uses diverse optimization techniques:** intermediate-layer/position/future-target supervision, independent entropy teachers, straight-through routing and smoothing, deletion control, stop-gradient compressor objectives, staged interface distillation and head-only adaptation. These have different targets and costs; do not summarize them as one generic auxiliary loss.
5. **Recent positive and negative adaptation evidence must travel together.** Bolmo's stage1 helps but is not strictly necessary under its approximate-FLOP comparison. Another conversion paper reports severe no-alignment degradation in its own recipe. Removable byte-head distillation does not automatically yield a byte-native deployed model and performs weakly in some true BPE-to-byte transfers.
6. Posterior-collapse and gradient-surgery sources are explicitly **background**. They do not establish deterministic-byte failure or justify classifying every residual path as pathological.

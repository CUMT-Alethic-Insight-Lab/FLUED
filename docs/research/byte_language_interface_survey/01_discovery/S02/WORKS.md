# S02 — Works: learned grouping and compression

Discovery/inventory specialist; independent field survey. Prepared 2026-11-08–09. This is not a FLUED assessment, a new experiment, or a full-reading batch.

## Counts and provenance

**32 independent works; 56 problem–solution–technique records.** Of these works, **24 already have W IDs in the old CATALOG**, **2 were already discovered in the old recovery audit but not catalogued**, and **6 are newly discovered relative to both that catalogue and the inspected recovery materials**. Therefore **8 are absent from the old CATALOG, but only 6 are fresh discovery here**. No new global W IDs assigned. New full readings: **0**. Twelve existing FULL cards were actually read and reused; their original scope limitations remain. A thirteenth card, W0014, was inspected for context but is not counted among the 32 inventory works.

`OLD` below means `docs/research/flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/`. `R` means actual old card read, retaining its FULL label rather than claiming a fresh FULL read. `P` means primary-source partial reading, with inspected sections specified. `A` means primary abstract/identity only. `M` means old catalogue metadata, not newly certified primary content. Units are **input → internal representation → output → supervision**. Character is not silently converted into byte or grapheme. No work here supplies grapheme-level evidence as such.

## Deduplication and identity decisions

- N01–N06 titles/IDs did not occur in the old CATALOG by title/identifier search. R01/R02 occur in the recovery search log and therefore count as reuse, even though CATALOG omits them.
- W0357 and W0358 are separate papers with different targets but **one dependent evidence cluster**. No independent-replication count.
- N05 `2205.14086` and N06 `2212.01304` have the same authors and related topic but distinct titles/IDs. The later paper cites the earlier; do not collapse them as versions, or move the earlier negative results into the later paper’s positive abstract.
- Charformer v3 identity is verified but mechanism extraction is v2. CANINE’s v4 abstract/HTML gains refer to different model variants in table1. MANTa is now fixed to v1. These are explicit version boundaries for synthesis.
- Clockwork RNN (1402.3511v1) was fetched as historical context only: its abstract evaluates audio/TIMIT, not text. It is **not a 33rd language-model work** here. The inventory does not manufacture a byte-language result from an RNN family resemblance.

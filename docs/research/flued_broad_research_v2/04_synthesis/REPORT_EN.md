## 0. Scope, evidence, and principal conclusions

### 0.1 The problem is broader than any proposed architecture

The governing question is how a learned system preserves distinctions, maintains them through subsequent computation, makes them accessible to a legal query, uses them correctly, learns that behavior over time, and composes with other components. These are six research axes, not six stages in an inevitable progression toward retrieval. A decoder may fail despite adequate retained information. A memory may fail because of interference, precision, or a wrong update policy rather than inadequate nominal size. A useful mechanism may form and later be replaced. A transplanted component can fail because its recipient expects different coordinates, timing, or companions. Conversely, source retention can genuinely provide information absent from a closed state. None of these possibilities is settled by the name of an interface or by fluent reconstruction.

The report restores the [original 167-direction scope](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/07_scope_recovery/FOLLOWUP_DIRECTIONS.md): **A20/B22/C20/D20/E22/F19**, totaling 123 core directions, with **28 independent interface questions** and **16 alternative-route comparisons**. Multigranularity—using multiple information resolutions—and returning to original sources are candidate techniques within that space. Bytes are an independent interface choice crossing it. Neither is the governing explanation. Appendix N gives each direction an explicit English disposition and a source-linked route; it does **not** declare 167 solved problems.

This is an evidence-led substantial rewrite. It gives native update rules, legal readout, learning history, and host composition their own substantive comparisons; retains positive and negative reconstruction results; distinguishes task accuracy from content-specific use; and makes resource and source permissions explicit. Relations, lifecycle, utility, and byte interfaces have finite, independently navigable sections without displacing the six axes. The report develops conditional next decisions rather than selecting an architecture by eliminating unread competitors.

### 0.3 Three contracts and four observable outcomes

Every meaningful comparison needs three contracts. **Information access** specifies what the writer, query generator, reader, evaluator, and teacher know, and when. It includes all surviving original and derived objects, including files, KV caches, local windows, pattern banks, routing moments, and fast optimizer state. **Learning opportunity** specifies labels, teachers, trainable modules, initialization, updates, stage doses, tuning, and selection. **Resources and outcomes** specifies physical bytes/precision, active operations, total computation, latency, unsuccessful work, and the promised task/query family. Equal parameters, tokens, steps, or active state matches only one part of those contracts.

Four outcomes must then be measured separately:

| Outcome | What would support it | What does not follow automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Recoverable target information | A declared reader predicts held-out target distinctions from retained state | The deployed model forms that query or uses that information |
| Correct native return | The legal deployed interface returns the intended association | Subsequent prediction depends on it |
| Content-specific dependence | Valid target-content changes cause corresponding output changes while non-target facts remain intact | The system follows the correct authority policy or improves absolute task quality |
| Task-correct utility | Performance improves against a relevant trained/budgeted comparator under the specified evidence policy | The same mechanism, benefit, or cost frontier transfers across hosts and histories |

These are not a one-way diagnostic ladder. Redundancy can suppress a single ablation even when a route is useful. A damaged hybrid can show a large intervention effect without representing a natural computation. Following supplied context can be correct in a stipulated-world task and wrong when sources are fallible. Training a stronger reader changes the learning contract. This is why D's measurement controls condition conclusions throughout A–F rather than becoming a final afterthought.

### 0.4 Principal conclusions

**A:** Sufficiency is relative to task family, loss, query timing, rate, and reader. Reconstruction, continuation, and retrieval objectives have favorable and unfavorable evidence. No universal best objective follows. Unknown-query exactness is a stronger promise than a finite predictive statistic.

**B:** Native memory is an editing policy as well as a container. Addition, residual correction, global decay, directional erase, covariance-aware filtering, partitioned state, nonlinear fast weights, and predictive state are substantive alternatives. Load, delay, key/value overlap, precision, rank, and reachable solutions are distinct. Retuned or curriculum success limits a particular incapacity claim without defeating finite-bit counting.

**C:** Legal query formation and read/write binding matter independently of payload quality. Normalization, temperature, output gating, head sharing, layer reuse, and iteration change different operators. Same-bank reader repair is a serious no-original-return route; oracle rescue is a diagnostic, not a deployable query.

**D:** Probe access, native return, dependence, and correct use are different measurements. Natural and latent substitutions, sham replay, screened negatives, target/non-target tests, grouped redundancy controls, authority policies, and explicit statistical power are necessary for strong interpretations. Non-rejection is not equality or demonstrated absence.

**E:** Formation, temporary facilitation, later substitution, repurposing, and resurgence all have evidence. Endpoint gradients and final checkpoints cannot reconstruct unavailable history. Raw cosine is weaker than actual optimizer-update benefit, held-out generalization, or content-use trajectories. Direct large-causal-model supervision and complete optimizer-history evidence remain limited.

**F:** Acute replacement, constrained repair, from-scratch formation, and practical recipe selection answer different questions. Exact replay, intact self-stitch, reset/relearning, companion packages, and unchanged-interface adaptation are indispensable controls. A useful connector need not preserve original semantics; failed bounded repair is not intrinsic impossibility.

Source-enabled access remains a legitimate competitor under richer retention permissions. Its omission detection, lifecycle, and full costs are not automatically solved. Byte choices require four separate units and remote-content outcomes, not legality or BPB alone. Recent public originals sharpen these comparisons, but **17 selected official ICLR 2027 manuscript bodies remain unavailable**. The report therefore offers selected verified submission metadata plus version-scoped public science, not a conference-wide results or novelty verdict. No new training is authorized and no numerical acceptance threshold is frozen.

## A. Compression and task sufficiency

### A.1 What a sufficient representation promises

#### A.1.1 Specify the experiment before choosing a loss

Let history or context be \(C\), the representation produced before a later query be \(Z=E(C)\), the query be \(Q\), and the required answer be \(Y\). A useful comparison declares the task distribution, loss, query timing, retained channels, permitted reader family, and optimization opportunity. Prediction sufficiency concerns whether \(Z,Q\) support the relevant conditional answer distribution, not whether a reconstruction looks fluent. Exact reconstruction asks for all original distinctions; retrieval may ask for a particular binding; continuation may emphasize common local dependencies.

The query timing is substantive. A writer that already knows a requested identifier can filter a long stream for that identifier. A writer that must later answer any identifier cannot use the same filtering privilege. Query-last evaluation with unseen examples from an unchanged generator also differs from evaluation on new query families, such as relational joins after training only exact lookups.

[W0080](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B24/W0080.md), **2608.14528v1, §3 and Appendix D**, makes this contract explicit. Its finite-equivalence-class bit result assumes a fixed task, countable histories, deterministic encoding, and a common exogenous subsequent-input distribution. Its state includes accessible external files, not just the short prompt. In its independent-fair-bit example, writing before the randomly selected query has an information requirement that query-visible writing avoids. These are conditional results about all retained channels, not a recommendation to use external files or a universal language compression rate.

#### A.1.2 Task invariance can justify forgetting—and exclude a later task

[W0059](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B04/W0059.md), **2106.10800v5, §§3.2–3.3 and Appendix B.3**, first chooses an input equivalence relation. Its task family consists of predictions invariant within those equivalence classes. Under the stated countability, finite-entropy, log-loss, and asymptotic coding conditions, a maximal invariant characterizes the worst remaining task information. This gives a principled no-revisit route: discard distinctions the promised task family does not need.

A later question about a detail erased by that equivalence relation lies outside the promise. For example, an augmentation that removes a serial-number digit cannot support a theorem about later exact serial-number questions merely because it preserves object category. The useful conclusion is to expose the invariance contract and test its deployment relevance. The legacy card's Appendix B.5–B.6 restrictions remain: its reported local issues in extensions to arbitrary losses and mean-squared error do not invalidate the separately supported log-loss argument.

[BR-A01-R04](../03_reading/V01/BR-A01-R04.md), **2203.07004v2, §3, Tables 1–4**, supplies an empirical counterweight to aggressive invariance. Own-view reconstruction or an information incentive improves some visual transfers after contrastive training; reconstruction also hurts supervised CIFAR100→DTD transfer, 36.06% to 34.79%. Retaining more information can preserve private task signal or preserve nuisance. Those table values belong to the fully read arXiv v2; the formal CVPR edition's bearing tables remain incompletely aligned.

#### A.1.3 Available information is not the deployed consumer

[W0092](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B13/W0092.md), **2002.10689v1, §§2–4**, defines usable information through optimal log-loss within a specified predictive family. A deterministic preprocessing can make existing information easier for that family to extract without creating new Shannon information. This supports reader-side repair as a real alternative to increasing storage. It does not imply that a particular trained model reaches the family optimum.

[W0091](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B04/W0091.md), **2009.12789v2, §§2–3 and Appendices B–C**, similarly ties representation quality to a decoder family and known labels. Its exact generalization statements require strong finite-space, deterministic-label, family-closure, and exact-optimum assumptions; the card records tensions in the literal closure and bounded-loss conditions. Its conceptual contribution survives without importing an unconditional guarantee for ordinary softmax networks.

Accordingly, an A/B study should report separately: task information recoverable by a diagnostic reader; performance of an equal-permission trained reader; the native returned content; final answer dependence on that content; and task correctness. A successful stronger reader rules out complete loss for the tested targets under that reader's permissions. Failed readers alone do not prove information-theoretic loss. A correct native return can still be bypassed, redundantly used, or overridden under a source-authority policy.

#### A.2.3 Continuous codes, discrete codes, and scalarization

[VQ-VAE / BR-A01-R09](../03_reading/V10/BR-A01-R09.md), **1711.00937v2, §§2–4**, uses nearest-neighbor codebooks, straight-through gradients, commitment loss, and a separately learned prior. Constant categorical KL under a uniform prior removes one training pressure; it does not ensure task-relevant code use. Its CIFAR bound is worse than the continuous VAE comparator, and a supervised code-to-phoneme mapping is a post-hoc readout. Compare actual payload, entropy, dead codes, estimator, reader, and exact-content outcomes—not “discrete avoids collapse” against unmatched continuous vectors.

[W0013](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B24/W0013.md), **2512.21913v1, §§II–III**, adds learned gates and variable-length quantized units. Its lossless text fallback checks the source at encoding time; this is not later recovery of an omitted original. Equal vocabulary and equal achieved compression are different comparisons. It supplies a staged-tokenization example, not a staged-versus-joint causal verdict.

[W0064](../03_reading/V10/W0064.md), **1808.07593v4, §§3–4, Appendix B**, shows another distinction: with deterministic labels and unrestricted stochastic bottlenecks, the usual linear information-bottleneck scalarization need not trace all desired frontier points. The squared penalty addresses that particular geometry. It is not evidence that useful compressed states cannot exist, nor that estimated information coordinates alone select an architecture.

### A.3 Exact detail, binding, order, and relations

#### A.3.1 Retention must be tested on distinctions, not semantic plausibility

Rare details, high-entropy identifiers, exceptions to a common rule, and rare output targets are different difficulty factors. A common identifier can have high entropy; a rare fact can be predictable from a template. A low-frequency output token can be difficult for the decoder even when the state preserves its identity. A clean test independently varies occurrence frequency, entropy, answer-token frequency, and task importance, holding rate and reader opportunity fixed.

ICAE's ordinary-text reconstruction versus structured-random and fully random failures is a useful anchor, not a complete exact-detail benchmark. Teacher-forced reconstruction also supplies earlier target tokens; it can look strong while the required later query needs a discarded binding. Measure exact strings separately from fluent paraphrase and semantic answers. Include unseen values, not only familiar names whose properties can be supplied from model parameters.

Binding and order require stronger contrasts than changing a bag of words. Preserve the same entities and values but permute which entity has which value, reverse a relation's roles, or alter within-segment order with boundaries fixed. Query both the original and changed association. If a representation tracks all values but not their assignments, a value inventory probe can succeed while the promised answer fails. Cross-segment composition needs its own training and test contract: ICAE's initially failed independent-segment concatenation shows that locally readable codes do not automatically compose.

## B. Native writing, retention, interference, and capacity

### B.1 Native writing as an editing policy

#### B.1.1 Addition, residual correction, and covariance

Use a value-by-key state \(M\) so the read is \(Mq\). Additive writing uses \(M^+=M+vk^\top\). A residual update instead uses

\[
M^+=M+\beta(v-Mk)k^\top.
\]

It reads the current prediction at the write key and corrects toward the new value. For unit keys, the immediate change at another query is

\[
\Delta(Mq)=\beta(v-Mk)(k^\top q).
\]

This elementary identity explains both the attraction and the limitation: orthogonal addresses avoid collateral change from this substep; correlated ones do not. Repeated identical keys with latest-binding truth favor correction, while first-binding truth can make that same correction destructive. An erase mechanism needs a definition of stale content before erasure quality can be assessed.

[W0199](../03_reading/V02/W0199.md), **2102.11174v3, §4.2 and Appendix B**, supplies the foundational residual-writing example and its orthonormal-key preservation condition. [W0228](../03_reading/V11/W0228.md), **2406.06484v6, §§2–3 and Appendix B**, makes the recurrence practical through WY/UT factorization and triangular solves. This changes the computation schedule, not ideal capacity. It preserves the chosen input-conditioned recurrence in real arithmetic; finite-precision agreement is a separate question.

[W0229](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B06/W0229.md), **2501.12352v3, §§3–4**, separates regression weights, function class, and optimizer. An exact linear least-squares memory uses both key–value cross-statistics and the key Gram matrix; additive linear attention corresponds to dropping a covariance correction, while one-sample residual writing is not the full-prefix optimum. Better covariance treatment is therefore a native no-revisit alternative. Its unweighted recursive least-squares comparator also adapts poorly after a process change (§5): retaining all past evidence optimally under one weighting can be the wrong policy under nonstationarity.

Average gains coexist with essay-number regressions and slower reported prefill+decode. Two updates, selector and coupled tiling add work. |
| Correlated observations need history-dependent correction geometry | **Bayesian Layers / W0226**: Kalman-style mean and covariance | Learned input-conditioned auxiliary dynamics/noise; innovation updates memory mean using covariance-directed gain; q reads updated mean | [2605.31163v5, §2, Apps.B–G](../03_reading/V03/W0226.md). These are genuinely different interventions. Independent strengths do not equal independent addresses; selective erase does not equal global decay; value-side reorganization does not equal an invertible relation store. A learned projection's weak cosine with the write key is descriptive geometry, not evidence that it targets the right obsolete fact.

Bayesian Layers makes a particularly informative distinction. Under its linear-Gaussian design model, the posterior mean and covariance are sufficient for its specified regression problem. Resetting predicted covariance to an isotropic matrix recovers a delta-shaped update while discarding accumulated geometry. Testing dense, diagonal, and reset covariance on a common stream can therefore locate a mechanism. It must charge \(D^2\) covariance state, FP32 arithmetic, and chunk history. Its layerwise teacher-fitted conversion and ordinary continued-training comparator differ in exposure, objective, and trainable modules; they cannot establish a clean transplant coefficient.

#### B.1.3 What should remain true after a conflicting write?

Three truth policies expose different failures:

- **First binding:** a later mention is distractor or misinformation; protect the original.
- **Latest binding:** a later event is a legitimate update; correct the original.
- **Context-tagged binding:** both versions are valid under different time, role, or source tags; retain enough information to answer the requested version.

Addition, residual correction, and erasure should be compared on all applicable policies. Querying only the newest binding can conceal destruction of useful neighbors; querying only old keys can penalize legitimate updates. After every controlled conflict, score the corrected association, unrelated old associations, near-key associations, and explicitly tagged historical associations. A multi-state solution additionally needs a consistency policy when the same identity routes to different states.

Prediction error is also not future relevance. An unusual but useless string can generate a large residual; a familiar relation can become essential only after a later question. Error-driven memory is a plausible learning signal, not a measured value-of-retention oracle.

### B.2 Retention, interference, and late relevance

#### B.2.1 Separate time, writes, and load

A long sequence changes several variables simultaneously: elapsed update steps, number of distinct associations, total writes, similarity of distractors, repeated conflicts, and distance from training lengths. To distinguish mechanisms, keep an association fixed and vary these axes independently. A no-write interval tests persistence without new interference; equally long unrelated writes add load; near-key writes probe crosstalk; repeated conflicting writes probe truth policy. If the actual recurrence always decays on every token, “no write” must specify whether its transition/decay still executes.

[W0212](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B06/W0212.md), **2610.09056v1, §§III–V and Appendices B–F**, analyzes a narrower but useful case: fixed additive outer-product storage queried at the exact stored key. For orthogonal values, squared key-kernel overlaps determine the returned-value cosine; finite random features add another approximation term. With correlated values, the value Gram matrix can amplify or cancel interference. Thus correlated keys are not a universal no-storage theorem. The result concerns finite features, not finite arithmetic precision, and does not cover arbitrary noisy semantic queries or trained delta writes.

Its Appendix F countercontrols matter: rotating orthogonal values does not create capacity, and learned orthogonal addressing or sparse synthetic codes do not have general advantage in the reported comparisons. Better-looking key geometry alone is insufficient. The experiment should independently manipulate key overlap and value overlap, keeping semantic truth and output discrimination fixed.

#### B.2.2 Trainability can reverse a benchmark diagnosis

[Zoology / W0060](../03_reading/V11/W0060.md), **2312.04927v1, §3 and Appendix E**, improves the measurement of multi-query recall with fresh key–value bindings and varied query positions. Its real-text rare-bigram slice is 6.4% of validation tokens; the reported 82% concerns a weighted log-loss-gap statistic, not 82% of all mistakes or a causal allocation of language ability. Its finite-state indexing argument requires explicit bit precision when translated from matrix elements.

[Revisiting / W0088](../03_reading/V03/W0088.md), **2508.19029v2, §2 and Appendix A**, finds strong sensitivity to learning rate, width, depth, and components. A parameter-matched 150M Mamba copying comparison changes from 16% to 100% under a different width/depth layout. That success refutes the particular observed failure as an unavoidable consequence of parameter count; it does not erase finite-bit limits. The reported program exceeds 3,000 runs and roughly 20,000 GPU-hours, so best-tuned accuracy and tuning efficiency should both be visible. Complete grid and copy-metric details remain reporting gaps.

[Anatomy / BR-A02-P03](../03_reading/V03/BR-A02-P03.md), **2609.16183v1, §§4–7, Tables 1–7**, adds within-cell controls, rebinding, first-binding collisions, and curriculum success rates. A success-gated length-512 curriculum reaches 6/6 reported successes where the time ramp reaches 0/6. Those lengths are trained, not extrapolated. Matched recurrent elements exclude some local history, and a reverse masked stream can see a query before the haystack. The source's expanded decay comparison is approximately .561 versus .518, p=.86, while §10 retains an older −.32 claim; the [R1 source check](../05_review/READING_AUDIT_R1.md) confirms this same-version conflict. Use the experiment-specific result rather than silently choosing rhetoric.

These studies preserve multiple causes: inadequate capacity, difficult binding formation, sparse supervision, local feature construction, and fragile optimization. A fair comparison uses the same generator and exam stream, equal per-arm search opportunity, complete failed seeds, and explicit training versus extrapolation lengths. A successful curriculum is evidence for reachable task solutions under that curriculum, not unrestricted memory.

#### B.2.3 Depth-late relevance is not time-late relevance

[W0213](../03_reading/V03/W0213.md), **2610.19709v1, §§3–6 and Appendix A**, studies query-first phonebook retrieval. Relevance becomes linearly separable earlier than the relevant gates; target-digit gate interventions can break or rescue performance. The interventions use the known locations of all ten answer digits. A separate state probe uses professions, not the same ten-digit task.

This is informative evidence for a mismatch between available relevance and native gating under a query-first contract. It neither trains a deployable target detector nor solves retention when relevance is unknown until after writing. For genuinely time-late relevance, reveal the question after the same history has been encoded, randomize which previously mundane fact matters, and include semantic reformulations and joins. Query-first, query-last, and an oracle relevance flag should be separate arms. Shifted semantic/query-family retention remains a substantive evidence gap, not a deficit repaired by additional exact-key titles.

### B.3 Bits, precision, rank, and memory layout

#### B.3.1 A finite-bit bound and its legitimate alternatives

For uniform independent length-\(L\) strings over \(D\) symbols, a deterministic one-pass system with \(|S|\) possible post-input states can correctly reproduce at most \(|S|\) different complete strings. Hence whole-string success is at most \(|S|/D^L\), capped by one. [W0061](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B04/W0061.md), **2402.01032v2, §2 and Appendix D**, gives this counting argument. Its safe non-strict error inequality should be used rather than the source's strict boundary wording.

This is a useful restriction, not a reason to reject compact native memory. A bounded set of likely questions, compressible natural data, approximate answers, known task statistics, growing state, or query-first filtering changes the promise. Retaining a pattern bank or original KV changes the total state/access contract. Nonlinear fast weights do not escape finite-bit counting if every history-derived channel is included. Conversely, finite real-valued dimension is not a finite-bit budget until precision and representable states are specified.

#### B.3.3 One state, many states, heads, and slots

[MoM / W0204](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B16/W0204.md), **2502.13685v4, §3, Tables 4,6 and Appendices C,G**, partitions writes across selected states plus an always-updated shared state. The same routing determines active writes and reads. A shared query reads the mixture; linearity makes mixing before versus after reading equivalent. Matching active state does not match all resident states, projections, and routing. Helpful averages coexist with task regressions and load-balance sensitivity.

[Merlin / W0210](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B06/W0210.md), **2610.37645v1, §§2–4, Appendix C.5**, stores independently built segment states and separate first/second moments for routing. It grows state with segments and softly reads all visible segments. Route-Q and state-read-Q are distinct. Separately continued-training routing arms do not freeze the same written content; partial-segment normalization and moment-to-softmax interpretation remain source limitations.

[Triadic / BR-A02-N03](../03_reading/V09/BR-A02-N03.md), **2609.36529v1, Tables 1–3**, expands state through a second key axis. It compares matched expanded state and approximate parameter counts, often by reducing MLP width; a hybrid larger-KV comparator wins one recall average. State tables assume two-byte entries while an example kernel uses FP32 state. The E-fold state expansion is real even when projection-parameter overhead is small.

[Slot Attention / W0100](../03_reading/V11/W0100.md), **2006.15055v2, §2 and Appendix B**, adds competitive assignments and GRU refinement over a fully visible image. More slots or test iterations add resources; one failed stripe-segmentation seed is excluded from a reported aggregate. It establishes a useful allocation mechanism, not causal streaming or fixed-bit text superiority. [GQA / BR-A03-D12](../03_reading/V11/BR-A03-D12.md), **formal EMNLP 2023, §2, Figures 4–6**, separately demonstrates distinct queries over shared KV with recovery training; distinct Q is not independent stored content.

The missing comparison is now concrete: cross layout, total bits, query independence, read width, routing metadata, and active update cost. A single wider state, partitioned states, and multiple queries over the same state should each receive appropriate tuning. No inspected result selects the winner at matched complete resources.

### B.4 Nonlinear fast memory and predictive state

#### B.4.1 Learning the memory function at test time

[TTT / W0242](../03_reading/V02/W0242.md), **2407.04620v4, §§2–3 and Appendix A**, makes a sequence's fast state the weights of a linear model or MLP. Input-derived key and value views define a self-supervised inner regression loss; a separate query view reads the updated model. Outer next-token training learns projections, initialization, and rates. This separates the architectural memory function from the algorithm that learns its sequence-specific contents.

The equivalence to simple unnormalized linear attention has specific assumptions: linear function, zero initialization, a particular batch gradient update, and learning rate. The practical residual, normalized, mini-batch models are different operators. Chunk gradients evaluated at a boundary state do not become exact sequential gradients merely because outputs are causal. TTT-MLP offers nonlinear storage but also more fast weights, worse practical wall time in some settings, and nonmonotone long-context behavior. Its state ledger includes convolution history and chunk buffers, not only one weight matrix.

[Titans / W0234](../03_reading/V03/W0234.md), **2501.00663v1, §§3–5, Tables 2,5**, adds gradient momentum, learned decay, persistent input-independent parameters, and alternative local-attention interfaces. Its useful needle and perplexity results establish a serious no-revisit fast-memory candidate, including a neural-memory-only variant. Hybrid success cannot identify which branch supplied the answer. The main Eq.13 uses the previous memory, whereas Appendix C Eq.32 prints the current memory on both sides; Eq.16's gradient-state convention also needs clarification. The expressivity theorem's proof was not located in this edition. These are source/algorithm gaps, not evidence that the implemented model or theorem is false.

[MIRAS / BR-A02-P01](../03_reading/V02/BR-A02-P01.md), **2504.13173v1, §§3–6, Appendices B–C**, broadens the design beyond squared error: choose memory architecture, associative loss, retention regularizer, and optimizer separately. Huber-like loss can reduce sensitivity to outliers; different norms or simplex/KL geometry change what is retained. Rare critical events can also resemble outliers. The useful experiment distinguishes irrelevant noise from valuable exceptions rather than assuming robust loss is always beneficial. Framework equivalences assume stated convex regularizers and minimizers; nonlinear chunk implementations approximate different sequential updates. Auxiliary fast state and hybrid KV remain real costs.

#### B.4.2 Predictive state is a bounded sufficiency alternative

[BR-A08-R07 / Predictive Representations of State](../03_reading/V08/BR-A08-R07.md), **institutional manuscript associated with NeurIPS 2001, §§1–3, revision unknown**, represents state by probabilities of selected future action–observation tests. Under known finite-POMDP dynamics, the construction selects a finite spanning core and expresses future tests through that core. Updating predictions uses normalized ratios, not a globally linear transition. Positive conditioning probability and well-conditioned test vectors matter.

Predictively equivalent histories may discard different details; the diagram-qualified known-model construction does not promise arbitrary-query reconstruction. **History-based learning now has direct primary evidence.**

[BR-PSR-0912.2385 / Closing the Learning-Planning Loop with Predictive State Representations](../03_reading/V18/BR-PSR-0912.2385.md), **0912.2385v1, §§2–4 pp.3–6**, estimates history/test moments, identifies transformed predictive coordinates/operators through SVD and pseudoinverses, separately regresses rewards, then plans. Author consistency requires finite predictive rank, rich tests/history features, nonsingular moments and convergent estimates. Reset sampling supplies intervention trajectories; suffix sampling from an unresettable exploration stream is explicitly approximate. This is coverage-conditioned identification from histories, not oracle dynamics or off-support identification. Finite estimates can predict negative probabilities; proposed thresholding is no exactness guarantee. The printed initial-state estimator in §3 step3(a), `U_hat^T P_hat_H`, conflicts with Eq.5a’s test/history-moment formula and feature Eq.10a (pp.3–5): `U` has shape `|T| × n`, while `P_H` has length `|H|`, so the product is undefined when those cardinalities differ. Literal reproduction requires reconciliation; this is not an inspected code repair or empirical failure. [Targeted source review, §5](../05_review/RECENT_ORIGINAL_CLAIM_RULES.md).

In simulated visual navigation, **78/100 trials succeed; mean 18.2 actions covers successes only**; 22 trials loop (§5.4, Fig.2D pp.8–9). A*'s 13.9-action comparator knows true configuration. Five deployed coordinates accompany 2,000 kernels each for histories/tests, 500 observation kernels, moment/operator estimation, reward regression and 8,000 planning points (§5.2). This foundational 2009 result supports learned state and closed-loop use in that environment—not equal-resource superiority, language transfer or arbitrary-query retention.

Compare oracle known-dynamics, history-estimated and neural filters in the same environment. Vary rank, exploration coverage, rare conditioning events, precision and horizon; separately score predictive risk, historical-detail recovery and all-trial planning success. This distinguishes sufficient state, identification, numerical filtering and planner failure.

#### B.4.3 A nonlinear read need not be fixed-state storage

[Hopfield / W0240](../03_reading/V11/W0240.md), **2008.02217v3, §2 and Appendices A.1,A.4**, provides attention-equivalent nonlinear pattern retrieval with separation and attraction-neighborhood conditions. The stored pattern bank costs O(Nd) elements. Exponentially many admissible patterns as a function of associative dimension is not exponentially long history at constant total bits. Correlated patterns can average, and a query outside the desired attraction region can converge incorrectly. Arbitrary value feedback also need not preserve the pattern-space energy dynamics.

[MemN2N / BR-A03-D11](../03_reading/V11/BR-A03-D11.md), **1503.08895v5, §§2,4–5, Table 1 p.6**, demonstrates trainable adaptive queries across an explicit bank. Its positive multi-hop results retain bank access, additional computation, and restart selection. It is an important reader comparator, not evidence that fixed compressed state gained capacity. Repeat-same-query and equal-compute nonread depth are needed to isolate the benefit of adaptive rereading.

### B.5 Compare native no-revisit routes directly

The following are eligible solution families, not selected architectures. A design hypothesis becomes stronger when its predicted discriminator succeeds at the promised task and budget, rather than when a competing literature body is unavailable.

| Route | Defect it could address | What remains resident and what is learned | Most informative competing explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task-aligned continuous or discrete compression | Objective-induced omission | Bounded payload; writer and consumer trained with declared labels/teachers | Gain comes from more adaptation or different rate, not better retained distinctions. |
| Additive, residual, gated, or directional editing | Conflicting writes, stale associations | Matrix state, gates and local buffers; outer loss learns update policy | Extra projections, normalization, or local context explain gains; useful old facts are erased. |
| Covariance-aware native memory | Correlated-key regression error | Mean plus covariance/statistics, with filtering arithmetic | Larger state or supplied read keys, rather than correction geometry, explain success. |
| Partitioned or tensor-product state | Allocation/interference under heterogeneous content | All states, joint-key axes, route metadata and projections | More total bits or narrower MLP, rather than layout, changes the frontier. |
| Native query/readout adaptation on unchanged state | Retained content is inaccessible | Same frozen state; restricted reader update and explicit supervision | Adapter learns answer priors or gains stronger query information. |
| Nonlinear fast-weight memory | Insufficient memory-function class or update flexibility | Fast network, optimizer state, rates, buffers and any local KV | Extra fast bits, inner steps or local path—not nonlinearity—carry the benefit. |
| Predictive-state learning | Full history is unnecessary for a bounded future-test family | Learned predictive coordinates and filtering parameters | Unknown-process identification, conditioning or query-family scope breaks the construction. |
| Protected exact payload plus learned task state | Rare identifiers or exceptions need exact retention | Explicit bounded exact channel plus learned summary; candidate design, not an evaluated source result here | Protected-item selection needs future labels, or exact storage consumes the relevant budget. |
| Accumulating compressed segments | A fixed overwrite state loses distinct histories | Growing summaries and adapted reader, as in AutoCompressors/Merlin | Improvement comes from growth and repeated access rather than compression quality alone. |
| Source-enabled bank, flat retrieval, or hierarchy | Later queries need details not in the retained code | Source/index/KV plus access policy and reader; additional permissions | Fresh information or more calls explains advantage; omission/fallback and lifecycle remain unmeasured. |

“Without original-source return” need not mean constant memory: growing compressed state is a different no-original-return contract from one bounded recurrent state. Likewise, holding a source bank outside the GPU does not make its storage or access free. For fair comparison, report fixed-state and growing-state frontiers separately, then compare source-enabled methods under their larger information-access contract. Hierarchy is one source-policy option; neither its necessity nor its net benefit follows from a native-state failure.

## C. Addressing and native readout

### C.1 Native query formation and read/write agreement

#### C.1.1 What is the query allowed to know?

“Query” can denote an input-conditioned vector, a fixed learned output request, a controller state after several reads, or an oracle supplied with the target location. These have different permissions. A native causal query may use only the deployed prefix and state available at its formation point. A training-only posterior, future answer, bidirectional suffix, or known supporting span is additional information even when the final evaluation uses a causal model.

**BR-A03-D02**, Dynamic Neural Turing Machine, is a direct address-learning example. A controller compares a generated key with memory cells containing both globally learned address vectors and episode-specific content. The address vectors remain fixed within an episode; writable contents reset. The authors observed that the model could exploit the fixed addresses as extra weights while neglecting useful episode content. Reading a previously written location is therefore weaker than reading the intended fact. Its overlap penalty, next-input prediction auxiliary, and soft-to-discrete curriculum are distinct learning interventions. [BR-A03-D02 · 1607.00036v2 · §2–4, pp.15–19][d02].

The quantitative controls resist an easy architectural story. On bAbI, three-step discrete and soft D-NTM mean errors are 21.79% and 24.24%, against MemN2N 4.24% and DMN+ 2.81%. A feedforward discrete controller improves from 68.30% error to 37.79% with curriculum, but feedforward and recurrent treatments have unequal training duration. Table 7's discrete system passes copy and fails associative recall, whereas the soft system passes both under the paper's in-training-length criterion. Address learning is possible, optimization matters, and neither result supplies a generic recipe. The paper's read/write state indexing is unresolved; that is an algorithm-description gap, not evidence that the implemented system read future information. [BR-A03-D02 · v2 · §6–9, Tables 1,3,7, pp.15,19][d02].

**BR-A03-D11**, End-to-End Memory Networks, supplies a positive contrast. Answer supervision trains soft access without supporting-fact labels. Each hop scores input memories with the current internal query, returns a weighted sum of output memories, and updates the query. The language-model version starts from a constant query of 0.1, then becomes content-adaptive through returned information. Thus useful query formation need not begin with an elaborate query encoder. However, the model repeatedly accesses an explicit bank of up to 50 QA sentences or 200 LM words; it does not demonstrate closed fixed-state retention. [BR-A03-D11 · 1503.08895v5 · §2,4–5, pp.2–7][memn2n].

#### C.1.2 Agreement means binding, not merely geometric similarity

Read/write agreement has at least three meanings: selecting a location previously written, matching marginal query/key distributions, and recovering the correct key–value association. A permutation of identities can preserve marginal distributions while destroying bindings. Conversely, a coordinate change can preserve the function despite making raw query/key vectors look different. A useful test must change fact identity, position, and payload independently where the representation permits legal combinations.

**W0229** makes the association explicit: fit a prefix regressor to key–value pairs and evaluate it at the current query. Its MQAR construction uses short convolution to arrange a previous symbol as key and the current symbol as value/query. Success depends on this learned or constructed interface, not merely on allocating a state matrix. Under its orthogonal, one-to-one cue–response setup, a suitable regression construction solves recall; this is not a claim about arbitrary semantic queries. [W0229 · 2501.12352v3 · §3,6, Eqs.8–9,56–58][w0229].

The recent **BR-A03-D20** strengthens evidence for native content paths in pretrained GLA/GDN. Donor writes, pre-query state substitutions, and query-time output patching connect written answer content to later four-choice recall. With four facts, target transfers reach 61–89% while non-target breaks are 0.4–1.4%; equal-norm random and control-head updates do not reproduce the same pattern. The results demonstrate useful selective effects under the tested task, not independent KV slots: state-direction interventions also produce opposing collateral margin shifts. Crucially, output patching intervenes after query formation. It cannot establish that q itself was correctly formed or identify a q-only remedy. [BR-A03-D20 · 2609.33093v1 · §2–3, App.B–C][d20].

This source also preserves a substantive boundary: its free-vocabulary prose pilot achieves recipient 56.3/71.9% and donor 53.1/68.8% at the two filler settings, below its own 80% competence criterion. That number is an author-specific assay prerequisite, not a threshold adopted here. The controlled four-choice result remains positive; semantic prose use remains unestablished by that experiment. [BR-A03-D20 · v1 · App.F.2][d20].

#### C.1.3 Isolating query reachability

A discriminating same-bank comparison begins with actual native q, then a bounded q-only adapter trained on legal task inputs, an equally trained unchanged/self-interface control, and a stronger diagnostic reader. Correct-address or ridge oracles belong in a separate diagnostic lane. Their success can show that a tested target remains accessible under stronger permissions; it does not establish a deployable query. Failure of several finite readers does not prove information loss.

Hold out source identities and bindings, not only phrasings. Evaluate exact cues, noisy approximate cues, paraphrases, role reversals, and relation joins separately. A q-only adapter that memorizes a small identity inventory has not solved shifted-query accessibility. This remains a direct experiment gap identified by C.06–C.08/C.18/C.20; neither more exact-key recall titles nor an oracle output rescue closes it. The relevant primary mechanisms are available, while the same-bank legal semantic comparison remains absent from the inspected packet. [W0092 · v1 · §2–4][w0092]; [BR-A03-D11 · v5 · §2,5][memn2n]; [BR-A03-D20 · v1 · §3, App.F][d20].

### C.2 Address compression, payload grain, and replacement semantics

#### C.2.1 Four compression coordinates

Temporal grouping, feature width, numerical precision, and read-access structure should be recorded separately. Sharing a key across positions changes temporal address resolution. Truncating key features changes the scoring subspace. Quantizing keys changes numerical error. Sharing KV between heads or layers changes parameterization and storage duplication. None is synonymous with replacing token payloads by a summary.

**BR-A03-D19**, Thin Keys, Full Values, isolates feature-width reduction conceptually: retain full-width values, reduce query/key width, and optionally absorb an SVD factor into queries. For an approximated matrix `W_K ≈ AB`, the rearranged dot product is exact for `AB`, not for the discarded singular components. The source's subsequent equality notation needs this qualification, as do positional rotation, bias, and scale. Its finite-point projection argument and tiny retrieval task do not establish logarithmic key dimensions for arbitrary semantic queries. [BR-A03-D19 · 2603.04427v3 · §2–3, pp.2–8][thin].

Adaptation changes the apparent cost of this replacement. GPT-2 key-only rank 192 has roughly +26–27.6% raw relative perplexity, while matched-control QK fine-tuning reduces the reported gap to +1.8%. Query-only reduction at that rank is much worse, +181%. The fine-tuning uses three epochs over 10M WikiText-103 tokens; math repair adds 7,473 GSM8K examples. Those are informative recovery results, not training-free equivalence. With equal original K/V widths, removing 75% of key storage saves 37.5% of total KV, before other resources. The v3 conflicting GSM8K cells and v4 revised throughput figures remain edition-specific, not independent confirmations. [BR-A03-D19 · v3 · §3.1,3.4,8,11, Tables 8,18; v4 · §4.2, Table 11][thin].

#### C.2.2 Count-correct merging is a conditional operator identity

**BR-A03-D03**, Homogeneous Keys, Heterogeneous Values, merges adjacent low-attention positions using a diagonal-Fisher key approximation, sums values, and retains multiplicities. If keys within group g have already been made identical, softmax output can be written

`y(q) = Σ_g exp(q·k_g) Σ_(i∈g) v_i / Σ_g c_g exp(q·k_g)`.

Using mean values instead requires the corresponding count in the numerator. This is exact relative to the equalized-key bank; making unequal original keys identical is the approximation. The method needs a selective backward pass per compression chunk, and its inference gradient/label visibility remains an implementation-level clarification. [BR-A03-D03 · 2506.05410v2 · §3.1–3.3, Eqs.7–11, App.C p.22][asym].

Its MuSiQue 4×2 grid is useful evidence rather than a universal K/V ranking. Asymmetric values improve mean-key 18.93→21.86, cardinality-weighted 20.90→23.91, attention-weighted 14.99→23.83, and Fisher-derived 21.21→24.43. Yet cache-quality and peak-memory rankings differ: the reported Llama2-7B peak is 24,923 MB against H2O 22,479 and StreamingLLM 19,592. Appendix comparisons also have category-specific losses. This supports preserving value mass while changing addresses in that procedure; it does not supply a frozen-host K-only/V-only causal factorial. [BR-A03-D03 · v2 · §4.5–4.6, Fig.5/Table 4 pp.8–9, App.D p.23][asym].

The recurrent analogue needs a different derivation. Repeating a summary at many positions can repeatedly erase, correct, decay, or gate state. A single mass correction at read time does not match those transitions. DeltaNet writes a residual at each key, and GDN combines global decay with directional correction. Matching summary vectors while changing write frequency therefore changes the operator. Equal token counts, equal final norms, and equal numbers of logical facts are not interchangeable controls. [W0199 · 2102.11174v3 · §4.2, App.B][deltaold]; [W0201 · 2412.06464v3 · §3.1, Eq.10][gdn]; [W0228 · 2406.06484v6 · §2–3, App.B][deltanew].

#### C.2.3 A replacement factorial before attributing an effect to granularity

The following contrasts answer different questions and should precede attribution to block size:

| Treatment | Address path | Payload path | Main distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact replay | Original | Original | Does the intervention preserve the intact computation? |
| K-only | Altered/shared keys | Original position-specific values | Address degradation without intended payload loss |
| V-only | Original keys | Summary or donor values at specified positions | Payload replacement under unchanged scoring |
| Both | Same K alteration | Same V alteration | Interaction between address and payload changes |
| Count/update control | Declared mass or transition schedule | Same content as comparison | Repetition and normalization rather than grain |

A block-size-one replacement is not automatically identity if it introduces a projection, normalization, or different update order. Nor is every cross-world K/V combination valid: contextual keys and values may encode inseparable identity or positional information. Where legal binding cannot be preserved, call the result a joint interface perturbation rather than pure content damage. Grain then becomes a controlled axis: keep total bits, source permissions, update schedule, query family, and return width fixed where possible, and report which resource cannot be matched.

This design distinguishes replacement shock from grain and from adaptation. At each selected grain, record frozen performance and bounded repair, alongside equally adapted original/self-interface controls. Compare candidate improvement minus control improvement, not only recovery from the candidate's damaged start. No universal acceptable drop or training dose follows from these papers.

#### C.2.4 Separate routing changes from coadaptation

Merlin illustrates why routing and state reading need different query names. **W0210** builds fixed-length segment states plus first/second moments of dedicated routing keys. A dedicated routing query determines segment weights; a backbone query reads each segment state. Independent routing projections improve the tested comparison, but separate computation paths do not make the learned representations statistically independent. GDN Meanpool→Merlin improves 16K RULER 57.9→62.7, while one multi-key needle cell falls 48.4→45.0. Each arm is separately continued-trained, including synthetic recall/QA exposure, so this is a routing-design comparison under a common recipe rather than routing-only surgery on identical frozen values. [W0210 · 2610.37645v1 · §2–4, Table 2, App.C–E][merlin].

Its resource and timing details also matter. At 16K with 256-token segments, GDN state cache is 72.1× the single-state baseline versus 64× for position/mean routing; the additional moments are not the whole cost of multiple states. All visible segments are softly read, not sparsely skipped. The pseudocode uses a fixed segment denominator for a partial segment, unlike the main formula's observed-count normalization. That unresolved description limits exact online-normalization claims; it does not establish an implemented error. [W0210 · v1 · §3.1, App.C.5/D, Table 9][merlin].

GQA supplies an unusually direct replacement-by-adaptation contrast. Mean-pooling old KV heads, choosing one old head, and random new heads are compared after uptraining; at zero dose MQA degrades much more than GQA, and both improve with 5%, with smaller gains at 10%. Five percent costs approximately 600 TPUv3 chip-days beyond the original checkpoint and downstream fine-tuning. T5-XXL average development scores are MHA/GQA-8/MQA 47.2/47.1/46.6. This establishes a real recoverable conversion in an encoder–decoder host, not cheap arbitrary transplantation or a decoder-only result. [BR-A03-D12 · formal EMNLP 2023 · pp.4897–4900, Table 1/Figs.4–5][gqa].

Together, these studies motivate three distinct outcome curves: acute frozen replacement, recovery under a specified adaptation dose, and independently trained performance. A grain comparison must hold the adaptation opportunity within each curve; a routing comparison must disclose whether its value states also changed during training. Positive recovery can reject an intrinsic-loss explanation for tested content under that repair class. Failed bounded repair rejects that class and budget, not every possible reader. Neither outcome substitutes for comparing untouched-model continuation and original-content positive controls.


### C.3 Conditioning, normalization, scale, and gates

#### C.3.1 A better-conditioned system need not be a better native reader

In **W0229**, full linear least squares uses key Gram correction; basic additive attention substitutes an identity approximation. The distinction concerns the covariance of key dimensions and associated value statistics, not deletion of a few token positions. Gram inversion can improve a fixed regression problem, but the paper's switching nonstationary process gives a reversing control: unweighted RLS adapts poorly after dynamics change, whereas query-local regression adapts better. Retaining all old statistics can be harmful when the desired mapping changes. Its local-linear method also incurs substantial storage and solve costs and is not presented as a practical long-sequence implementation. [W0229 · 2501.12352v3 · §4–5, Eqs.10–13,42–55][w0229].

**W0212** further specifies when key geometry predicts retrieval. Its fixed additive outer-product memory queries the exact stored key and measures value cosine. With orthogonal unit values, interference depends on squared kernel overlaps; correlated values require their joint Gram matrix and can reinforce or cancel interference. ResNet→ViT prediction error falls from 0.113460 using key-only geometry to 0.004209 with joint value geometry. A CIFAR100 PCA-plus-whitening transformation changes the reported limiting cosine from 0.652 to 0.996, but simultaneously changes dimension and geometry. Appendix F finds no universal advantage from learned orthogonal addresses or sparse synthetic codes. [W0212 · 2610.09056v1 · §III–V, App.C–F][geometry].

These are meaningful conditional results, not finite-precision theorems or noisy-query guarantees. A low condition number, high spectral concentration, or improved ridge reconstruction must be paired with target-sensitive native returns. **W0086** illustrates why average language quality is insufficient: after 50% GDN state reduction and recovery, WikiText perplexity improves 16.8→16.3, but LAMBADA worsens 9.7→12.0 and 4K RULER falls 38.8→27.5. State rank, average prediction risk, and retained rare associations measure different things. [W0086 · 2602.04852v3 · Tables 1,4, App.F][prune].

#### C.3.2 Normalize directions and control temperature independently

**BR-A03-D13**, QKNorm, replaces unbounded dot products with unit-norm q/k cosine scores and a learned scale. Its largest ablation is informative: removing learned g gives 24.53 BLEU against 33.24 for the full setting; normalizing V as well gives 32.34. Without scale, many candidates compete through logits restricted to a narrow interval. Directional normalization can therefore make attention too diffuse even when it improves numerical behavior. [BR-A03-D13 · formal Findings EMNLP 2020 · pp.4248–4253, Tables 2,6][qknorm].

The main reported +0.928 average BLEU comparison also changes LayerNorm/ScaleNorm and warmup, so it is not a normalization-only causal estimate. Its head-count sweep is nonmonotonic: 2/4/8/16/32 heads yield 32.40/33.16/33.24/32.42/32.30 in the specified translation task. Neither eight heads nor its sequence-length-derived temperature initializer becomes a default for a different memory system. [BR-A03-D13 · formal edition · pp.4250–4253, Tables 2,4–6][qknorm].

For whitening or centering, distinguish a paired coordinate transformation from a functional change. If the scoring inner product is to remain invariant under an invertible key transform, the query must receive the compatible inverse-transpose transform. State and output conventions may need corresponding changes. One-sided whitening deliberately changes similarity. Fit statistics on training sources only; otherwise test-distribution information enters the operator. Measure logits, angular margins, norms, denominator mass, return amplitude, and final content dependence separately.

#### C.3.3 Gates can alter consumption without altering addresses

**BR-A03-D15** places a query-dependent, head-specific sigmoid after the attention-weighted return. That gate does not change the current softmax logits. In its MoE ablation, baseline perplexity is 6.026, output-elementwise gating 5.761, headwise gating 5.792, value gating 5.820, q gating 5.981, and k gating 6.016. A one-expert value-gating control reaches 5.808, showing that routing among multiple experts is not required for the observed gain. [BR-A03-D15 · 2505.06708v1 · §2–3, Tables 1,6][gated].

Output gating and value gating also differ: first-token attention changes from baseline .467 to .048 with output gating, but remains .297 with value gating despite reduced large activations. These observations separate candidate mechanisms; they do not prove that a particular fact was used. Long-context results require an additional 80B-token continuation. A useful target-host test holds logits and bank fixed, changes the post-read gate, includes a matched-return-norm control, and asks whether correct-versus-wrong value dependence changes. Stronger stability or lower sink attention alone cannot identify content consumption. [BR-A03-D15 · v1 · §4, Tables 4–5, App.A][gated].

### C.4 Slots, heads, depth, and iterative reading

#### C.4.1 Independence has several resource meanings

Independent q vectors, independent KV projections, independent resident states, independent write policies, and independent semantic contents should not be counted as the same capacity. GQA retains H query heads while sharing G KV groups. LRKV retains independent queries and combines a full-width shared KV base with head-specific low-rank corrections. MoM has multiple state matrices but uses a shared q after linear mixing. For a shared linear read, mixing states before reading is algebraically equivalent to mixing their reads. A change in that setting cannot be credited to query diversity that is not present. [BR-A03-D12 · formal EMNLP 2023 · pp.4896–4898][gqa]; [BR-A03-D17 · 2601.11471v3 · §3, Eqs.2–9][lrkv]; [W0204 · 2502.13685v4 · §3.2–3.3][mom].

MoM's active-state-matched comparison improves mean recall 26.32→28.16, but SQuAD falls 30.03→29.69 and NQ 17.74→16.60. Activity matching does not match total resident state, projections, or parameters. Triadic attention increases recurrent state by factor E and binds values to two keys, with scores factoring into two query–key products. Its E=4 GDN recall 31.1 exceeds base 26.2 and larger-head 28.5, while a hybrid comparison favors larger-KV recall 45.3 over Triadic 44.5. The tensor can be flattened into a joint-key matrix; tensor order does not demonstrate a persistent relation object. [W0204 · v4 · Tables 4,9–10][mom]; [BR-A02-N03 · 2609.36529v1 · §2–3, Tables 1,3][triadic].

LRKV's useful design should be separated from its unresolved cache ledger. Appendix G prints 48/72 MB beside 52.6%, although that displayed ratio is 66.7%; other displayed MB ratios also disagree with percentages. Per-scale ranks elsewhere partly explain main-table percentages but not the appendix's separate rank-64 description. Rotation through factor matrices likewise needs an implementation-compatible construction. These are source arithmetic/positional ambiguities, not proof that low-rank sharing is invalid. [BR-A03-D17 · v3 · pp.24–25,29, Tables 7,8,12][lrkv].

#### C.4.2 Fewer writers are not fewer reads

Cross-Layer Attention has one KV producer for a group of layers and separate q projections in later readers. It reduces duplicated persistent cache, while each reader still accesses the shared bank. At 1B, MQA-CLA2 and narrower-head MQA both use 5,120 bytes/token, with perplexities 13.60 and 13.81 before the separately reported learning-rate sweep. At 3B, a tuned CLA2 setting gives 9.34 versus memory-matched MQA 9.48, but downstream results have mixed winners. End-to-end serving acceleration is unmeasured. This is one-writer/many-reader sharing, not independent layers writing a common summary or mixing residuals across depth. [BR-A03-D04 · 2405.12981v1 · §2–4, Tables 1,3,5–8][cla].

MHLA instead retains multiple block summaries and applies learned block-index mixtures. Its coefficients are not shown as an input-conditioned router. Its printed intra-block expression lacks an explicit token-triangular mask, while prose describes incremental inference; therefore actual token causality needs implementation/prefix evidence, not an accusation of demonstrated leakage. Main and appendix training-data descriptions also conflict. Its unmasked rank argument cannot simply be transferred through causal masking, which can increase matrix rank. [W0219 · 2601.07832v2 · §4, App.C–D/G, pp.9–11][mhla].

#### C.4.3 Iteration can refine, reason, or converge to the wrong answer

MemN2N's 1/2/3-hop joint bAbI errors of 25.8/15.6/13.3% are positive adaptive-read evidence; path-finding nevertheless remains at 90.2% error in the specified three-hop setting. Ten restarts are selected rather than averaged, and equal-extra-compute nonread controls are absent. Slot Attention shows another mechanism: competition is normalized over slots, then a weighted mean over input positions updates each slot through a shared GRU. More test slots/iterations can improve image grouping, but increase storage/compute; one Tetrominoes run stuck in stripes is excluded from the reported aggregate. Neither is a fixed-bit causal text-memory superiority result. [BR-A03-D11 · v5 · p.6, Table 1][memn2n]; [W0100 · 2006.15055v2 · §2–4, p.6, App.B–C][slots].

Hopfield attention supplies conditional one-step recovery bounds with separated patterns and sufficiently close queries. Its explicit pattern bank costs O(Nd); correlated clusters can produce averaging or metastable states. Feeding arbitrary independent values back as new queries does not automatically inherit the original energy-decreasing dynamics. [W0240 · 2008.02217v3 · §2, App.A.1.4–A.1.8/A.4][hopfield].

A clean iteration comparison fixes the same bank and initial q: one read; repeated identical-q reads; adaptive q updates from previous returns; and equally costly transformation without another read. Separate parameter tying, fusion width, and temporary states. Exact lookup, noisy completion, and relational joins should each be scored. Improved output with unchanged address margins can reflect reasoning rather than better access; sharper wrong margins can reflect attractor amplification.

## D. Deployed content use and causal measurement

### D.1 Probe evidence, restorative interventions, and native use

#### D.1.1 Probe success is a learning result with its own budget

MDL probing measures label-coding cost conditional on representations, not only endpoint accuracy. In **W0370**, an ELMo layer-0 probe obtains real/control accuracies 93.7/96.3%, yet online codelengths are 173/302 kbits; at layer 1 they are 96/515 kbits. A control task can be memorized accurately while requiring substantially more supervision. Random contextual layers also retain pretrained lexical embeddings, so they are not fully random-input baselines. Probe architecture, labels, spans, training, selection, and seeds must travel with recoverability claims. [W0370 · formal EMNLP 2020 · §2–4, PDF p.6/Table 1, App.B][mdl].

Restorative tests strengthen some causal claims but can change the system. In **W0372**, amnesic projections remove linearly recoverable properties and compare same-rank random damage. For masked dependency, targeted and random projections both reach 4.67% from 56.98%, removing 820 dimensions; adding gold labels and retraining reaches only 20.46%. That condition does not establish target-specific damage beyond random destruction. Conversely, coarse-POS label addback exceeds the original baseline, demonstrating that new labels and adaptation can add capability rather than merely restore a clean interface. [W0372 · formal TACL 2021 · §2.3,5–7, Tables 1–2][amnesic].

**W0374** improves intervention validation through independently trained target and non-target probes. Its formal six-model study finds completeness–selectivity tradeoffs, with nonlinear counterfactual methods usually stronger but late BERT AlterRep exceptions. In that BERT setting, AlterRep reliability .8346 exceeds FGSM .5518; these are independently optimized intervention strengths, not equal perturbation norms. Independence from the surgery probe is valuable, yet hidden-state gold after intervention remains unavailable and unmeasured properties may change. The older BERT-only conclusions cannot be spliced into the later multi-model study as one unchanged experiment. [W0374 · formal IJCNLP-AACL 2025 · §3–6, App.C.1–C.5][reliability].

#### D.1.2 A successful learned intervention need not reveal native access

RAVEL trains a subspace intervention with donor activations and counterfactual labels while freezing the base model. Cause changes a target attribute; Iso preserves enumerated non-target attributes. MDAS improves aggregate disentanglement over DAS, but verb-Entity gives 49.5 versus 51.8, with Iso 90.2 and Cause only 8.8. High preservation can coexist with almost no intended target change. Entity and context holdouts are separate, not joint; source wording about layer selection remains unresolved. [W0531 · 2402.17700v2 · §2–4, pp.17–19, Tables 4,6][ravel].

The recent latent-strategy source provides a complementary learning route. **BR-A08-V17** adapts a decoder with a latent pseudo-token, a response-aware training posterior, and surprisal-weighted reconstruction. Inference routing sees only input, but the decoder still sees input and previous output. Its valid-trace fidelity is not equality of full response distributions; inverse-surprisal controls and lost low-surprisal fidelity delimit the objective. This is evidence for changing training incentives under a specified task, not proof of a bottleneck-only memory or spontaneous latent use in an unchanged host. [BR-A08-V17 · 2607.17674v1 · §3–5, Apps.D–F][latent].

The useful set of questions is consequently: what content can a declared reader recover; what does native read return; what causal change follows a legal content substitution; and does that change improve the task? Success at one stage motivates the next measurement rather than replacing it.

### D.2 Natural counterfactuals and negative controls

Natural input changes and internal substitutions answer complementary questions. A natural A/B pair recomputes the whole system and estimates the effect of a task-valid changed world. A local state swap holds other parts fixed and targets a particular interface, at the cost of a potentially unnatural hybrid. Agreement strengthens interpretation; disagreement can reveal recomputation, binding mismatch, or intervention artifacts rather than choosing a winner by fiat.

**W0373** makes naturalism operational rather than rhetorical. Spanish gender/number interventions repair dependent determiners, adjectives, verbs, and contractions through dependency-tree transformations. Gender analysis restricts animate nouns because fixed-lemma positivity fails for many lexicalized genders. Even grammatical changes can be semantically implausible. One native speaker's check finds 73% accuracy over 100 transformations, with 76% gender and 70% number. Its backdoor argument requires the stated graph, positivity, valid transformation, and sampling assumptions; those do not make heuristic outputs perfect. [W0373 · formal TACL 2023 · §3–5, Apps.B–C, PDF pp.9–12][natural].

Activation-patching evidence quantifies why corruption choice matters. In **W0533**, Gaussian noise yields factual peaks 2–5× sharper than symmetric replacement, while arithmetic tasks can show the reverse. For one IOI contrast, logit difference detects positive heads 9.6/9.9/10.0 and negative heads 10.7/11.10, whereas probability detects only positive 9.9. Joint 3/5/10-layer windows also exceed summed single-layer effects by 1.40/1.75/1.59 in the specified comparison. A joint window cannot localize its whole effect to the midpoint layer. [W0533 · 2309.16042v2 · §3–6, pp.4–7,19/Table 4][patching].

The compiled X-Proportion counterexample in **BR-A04-N01** is especially decisive for null interpretation: positional information constant across donors survives resampling but is removed by zeroing. The appropriate ground-truth circuit changes with the intervention. A donor distribution that never varies the target feature cannot establish that the feature is unused. Restoration and complement destruction also restrict different paths; the operator must match the claimed necessity or sufficiency. [BR-A04-N01 · 2407.08734v1 · §3–5, pp.6,20–21][robust].

A practical semantic control family is:

| Intervention | Desired endpoint under a declared closed world | Confound addressed |
|---|---|---|
| Replace target value consistently, preserve key and unrelated fact | Answer follows new value; unrelated answer remains stable | Target-content specificity |
| Paraphrase unchanged fact | Semantic answer stays invariant | Surface matching rather than content |
| Swap relation roles with the same value bag | Answer changes according to binding | Bag-of-values shortcut |
| Change irrelevant same-format fact | Target answer stays invariant | Generic channel/scale sensitivity |
| Delete all supporting evidence | Abstention or declared no-answer behavior | Prior guessing mistaken for evidence use |
| Add a contradiction | Conflict handling under the specified policy | Unconditional copying |
| Same-state replay and donor-positive recovery | Intact function and detectable intended effect | Broken or insensitive assay |

Match length, position, byte/script class, and norm where relevant, but report residual differences instead of claiming an impossible perfect match. Screen negative donors for actual answer content; W0050's donor paragraphs were not guaranteed answer-free. A natural donor marginal does not certify the joint hybrid, and a norm-matched random vector is not a semantic counterfactual. Both remain useful when named accurately. [W0050 · v1 · §5.2, Apps.K/Q/S][w0050]; [W0374 · formal edition · §3–4][reliability].

### D.3 Mediation, redundancy, and self-repair

For two specified routes A/B under one background and score, record all four outcomes and compute `I = Y11 − Y10 − Y01 + Y00`. This is exact background-specific nonadditivity. It is not a unique share of information, proof of global increasing returns, or proof of a particular backup mechanism. Static duplicates, conjunctive cooperation, saturation, and downstream compensation can produce overlapping patterns.

**BR-A04-R08** shows why noising and denoising need not agree. For clean a, corrupt b, and mediator M, its four structural evaluations give a restoration effect, a destruction effect, and their interaction. L9H9 has restoration +2.575, interaction −2.338, and noising +.237 logit-difference units. Individual backup-pair effects reach only 7.1% of its interaction, while some group contrasts are much larger. Table 2 gives L11H2 pair/group interactions +.013/−.596, even changing sign. Exact finite differences require no differentiability; interpreting a local mixed-Hessian approximation requires smoothness and local scope, and path-specific language requires the stated graph/freezing convention. [BR-A04-R08 · 2606.27510v1 · §3–5, pp.17–18, Tables 1–3][mediators].

The original IOI circuit supplies a concrete redundant-route control. Ablating three name movers reduces logit difference only about 5% because other heads compensate. Its 87% fidelity result does not establish completeness: greedy mixed-group tests expose a gap up to 3.09 against baseline 3.56, despite random/group tests looking stronger. Group-conditioned minimality is therefore different from unconditional single-node necessity. The selected groups and thresholds also need independent evaluation if transported into a new assay. [BR-A04-N09 · 2211.00593v1 · §3–4, pp.10–11, Apps.J–M][ioi].

Hydra distinguishes a component's direct contribution from the total effect after downstream reaction at fixed weights. At layer 23, r²=.92 with slope .69 describes variance explained and incomplete logit compensation, not 92% restored task accuracy. Attention-response r²=.13 and MLP-response .87 further separate responses. Its outcome is the intact model's preferred token, not necessarily a verified fact. Thus compensation is observed in that host, but semantic identity restoration requires an additional task-correct test. [BR-A04-R02 · 2307.15771v1 · pp.9–14, Figs.7–8][hydra].

**BR-A08-V19** extends factorial entry interventions to evolving memory and simulated question feedback. Later trajectories evolve independently; it is not an intervention fixing every later path. Of 144 constructions, only successful-corruption/clean-counterfactual cases remain: 125 and 120 for the two hosts. In one oracle repair comparison, residual probe effect changes .178 without repair to .129 question-only, .053 memory-only, and .003 jointly. This is meaningful restoration under access to clean counterfactual dialogue/memory, not a deployable correction policy. The construction supplement remains missing, so the source stays PARTIAL despite its read body. [BR-A08-V19 · 2608.30198v1 · §4–5, Table 2][errors].

For D.06, distinguish current-position self contribution, nearby context, and genuinely remote evidence before combining them into a route score. In a write-before-read recurrence, the current token can enter the state immediately; a correct output need not demonstrate retention of an old fact. Query text may also repeat the answer or provide an easy local completion. A proposed local×remote factorial should preserve the question's meaning while independently varying local answer leakage and the remote binding. Record whether recomputing local context also changes remote KV construction or q. If those channels cannot be varied independently, report a joint treatment instead of additive self/local/remote information shares. BR-A03-D20's hybrid boundary result—over 99% of its normalized pre-query donor effect lies in KV—does not exclude recurrent influence on earlier writes or subsequent queries. [W0199 · 2102.11174v3 · §4.2][deltaold]; [BR-A03-D20 · 2609.33093v1 · §4, App.A][d20].

A target-host redundancy test should first demonstrate competence, then compare individual routes, a functionally justified whole-route group, and matched random groups. Observe the initial returned content and downstream target-related changes. Holding downstream variables fixed estimates another effect than allowing natural compensation; report both when feasible. Pairwise screens guide group selection but do not certify exhaustive mediation. Rescue after damage narrows explanations for that repair class; it does not uniquely reconstruct the original cause or its training history.

### D.4 Correct use requires an authority policy and an honest score

FaithEval and ClashEval ask different questions. FaithEval instructs models to follow supplied context, say unknown when unsupported, and conflict when evidence disagrees. Its counterfactual context deliberately supports a world-false answer. ClashEval treats erroneous sources as a hazard and tests both correct-prior/wrong-source and wrong-prior/correct-source strata. A system that always follows context can succeed under one stipulation and fail under the other. [BR-A04-N07 · 2410.03727v3 · §3, Apps.A–D][faith]; [BR-A04-N08 · 2404.10198v3 · §3, pp.6,9][clash].

The numbers show the cost of collapsing these contracts. FaithEval reports GPT-4o closed-book 96.3% versus counterfactual 47.5%, while conflict prompting reduces normal-context GPT-4o .85→.83 and Claude .90→.85. Its answer-mention matching, including a permissive unknown keyword list containing “not,” is weaker than whole-response entailment or exclusive endorsement. ClashEval's GPT-4o chooses wrong context .608 when its prior is correct, and correct context .903 in the reverse stratum. Aggregate context bias .304 is plausibly half the conditional rate under balanced strata, but exact code/denominators remain unreconciled. Calibration improves aggregate accuracy .615→.754 while prior bias rises .021→.085. These are tradeoffs, not universal truth recognition. [BR-A04-N07 · v3 · pp.6–9, Table 1, App.A][faith]; [BR-A04-N08 · v3 · Tables 2–4, p.9][clash]; [reading audit R2 · §3.4](../05_review/READING_AUDIT_R2.md).

ERASER supplies deletion/retention metrics for the original predicted class, not gold correctness: comprehensiveness measures confidence lost when the rationale is removed; sufficiency measures how much confidence is lost when only the rationale remains. These can reveal useful behavior without identifying its semantics. Goodhart's counterexample trains a detector to recognize intervention cases and manipulates those scores. Detector accuracy ranges 77.5–99.8%, and some untouched-input F1 scores change, so perfect preservation is conditional, not universal. Nevertheless the concrete inflation demonstrates that optimizing the evaluator can diverge from improving explanation. [BR-A04-R04 · formal ACL 2020 · §4.2, PDF p.8][eraser]; [BR-A04-R05 · 2308.14272v1 · §3–4, pp.15–17, Tables 2–6][goodhart].

W0050 contributes a positive anchor: QASPER document-input training yields 19.98 F1 against trained constant input 12.54 and shared zero input 6.82, with reported content benefit +7.43 [6.15,8.75]. Both genuine document benefit and non-document channel effects exist. Its gold supplied evidence does not test retrieval, and its unequal training references plus missing supporting PDF preclude a clean nine-system ranking. Dependence, content specificity, absolute quality, and reference-system utility should be reported side by side. [W0050 · 2610.19373v1 · §3–5, Apps.C/J/O][w0050].

### D.5 Statistical validity and training-time tracking

**BR-A04-N11** turns circuit claims into explicit tests against input and random-circuit references. Its sign-balance test is not a magnitude-equivalence test; exact ties make it inapplicable in a compiled example. Its independence null can remain unrefuted because of low power, and the paper explicitly does not study Type II error. Random-reference superiority is not reproduction of the intact model. The v1 Table 1/App.E conflicts over pass marks and docstring minimality remain source ambiguities rather than corrected results. No inspected positive establishes nonlinear conditional-redundancy sensitivity. [BR-A04-N11 · 2410.13032v1 · §3–5, pp.5–9,15,17,21–22][testing].

**BR-A04-N12**, Certified Interventional Fidelity, addresses a different problem: confidence for a fixed bounded mean over declared input/intervention laws. Predictable adaptive proposals require support and recorded weights; changing the selected circuit or target after inspection requires additional selection control. Its clipped recovery score changes the estimand by hiding negative effects and over-recovery. Raw gaps and denominator handling remain necessary. [BR-A04-N12 · formal UAI 2026 · pp.212–214, Apps.C–D][cif].

The reported sample savings are conditional: one IOI certification uses 102 betting versus 1,875 Hoeffding samples, while failure-exclusion medians are 88 i.i.d., 201 static-adaptive, and 118 history-adaptive; a borderline condition takes 1,217 i.i.d. versus 3,800 history-adaptive. Adaptive sampling is not uniformly better. Statistical precision cannot validate semantic donors or rescue an insensitive score. [BR-A04-N12 · formal edition · pp.217,227, Tables 3,6][cif].

D.20 therefore remains a longitudinal experiment obligation. Track the same held-out source/binding families across available checkpoints: native return quality; correct/wrong-content dependence; task-correct answers; collateral effects; and fixed instrument controls. Separate discovery items from evaluation items and preserve clustering by source/entity. Treat checkpoint, hypothesis, and intervention selection explicitly; use fixed evaluation or a justified sequential method. Sparse snapshots censor onset and disappearance, and missing history cannot be inferred from endpoint non-use. A decline in a circuit score can reflect extraction instability, changed reference sensitivity, replacement, or compensation rather than lost content.

## E. Learning dynamics and changing computational routes

The learning problem is not simply whether a route exists, but how it becomes useful and what later optimization does with it. The following technique comparison previews the source-specific evidence developed below; each solution changes a learning contract rather than diagnosing the current host.

| Original problem | One-sentence solution | Borrowed AI technique and decisive comparison |
|---|---|---|
| An inference network cannot track its changing generator | Give inference extra early optimization before ordinary joint updates. | Alternating variational optimization: lagging-inference v2 Algorithm 1; compare actual work and annealing, not latent KL alone. |
| A competitor both helps formation and later displaces contextual behavior | Vary when shared competitor-trained components or bypasses are available. | Curriculum and mechanistic interventions: Coopetition v2 Fig.4; intermediate/early/late histories and resurgence reverse an always-harmful account. |

### E.1 Formation, disappearance, and observable history

#### E.1.1 Endpoint necessity is not formation necessity

The induction-head study gives a particularly direct separation. In a two-layer attention-only model trained on randomized-label Omniglot sequences, deleting individual heads at the endpoint often has little effect; retaining one important second-layer head can still support approximately 98% accuracy. Yet restricting formation to one second-layer head delays acquisition. Heads that are unnecessary individually in the mature network can contribute to how the network reaches that state. Parallel compensation and learning-history effects are both plausible, and they are different claims. [W0337, 2404.07129v1, §§3–5, Figs.4–6 and App.D](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B16/W0337.md).

The study's clamping interventions further separate previous-token transport, query–key matching, and value copying. More classes chiefly delay matching; more labels chiefly delay copying. These are concrete distinctions between learning an address and learning a returned value. They do not establish that every memory failure has the same bottleneck. Ideal clamping sometimes requires two forward passes and removes the need to superpose query/key and value representations. It changes the problem being learned, rather than merely observing it. Fixing a first layer trained under another seed also changes formation speed, making frozen pretrained features an active treatment rather than a neutral control.

A complementary example comes from modular arithmetic. Mechanistic progress measures reveal gradual formation before aggregate test accuracy improves: a restricted Fourier loss falls before ordinary test loss, while a residual memorization measure changes during cleanup. The crucial qualification is that the Fourier frequencies were identified from the endpoint model. They retrospectively describe the observed trajectory; they are not blind predictions of a new run's transition. Other seeds select different frequencies, and modulus and regularization change whether the reported stages appear. [W0336, 2301.05217v3, §§4–6, Fig.7 and App.C](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B16/W0336.md).

Together these works support recording mechanism-sensitive trajectories alongside task performance. They also show why an endpoint-selected probe can make history look more orderly than it was. A measure designed after seeing the successful final circuit should be labeled retrospective and assessed on independent runs before it becomes a selection rule.

#### E.1.2 “Never formed,” “not yet observed,” and “formed then retired” differ

Differential learning kinetics studies competition between contextual attention and weight-based memorization in a synthetic classifier. Its simplified analysis assumes additive independent circuit logits, large-dimensional limits, small initialization, and particular symmetry conditions. These assumptions differ from evidence for shared subcircuits discussed below. Its most transferable reporting contribution is the separation of runs that never acquire the skill within observation, runs that acquire it without observed transience, and runs with observed acquisition and decline. A finite run ending with the skill intact is right-censored, not evidence of permanent retention. [BR-A05-N06, 2412.00104v2, §3 and App.A.4–A.6](../03_reading/V06/BR-A05-N06.md).

Saved checkpoints similarly censor the onset and disappearance of a mechanism. If a dependency is absent at one saved time and present at the next, emergence lies somewhere between them, assuming the assay measures the same property. An ability present at both times could disappear and return between them. A missing early checkpoint cannot be replaced by a story inferred from final gradient alignment. Seed-specific latency distributions, unsuccessful runs, and incomplete histories should remain visible rather than selecting only runs whose endpoints support the favored account.

The language-model evidence makes the observable itself consequential. In controlled nonce morphology, prompt-control effects, candidate preference, four-choice accuracy, unrestricted construction, and external-task transfer emerge at different observed times. For OLMo-2-7B, a control-sign change is bracketed between 336B and 630B training tokens, earlier than fitted choice and exact-generation crossings. Other models reverse signs or already exceed a criterion at the first available checkpoint. Direct task fine-tuning can produce a different ordering. [BR-LD-N06, 2610.19113v1, §§3–6 and Apps.B/E/I/J](../03_reading/V06/BR-LD-N06.md).

That card remains PARTIAL because the paper explicitly refers to an external supplement that was not located; its body and included appendices are substantive, not blocked. Its temporal estimates are author-reported sparse-checkpoint/fitted quantities. They are useful evidence against one universal acquisition time, but neither their fitted crossings nor their study thresholds become local experimental gates.

### E.2 Lag, initialization, and reader–writer coformation

#### E.2.1 Inference lag is a concrete alternative, not a generic diagnosis

Lagging Inference Networks studies a variational autoencoder: a Gaussian encoder approximates a moving model posterior while an autoregressive generator can fit data without depending strongly on the latent. Repeated early encoder updates, followed by ordinary joint training, improve activity and likelihood in the reported text and image settings without changing the evidence lower bound objective. This establishes an update-allocation explanation under a specific stochastic objective. It does not establish that deterministic compressed-state non-use is posterior collapse. [BIW-0096, 1901.05534v2, §§3–6, Algorithm 1 and Apps.A–G](../03_reading/V06/BIW-0096.md).

The positive results carry meaningful costs and countercontrols. Typical aggressive phases use 30–100 inner updates. Reported aggressive/basic wall-time ratios are 2.20×, 3.73×, and 2.19×, not a uniform two-to-three-times range. The Yelp inner-update sweep changes both likelihood and time; practical convergence can stop aggressive mode earlier than a fixed iteration count. Slow KL annealing also avoids collapse, with some single-run likelihood cells slightly better than the unannealed aggressive comparison. Thus the evidence favors taking lag seriously, not selecting encoder-extra updates without an opportunity-budget comparison.

For a writer–reader system, the analogous hypotheses should be kept separate. The writer might supply inadequate content; the reader might learn too slowly; or both might keep changing coordinates so that neither sees a stationary problem. A frozen writer with an adapted reader tests accessibility under a new training permission. It does not reconstruct the original coformation history. Cross-time writer/reader combinations can reveal compatibility variation, but they also create unfamiliar interfaces. Same-time self-connections are needed before interpreting cross-time failures as harmful coadaptation.

A strong comparison between lag and objective-landscape explanations remains source-limited. The identified linear-likelihood alternative [BR-A01-R16=LD-N08, 1911.02469v1](../03_reading/V10/BR-A01-R16.md) is still body-blocked at the audited edition. Existing variational counterexamples already prevent a universal lag claim, but the missing original cannot be filled with its title. The unresolved question is which objective and optimization conditions make extra inference updates effective, not whether every unused representation has an encoder that is behind.

#### E.2.2 Equal functions can have unequal learning dynamics

The exact deep-linear analysis shows why parameter count and initial predictions are incomplete controls. With output `W₂W₁x`, rescaling the factors can preserve their product while changing internal feature evolution. The analysis solves gradient-flow dynamics for whitened inputs and a specified balanced-factor relation; aligned-mode solutions impose additional assumptions. Different encoder/decoder scale allocations can produce different apparent lazy and feature-learning behavior even when the initial input–output function agrees. A nearly unchanged kernel can conceal a smaller task-structured component that is learning. [BR-LD-N07, 2409.14623v2, §§3–6 and Apps.A–D](../03_reading/V13/BR-LD-N07.md).

Its transfer examples are equally permission-specific. In a toy semantic hierarchy, an additional output row learns from one labeled item while other weights remain frozen. Generalization depends on the learned representation and factor balance. This is a controlled illustration of initialization and receiver accessibility, not evidence that rescaling a nonlinear Adam-trained language interface repairs it.

The complete exact-v2 HTML, raw tables, and carrying figures are now read; it is stale to call this a missing-figure source. PDF alignment and several local notation discrepancies remain unresolved. Those discrepancies limit reliance on the affected algebraic steps, rather than independently refuting the main theorem. For practical comparisons, record initial function, intermediate activation scale, parameterization, and optimizer state separately. Rebalancing factors changes the geometry seen by the optimizer, even where their product agrees.

#### E.2.3 Early damage is selective, not universal irreversibility

Critical Learning Periods tests temporary deprivation rather than merely comparing endpoints. Early blurred images can leave substantial finite-horizon impairment after clean images return, and a fixed learning-rate control argues against annealing as the sole cause. But vertical flips and label permutations recover near baseline, while fixed random-noise inputs have milder residual effects than blur. Not every early deviation creates the same long-lived deficit. Fisher-based sensitivity co-varies with the effect but is not proof of a unique structural cause. [BR-LD-N03, 1711.08856v3, §§2–4, Figs.1–5 and Apps.A–C](../03_reading/V13/BR-LD-N03.md).

This comparison suggests a useful distinction: an early signal can steer feature allocation in a way later training does not readily undo, while other disruptions are mostly remappable coordinates. A restoration horizon is part of that finding. Finite recovery failure does not imply that no learning rate, task order, or longer budget could recover. Conversely, a recoverable label permutation does not show that lost fine detail is similarly recoverable. For memory formation, task-specific corruption, permutation, and no-information controls distinguish selective learning-history effects from generic disruption.

### E.3 Feature competition, useful bypasses, and changing roles

#### E.3.1 Competition does not require a universal easy-first chronology

Gradient Starvation formalizes a mechanism in which a strong predictive direction reduces learning response along another direction under cross-entropy. Its central theory concerns fixed linearized features in a restricted binary setting; it is not a theorem that deep internal representations never form. The empirical remedy still learns color in colored-MNIST experiments while changing the balance of evidence. Its large worst-group gains coexist with lower mean accuracy in CelebA, and one reported tuning setup uses the test environment. [W0333, 2011.09468v4, §§2–4 and Apps.A–B](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B16/W0333.md).

Complexity Matters supplies an important reversal. A simple strongly correlated shortcut can slow the core feature, yet more complex settings permit core and shortcut learning together. Retraining the last layer on balanced data can expose accessible core information before the deployed output uses it. Weak spurious features can later disappear, whereas strongly correlated ones persist in the reported horizon. The same literature therefore supports delayed core learning, simultaneous learning, and selective forgetting under different conditions. [W0334, 2403.03375v3, §§2–5, Figs.2–6 and Apps.A–D](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B16/W0334.md).

This matters for compressed interfaces. Local completion, an easy decoder path, and remote semantic dependence may not turn on in a neat sequence. A learned external probe can reveal latent information without showing that the original reader consumes it. Early stopping based on a supposed shortcut-only phase can misidentify examples when features form together. Difficulty, correlation, entropy, output frequency, and task diversity should be distinct variables; “harder” is not an adequate substitute for their specification.

#### E.3.2 An early competitor can help the desired mechanism form

Strategy Coopetition offers stronger history interventions than a generic shortcut narrative. Its task supports exemplar-based contextual mapping, weight-based classification, and a hybrid context-constrained strategy. The later hybrid still requires the correct label to appear in context. Declining exemplar mapping is consequently not equivalent to complete context non-use. [BR-LD-N05, 2503.05631v2, §§2–5, Figs.1–4](../03_reading/V06/BR-LD-N05.md).

Shared components make the learning interaction nontrivial. Components taken from an intermediate competitor-only checkpoint accelerate contextual learning, whereas early and late competitor checkpoints do not. The reported helpful range spans 9.8M–31.5M competitor-training sequences. Continuing a mature competitor does not recover the desired contextual mapping within the observed run. Other configurations show resurgence, and exact exemplar matching can preserve the contextual strategy. These outcomes reject both “a bypass is always harmful” and “once displaced, a mechanism can never return.” They do not establish equal-compute superiority, since the helpful components bring additional pretraining history.

The comparison with Differential Learning Kinetics is informative rather than redundant. The latter analyzes an independent additive-circuit model and a regularization-dependent decline; Strategy Coopetition emphasizes reuse of shared copying machinery with a changed earlier computation. Both can generate transient performance, but they imply different interventions. Suppressing a competitor may protect a target route in one setting and deprive it of a useful forming component in another. Timed attenuation followed by restoration, compared with an equally disruptive unrelated attenuation, is more discriminating than permanent deletion alone.

#### E.3.3 Better training-distribution loss can accompany worse contextual generalization

Competition Dynamics trains autoregressive models on mixtures of Markov chains and fits their outputs with four candidate algorithms. A context-generalizing bigram-inference phase can precede a training-matrix-based phase: in-distribution performance improves while behavior on fresh transition matrices worsens. At higher diversity, the context-sensitive phase persists within the available budget; changing state count, width, or transition encoding changes the observed sequence. [BR-A05-N05, 2412.01003v4, §§2–4, Figs.5–8 and Apps.C/I](../03_reading/V13/BR-A05-N05.md).

Here “retrieval” refers to weighting learned training transition matrices, not navigating an external document store. The behavioral mixture predicts held-out trends and includes arbitrary-algorithm negative controls, but does not uniquely identify a circuit among correlated or omitted strategies. Its strongest contribution is that task distribution and representation can alter which competent strategy wins. It does not show every sufficiently trained model becomes context-blind.

For target histories, the informative trajectory jointly records clean task risk, correct-versus-wrong-content dependence, native returns, and shifted-query behavior. A falling loss with falling targeted dependence could indicate replacement, redundancy, improved local inference, or a changed meaning of the assay. The morphology study's negative transfer after adapters and ineffective selected-head penalties reinforce this point: reducing an attention pattern need not teach novel answer construction, and improving one diagnostic need not transfer to external compositional tasks.

### E.4 Auxiliary targets, bottleneck timing, and decoder masking

#### E.4.1 “Add reconstruction” names several different treatments

Compressive Transformers distinguish full-state reconstruction from reconstruction of the attention response to old states. The auxiliary gradients stop at the compressor in the principal setup; a separate longer-unroll comparison lets the primary loss reach it. Attention-response reconstruction gives the strongest listed Enwik8 cell, but rows select their best compression rate and the backpropagation comparison changes unroll and batch size. Larger compressed memory also sometimes worsens evaluation. The evidence concerns a task-dependent target and gradient destination, not a universal reconstruction prescription. [W0023, 1911.05507v1, §3.2, Table 5 and App.B](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B24/W0023.md).

ICAE supplies a favorable mixed-objective example that should survive negative reconstruction arguments. Its autoencoding-plus-continuation pretraining improves judged outcomes over either single objective at the tested slot count, followed by instruction tuning. The frozen host still loses quality relative to raw context, random strings reconstruct poorly, and independently compressed segments require additional multi-segment training before concatenation works. Thus reconstruction can help establish a usable interface while leaving exact-detail and composition failures. [W0038, 2307.06945v4, §3.2, Tables 3–5 and §3.3.3](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B24/W0038.md).

Deeply-Supervised Nets is different again: companion classifiers receive class labels at hidden layers, improving several image-classification results. They add heads and backward work and may be attenuated before deployment. The local theory assumes compatible optima and regularity conditions and compares convergence bounds, not actual universal convergence. It neither reconstructs old states nor demonstrates large-causal-LM semantic-memory use. [BR-LD-N02, formal AISTATS 2015, pp.562–570, §§2–3 and appendix](../03_reading/V13/BR-LD-N02.md).

For I.12, a strong output decoder could improve spelling or local completion while masking a weak trunk. The required comparison allocates encoder, trunk, and decoder capacity explicitly, with remote-content outcomes separate from output legality. The inspected vision supervision and stochastic word-level results do not supply that dedicated large-scale causal byte-model comparison. Direct large-causal-LM intermediate supervision remains a precise primary-evidence and empirical gap.

#### E.4.2 Apparently opposite schedules address different bottlenecks

Continuous First, Discrete Later delays vector quantization so weaker latent directions can form. Its linear rate–distortion channel explains one mode-suppression mechanism under Gaussian and balanced-factor assumptions. Image comparisons exchange continuous and discrete steps within a 100k-step total, while audio comparisons use a common 100-epoch total. Positive reconstruction results coexist with an audio test-clean PESQ reversal at K=4096. All-codebook PCA rank can be inflated by unused random vectors; assigned-code behavior and utilization are necessary companions. [BR-A05-N02, 2605.06870v3, §§3–4, Tables 1–4 and Apps.A–D](../03_reading/V06/BR-A05-N02.md).

GenFirst instead gives a generative prior greater relative influence before reconstruction refinement. Reconstruction weights remain fixed; prior and entropy weights decrease together. This is not a reconstruction-free first stage. Its headline schedule comparison runs 500+140 epochs against constant/cosine controls at 320 epochs. Guided generation FID improves, while cosine and frozen-VAE reconstruction FID can be better. Other variants include substantial subsequent frozen-VAE prior training and decoder fine-tuning. [BR-A05-N03, 2608.29335v1, §4.3, Table 2 p.10 and App.C pp.20–24](../03_reading/V06/BR-A05-N03.md).

These are positive recipe results for different obstacles, not competing universal stage-order theorems. Quantization can suppress useful directions early; reconstruction can also create a latent distribution difficult for a prior. Equal steps do not necessarily equal FLOPs, and GenFirst's doubled epoch exposure prevents isolating order alone. Its within-paper text-to-image variant-label conflict should remain unresolved where relevant rather than silently assigning numbers. A future schedule comparison would need constant and reversed orders, common final rate, entropy treatment, exposure, and separate evaluation-prior budgets. None is selected here.

### E.5 Same-sign gradients, scale, curvature, and plasticity

#### E.5.1 Positive cosine is weaker than optimization benefit

For primary loss gradient `g` and auxiliary gradient `v`, a positive inner product says that a sufficiently small step along `−v` decreases the primary training loss to first order at the measured point. It says nothing by itself about the held-out loss, the magnitude of the combined step, or the history of content use. A scalar cosine also discards blockwise scale and can conceal cancellation between components.

The local diagnostic is

\[
\Delta L_{\mathrm{primary}}\approx
 g^\top\Delta\theta+
 \tfrac12\Delta\theta^\top H\Delta\theta.
\]

The realized update `Δθ` is not generally a scalar multiple of the current raw gradient. Momentum, adaptive preconditioning, clipping, weight decay, and mixed-precision step skipping can change it. Without saved optimizer state, recomputing gradients does not recover the historical update. Even with that state, a local training-loss approximation is distinct from validation benefit and from natural content dependence.

Adapting Auxiliary Losses Using Gradient Similarity contains its own decisive negatives. A locally descending auxiliary direction can slow primary learning. In the perfect-teacher experiment, cosine gating can underperform simply adding the auxiliary, and rotated-MNIST gating remains worse than primary-only in some cells. These findings limit the use of sign as a decision criterion without denying the method's useful cases. [W0331, 1812.02224v2, §4, Fig.4 and Apps.B/F](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B16/W0331.md).

PCGrad adds a joint role for direction, magnitude, and curvature. Its guarantees are conditional local results, not guarantees for arbitrary Adam trajectories or every task. In several-task projection, random order matters; the resulting gradients need not all be pairwise nonconflicting. A questioned necessity argument in its appendix remains a proof-review issue, not an independently established theorem refutation. [W0328, 2001.06782v4, §§2–3 and Apps.A/H](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B16/W0328.md).

#### E.5.2 Validation transfer and full-history mechanisms need different evidence

ForkMerge directly compares one-step updates from the same target-trained 25k-iteration checkpoint and measures validation gain. It shows why local cosine is an incomplete proxy: aligned gradients can generalize poorly, while a conflicting regularizing update can help. Its later branching-and-merging procedure uses additional branches, repeated validation selection, optimizer state, and search. Multi-step interpolation is not algebraically identical to a single trajectory with an intermediate loss weight. [BR-LD-N04, 2301.12618v3, §3.1, Fig.2 p.4 and Apps.A/B](../03_reading/V06/BR-LD-N04.md).

The useful inference is narrow and important. A positive endpoint cosine can reject only the measured contemporaneous directional-conflict account. It cannot reject earlier starvation, harmful scale, blockwise disagreement, shared shortcut reinforcement, or generalization failure. Conversely, a negative cosine is not sufficient evidence of harmful transfer. Matching a common starting checkpoint improves local interpretation but does not establish that two losses remained aligned throughout training or caused a particular historical memory policy.

#### E.5.3 Rank and update norms are diagnostics, not exclusive causes

Directions of Curvature studies continual supervised learning and supplies counterexamples to several simple diagnoses. Leaky-ReLU networks can lose plasticity while update norms increase. Tanh feature rank can fall without comparable impairment, and rank-promoting regularization can coexist with deterioration. A distributional regularizer improves several harder cases but changes weights, geometry, and learning simultaneously. [BR-A05-N11, 2312.00246v2, §§3–6 and Apps.B/C](../03_reading/V13/BR-A05-N11.md).

Its practical curvature matrix is a per-example-gradient Gram proxy with rank capped by sample count, not an exact Hessian. Validation against the Hessian is restricted to a small projected problem and weakens later. Correlated rank change plus successful regularization does not establish an exclusive curvature cause. The reading is exact v2, not the later renamed v4.

The synthesis therefore leaves optimizer noise, momentum, preconditioning, and writer–reader coformation in large causal hosts as substantive gaps. Another gradient-surgery name would not recover missing optimizer history. Where a tractable checkpoint exists, directional finite differences and Hessian-vector products can test local approximations without updates. Establishing whether preserved plasticity prevents later loss of content use requires a separately authorized training comparison and retention of earlier-task quality.

### F.1 Frozen composition, self-connection, and semantics

#### F.1.1 Three self-controls answer three questions

An untouched model or exact tensor replay tests whether extraction and reinsertion preserve the original computation. An intact self-stitch inserts the proposed connector between the original halves and tests the connector's own burden. Reset-and-relearn deliberately randomizes a region and asks whether the surviving representation supports relearning. Calling all three “self-connection” conceals the causal contrast.

Yosinski's BnB baseline copies and freezes lower layers from the target-trained model but randomizes and retrains the upper layers. Its middle-layer penalty and recovery after fine-tuning demonstrate relearning/coadaptation costs, not failure of intact replay. Cross-task AnB adds task and source-history differences. Similar versus dissimilar task splits also differ in class counts and difficulty. [BR-N06-01, 1411.1792v1, §2 Fig.1, §4 and Apps.A–C](../03_reading/V07/BR-N06-01.md).

Mai supplies the closer modern self-stitch: the same trainable connector type, site, objective, and data are used within and across foundation models. Self-stitch improvements themselves show that an inserted learned module can improve the receiver, so its contribution must be separated from cross-source information. Identical connector initialization seeds are not specified in the inspected protocol. [BR-N06-04, 2603.12433v3, §§3–5 and App.A](../03_reading/V07/BR-N06-04.md).

#### F.1.2 Frozen halves can be compatible after a learned interface

Bansal operationalizes directional compatibility: freeze sender and receiver, fit a restricted bridge on the recipient's labeled task, then evaluate held-out performance. Independently trained models can stitch with small penalties, stronger sources can improve weaker recipients, and deep random sources generally fare worse. But random early layers can also stitch well; fully randomized labels are an exception; extensive receiver fine-tuning can conceal distinctions. Missing bridge schedule, initialization, and batch-normalization details limit precise quantitative transport. [BR-N06-02, 2106.07682v1, §§2–6, Figs.2–3 and App.B](../03_reading/V13/BR-N06-02.md).

Mai shows why one failed adapter is weak evidence of intrinsic component harm. At a shallow DINOv2→SigLIP2 stitch, task-loss training gives 25.1%, local feature matching 49.4%, and final-feature matching 51.4%, compared with source/recipient linear probes of 46.7%/53.5%. Both original models remain frozen. Matching the recipient's final features backpropagates through its suffix and brings stronger teacher access than local matching. A weaker CLIP sender can still fail. These are practical compatibility gains with bounded failure cases, not information-preservation guarantees.

#### F.1.3 Task-fit success is not shared original semantics

Smith constructs successful stitches between different cues and from label-coded random clusters. The synthetic noise is structured: one prototype per class is shared between training and test, with independent sample noise around it. Therefore the bridge can learn class information without an image-derived sender. It is not a demonstration that arbitrary fresh noise contains labels. Cross-domain directions also differ, and random senders do not always work. [BR-A06-X04, formal ICML 2025, §§4–5, Table 1 and Apps.A/B/G](../03_reading/V07/BR-A06-X04.md).

Invariance-aware stitching offers a constructive response by generating inputs nearly indistinguishable to the sender and checking receiver consistency. It often exposes incompatibility hidden by ordinary stitching, but uses input inversion and sometimes receiver outputs/internal features. Its explicit ViT identity failure and residual shortcut use prevent treating the improved score as semantic ground truth. Standard and invariance-aware training also differ in batch-normalization statistics and augmentation. [BR-A06-X05, 2505.20142v2, §§2–3 and Apps.B/F](../03_reading/V13/BR-A06-X05.md).

Practical fit and content preservation should consequently be reported separately. A coordinate connector should retain its utility on new bindings, conflicting cues, and legal content substitutions, not merely predict familiar class labels. Constant, random, weak-source, self, and cue-disjoint inputs test whether the connector has learned an answer route of its own. Success can reject content-loss accounts for the tested targets and repair class; failure rejects only that bounded repair. Neither supplies a host-independent semantic certificate.

### F.2 Attention migration: stages, companions, and reversing outcomes

#### F.2.1 LoLCATs is a meaningful strategy comparison with unequal stage doses

LoLCATs first trains feature maps against teacher attention outputs and then adapts the assembled linearized stack with low-rank updates. During transfer, teacher outputs feed later layers, suppressing upstream student-chain errors. Local matching and natural-chain prediction are therefore different measurements. The hybrid also retains a recent exact-KV window; it is not a pure recurrent state with no companion channel. [BR-N06-07, 2410.10254v3, §§3.1–3.3 and App.A](../03_reading/V07/BR-N06-07.md).

The audited Table 2 comparison must be stated exactly. Llama-3-8B Hedgehog without transfer starts at perplexity 2448.01 and reaches 9.02 after four LoRA epochs. With transfer it starts at 60.86 and reaches 3.90 after two transfer epochs plus two LoRA epochs. Mistral's corresponding values are 561.47→4.87 and 18.94→3.04. The planned total-step/epoch envelope is intentionally matched; dismissing it as wholly unmatched would erase a legitimate recipe comparison. But LoRA dose differs, and early stopping/best-validation selection leave realized work incompletely specified. These cells cannot estimate equal-stage-dose effects or interaction coefficients. [Reading audit R1, §3.4](../05_review/READING_AUDIT_R1.md).

The long-context countercontrol is as important as perplexity recovery. At 8192 tokens, Alpaca-only linearization achieves 0% exact passkey retrieval across placement deciles, whereas passkey-format training produces 100%; extrapolation to 10240 fails. Format-specific adaptation can rescue trained-length behavior without guaranteeing general long-range retention. Teacher passes, low-rank training, cache state, and selection costs travel with the recovery result.

#### F.2.2 MOHAWK changes the block as well as the objective

MOHAWK aligns mixing matrices, then teacher-fed block outputs, then assembled-model output distributions. Its migration initialization makes convolution identity-like, opens a gate, and removes normalization/activation incompatible with the copied block. Components beneficial in a from-scratch state-space model are not automatically suitable in a pretrained transplant. The pure model uses 80M+160M+2.76B=3B tokens; the hybrid retains four full-attention layers and their growing KV. [BR-N06-08, 2408.10189v2, §4 and §§5.4–5.7](../03_reading/V07/BR-N06-08.md).

The 5B-token strategy table favors the full stage sequence, but matches tokens rather than teacher/student FLOPs. Its same-architecture Phi→Phi control resets attention and relearns it, rather than replaying intact weights. That strengthens evidence for staged restoration while leaving exact-replay validity a separate question. Offline matrix fitting with many per-sample optimization steps tests representational fit under stronger permissions, not native deployed content access.

#### F.2.3 Companion paths can change an apparent recurrence ranking

The Key to Going Linear first freezes original Q/K/V and the host, training only new mechanism parameters on 10M tokens. Strict Llama LAMBADA scores are 28.26 for gated linear attention and 44.67 for the gated delta network. With short convolution they become 68.70 and 69.01; MMLU likewise nearly converges. A large strict-regime gap can almost vanish after a companion changes. [BR-N06-12, 2607.07706v1, Tables 1/3 pp.7–8 and Apps.B/C](../03_reading/V07/BR-N06-12.md).

This does not establish that convolution explains every transfer failure. Local/prefix caches add retained channels, low-rank adaptation uses a reduced learning rate for instability, and no equal-tuning/FLOP comparison is established. Retrieval-heavy tasks remain weak even with larger cache variants. The evidence requires naming the complete package—recurrence, projections, normalization, gates, convolution, position handling, cache, and output fusion—before attributing a transplant outcome to its central update equation.

#### F.2.4 RAM rankings depend on stage and metric

RAM migration progressively aligns projections, head outputs, blocks, the propagated stack, and the full model. Removed stages return their token opportunity to full-model recovery, a stronger control than simply deleting training. Yet trainable sets and losses differ, and teacher work remains additional. ### F.3 Capacity controls, host interactions, and manufacture versus repair

#### F.3.1 Parameter matching leaves important quantities unmatched

A trainable-parameter count is not a state-capacity measure. Record total and active parameters, resident state bits, precision, local cache, read width, fast optimizer state, teacher access, training exposure, and tuning effort separately. Equal active state can coexist with greater total resident memory. Equal tokens can mean unequal raw text after changing the tokenizer. Equal epochs can hide different losses, teacher passes, early stopping, or stage permissions.

Interface geometry illustrates why initialization and feasibility matter. Pseudo-Inverse Tying improves some continued-training cells but worsens all three reported random-initialized Llama-like models and the continued GPT-590M comparison. Its construction requires a vocabulary matrix with orthonormal columns, hence V≥d; it is not directly feasible as written for 257 symbols with wider hidden states. Frozen vocabulary factors, additional quadratic-size metric work, and nonidentical initial functions further affect interpretation. [W0367=BIW-0038, 2602.04556v2, §§3–4 and App.B](../03_reading/V14/W0367.md). Neither favorable geometric consistency nor a small vocabulary establishes memory retention or natural use.

MoCE provides a separate domain-sensitive composition example. The exact audited title is **MoCE: Adaptive Mixture of Contextualization Experts for Byte-based Neural Machine Translation**. Its v1 byte model has byte inputs and byte outputs, with local contextualization in the encoder—not a byte-input/nonbyte-output interface. Ted-59 favors its adapted byte model, whereas OPUS-100 favors the subword baseline; active-expert count changes compute. Shared encoder/decoder embeddings do not identify the effect of tying independently. [BIW-0069, 2411.01474v1, Tables 3–5 and App.B](../03_reading/V14/BIW-0069.md); [reading audit R2, §3.5](../05_review/READING_AUDIT_R2.md). The later v2/camera-ready comment is metadata, not a reading of v2 results.

#### F.3.2 Host interaction is a within-host comparison before a cross-host conclusion

For a quality outcome Q, the four-cell quantity `Q_AB−Q_A−Q_B+Q_original` describes nonadditivity on a declared host, background, and metric. It is not invariant to changing accuracy into log loss or perplexity, and it does not identify unique causal information shares. Separately optimized architecture-search winners are not fixed-component cells. Better search results can reflect search opportunity rather than an intrinsic interaction.

A transport claim should first establish the original, A, B, and AB contrasts within each competent host, at specified adaptation permissions. Only informative cells need cross-host repetition. Raw content, source permissions, target family, initial ability, trainable sets, and resource contract must remain explicit. A difference across hosts can then support a bounded interaction, rather than a universal recurrence ranking. Seed/window and hardware/environment fingerprints are part of from-scratch formation evidence; the literature supplies no certificate that those dimensions can be ignored.

#### F.3.3 Manufacturing and restoring are different histories

An already competent host can be damaged, exactly restored, or repaired through a restricted alternative. This establishes an ability before the intervention and supports acute-dependence and restoration questions. A from-scratch model lacking the ability does not supply that anchor. Failure to manufacture may reflect optimization, task exposure, or capacity; failure to repair may reflect a narrow connector or insufficient adaptation. They are not interchangeable evidence.

Frozen, connector-only, projection-adapted, and full-host treatments should therefore be named separately. Char2Subword's contextualization through frozen mBERT can worsen embedding-neighborhood agreement before re-approximation improves it; downstream freeze permissions are incompletely specified. [BIW-0122, 2010.12730v3, §§3–5 and Fig.4](../../byte_language_interface_survey/03_reading/R06/BIW-0122.md). Zero-Shot Tokenizer Transfer has a trained per-host hypernetwork despite its name, optional full-host continuation, and substantial task/language failures. Its untied-GPT-2 instability without an anchor is not a theorem against untied hosts. [BIW-0126, 2405.07883v2, §§3–5 and Apps.B/F/J](../../byte_language_interface_survey/03_reading/R06/BIW-0126.md).

A recovery curve should include immediate disruption, bounded repair, clean-model continuation, and preservation of non-target abilities. Returning a deleted stage's opportunity and counting teacher construction make a schedule comparison stronger. They do not turn it into a content-preservation or equal-FLOP proof. Architecture selection ultimately concerns complete frontiers; causal attribution concerns controlled components. Both are useful, but require different comparisons.

<a id="source-alternatives"></a>
## G. Source-enabled alternatives and omission recovery

### G.1 Fixed fine access remains a strong baseline

Source retention grants information unavailable to a closed compressed state, so compare explicit permission contracts before comparing frontiers. Within a source-enabled contract, retain strong flat retrieval, original-value fine chunks, and stronger readers before attributing gains to hierarchy or learned granularity.

SSA selects gist-addressed blocks and then attends to their original token KV. Hierarchical SSA adds meta-gists but does not uniformly win: at 32× continued training, SSA scores 44.07 versus H-SSA 42.56; after fine-tuning the order reverses, 43.35 versus 44.94. Selected gist-plus-original 53.39 versus original-only 52.76 is a small setup-specific comparison, not proof that every gist detail is consumed. Its decode implementation also scans all-position byte metadata despite ideal logarithmic routing analysis. [W0162 · 2604.20920v2 · §3–4, Tables 3,6–7, App.A–B][ssa].

ReadAgent compares parallel page selection and sequential rereading. QuALITY gist-only 77.52% improves to parallel one-page 84.13%, parallel two-page 86.16%, and sequential up-to-six 87.17%, against full text 85.83% and neural top-three retrieval 83.41%. These are meaningful positives for selected original access, but not equal-call comparisons. On QMSum, sequential 46.57 versus parallel 39.09 uses up to six times the selection requests. The reported 20.4% processed-word saving is amortized over multiple questions per document, not a universal serving saving. [W0138 · 2402.09727v3 · §3–4, Tables 1–4, App.I][readagent].

### G.2 Backtracking is real; calibrated omission recovery remains open

MemWalker can return to a parent and choose another branch. Its 15.0/18.6/18.8% backtracking-path proportions and corresponding final correctness 70.0/59.6/79.0% describe attempted paths, not recall of all initially missed evidence. Original QuALITY last-context 70.1% exceeds MemWalker 67.4%; a strong retrieval/navigation comparison must retain that negative. ReadAgent explicitly reports summary omissions, premature no-reread decisions, and hallucinations. [W0139 · 2310.05029v1 · §3–5, Tables 2–4][walker]; [W0138 · v3 · §5, App.H–I][readagent].

Self-Route falls back to long context when the model says unanswerable. Its table-level GPT-4o average 48.89 slightly exceeds long-context 48.67, while Gemini 46.41 trails 49.70. The paper does not measure confidently accepted cases that should have fallen back. Its paraphrased PassKey RAG collapse 80.34→4.58, against long-context 65.25→69.32, is a direct query-formulation countercontrol rather than evidence that all flat retrieval fails. [W0185 · 2407.16833v2 · Tables 1,3,7, §5.2][selfroute].

DRAGIN legally uses provisional generated tokens to detect a need, rewinds, retrieves, and regenerates. That is extra speculative work, not future ground truth. Llama-13B Hotpot improves EM/F1 .263/.3706→.314/.4238 over single-round retrieval; IIRC reverses .196/.2303→.185/.2221, and 7B StrategyQA no-retrieval .659 exceeds DRAGIN .641. Retrieval counts differ across methods and tasks. Entropy/attention is an observable trigger, not calibrated detection of evidence absent from the current representation; confident misses, irrelevant reads, and hard-cap failures remain required denominators. Its threshold/label table tension stays unresolved. [W0450 · 2403.10081v3 · pp.7,15, Tables 2–3,9, App.D][dragin].

Mnemis shows a different competitor: similarity retrieval plus hierarchical graph navigation reaches 93.3 versus similarity-only 89.1 in the specified LoCoMo comparison, but its global-only 87.7 is averaged over 1,387 returned questions rather than all 1,540. Build prompts reach tens of millions of tokens, and hierarchy construction and selection incur additional calls. Raw-chat search supplies another strong alternative, with ReFind macro-average 58.2 versus single BM25 48.8, yet multi-hop update integration only 8.8 and much larger processed-token budgets than a generic iterative comparator. Neither summary structures nor raw search receive a free cost advantage. [W0449 · formal ACL 2026 · pp.23914–23928, Tables 3–4][mnemis]; [W0124 · 2608.12888v1 · §4–5, Tables 2,11][raw].

A strong-reranker study also prevents treating candidate-pool elaboration as the default improvement: full F1 .548 versus no-RAPTOR .523 has test significance but reversed development direction, so it fails that study's stability rule. Removing graph expansion gives .542, not a proven equivalence to .548. Its no-reranker collapse additionally mixes incompatible score sources, so it does not isolate reranker quality alone. [W0156 · 2606.28367v1 · §5–8, Tables 2–3][rerank].

### G.3 Utility identification is not omission confidence

Randomized assigned exposure need not randomize realized inclusion after union with deterministic retrieval. CMP’s absent-exclusion example and mixed answer-quality outcomes are detailed in [Section J](#utility), with actual treatment support and continuation limits; they do not establish omission confidence. [BR-A08-V18 · 2610.02070v1 · §3, Apps.C/E/H][cmp].

For R.03–R.08/R.13, the unresolved comparison is precise: same source pool, fixed-fine original values versus learned grouping; flat versus hierarchy; parallel versus serial access; observable no-gold trigger; confidently wrong stops; unnecessary fallback; and complete construction, reading, regeneration, and failure costs. Include equal-compute no-read and same-source stronger-reader arms. If two hidden worlds yield identical observed summaries but require different answers, an omission detector needs some additional discriminating observation or must retain uncertainty. This is a statement about that observation contract, not a universal theorem that source revisiting is necessary.

<a id="lifecycle"></a>
## H. Source lifecycle, placement, and selective updating

Closed-state reform and source-enabled memory have different information-access contracts. If original content remains available, the system also acquires lifecycle obligations: source→summary→index→KV→answer-cache→backup dependencies, model versions, timestamps, authorized refresh, and completion evidence. A graph link to an episode is not proof that all derived objects become fresh or disappear when that episode changes.

Provenance as Dependency Analysis gives a precise model-specific distinction between conservative dependency and actual influence. A safe dependency set can include irrelevant inputs; its explicit `R−R` example retains provenance although the result is empty. Correct conservative propagation supports invalidation without certifying causal consumption. It applies to the specified calculus, not automatically to a neural summary. [W0420, 0708.2173v2, §§3–5](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B19/W0420.md).

Datalog maintenance adds a useful cost reversal: a small input deletion can trigger much larger backward checking, while recursive support cannot be preserved merely by counting cyclic derivations. Optimized maintenance distinguishes nonrecursive support, overdeletion, and rederivation. That is a model for making support explicit, not a guarantee that learned summaries possess such support graphs. [W0425, 1711.03987v2, §§3–6 and App.A](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B19/W0425.md).

DELF distinguishes synchronous invisibility, read-time descendant masking, asynchronous deletion, recovery logs, and expiring backups. Its strong completed-deletion statistics do not cover every initiated request unconditionally, and undeclared references can escape the graph. Lethe addresses delayed cleanup inside a storage engine, under resource and scheduling conditions, not all replicas, backups, or underlying-device erasure. [W0429, formal USENIX Security 2020, §§4–6](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B19/W0429.md); [W0431, 2006.04777v4, §§3–5](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B19/W0431.md). Logical invalidation, lawful recomputation, and physical deletion remain separate outcomes.

Pensieve supplies a concrete residency comparator: retain conversation KV across GPU and CPU tiers, asynchronously restore it, and recompute evicted portions from retained raw tokens. [W0435, 2312.05516v3, §§3–4/6](../03_reading/V15/W0435.md) reports workload-dependent throughput gains, with diminished benefits at longer simulated think times and some GPU-only configurations losing to a comparator. It is transparent prefix reuse, not learned semantic compression. Its causal correctness presumes unchanged preceding context and parameters; source/model-version invalidation and deletion were not demonstrated. LONGMEM instead freezes the memory encoder and trains a side reader to reduce one form of coordinate drift; its independently FULL [2306.07174v1, §§2–3](../03_reading/V15/W0143.md) remains distinct from the blocked assigned formal edition. Residency, encoder freezing, normalization, and lifecycle invalidation thus solve different problems.

Neural systems introduce representation staleness as well. Memorizing Transformers normalizes queries and keys to reduce stale-coordinate mismatch as weights change but explicitly does not eliminate it; larger memory sometimes ceases to help or hurts individual tokens. [W0144, 2203.08913v1, §3 and Apps.A–B](../03_reading/V15/W0144.md). Zep retains original episodes, temporal facts, and community summaries, yet its source links were not directly evaluated and complete deletion propagation is unspecified. [W0118, 2501.13956v1, §§2–4](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B10/W0118.md).

Placement must also include cold/hot traffic and reuse. Denning's working-set model motivates measuring active sets and reentry traffic, not assuming equal-cost semantic objects or future locality. CacheGen shows a hardware/bandwidth-dependent choice between transporting compressed KV and recomputing from text; arithmetic coding is lossless only relative to quantized symbols. Its text fallback requires source and usable prior context. [W0432, CACM 1968, pp.324–328](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B20/W0432.md); [W0434, 2310.07240v6, §§5–7](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B20/W0434.md).

Mooncake supplies substantial scheduling evidence, but all reported experiments use a LLaMA2-70B-shaped dummy model. Throughput and rejected-request counts are system outcomes, not measured answer-quality equivalence. Prefix hashes alone do not establish model/source-version invalidation. [W0438, 2407.00079v5, §§6–8](../03_reading/V15/W0438.md). No source-placement winner follows without construction, source and derived-state bytes, update fan-out, transfers, rejected work, tail latency, and repeated-query amortization.

<a id="utility"></a>
## J. Local action value and closed-loop utility

**R.14–R.16, with D.11/E/F consultation.** A useful action score must state its continuation. For history h, action a and subsequent policy π, compare expected terminal task quality minus complete cost after taking a and following π against the specified alternative. Stopping immediately, following the current policy and following an optimal policy are different counterfactuals. A current-query content effect is also different from keeping an item for unknown future queries.

### J.1 Complementarity changes which labels are worth collecting

[W0301, *Learning to Select Computations*](../03_reading/V16/W0301.md), **1711.06892v3, §2.2/§§4–5**, explicitly distinguishes myopic stop-after-one value from continuation-aware computation value. Its learned policy performs well in known-model simulations, with blinkered policies winning some cells and offline fitting amortized/excluded in the application ledger. Selection overhead can eliminate gains when it exceeds the useful computation's cost.

The elementary example retained in [W0483, *Informational Substitutes*](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B17/W0483.md), **1703.08636v1, Example 2.2.2**, explains the risk: two independent fair bits reveal an XOR target jointly, although neither alone improves optimal guessing. Under 0/1 reward, the joint gain is one-half; under information gain it is one bit. A policy trained only on immediate gain may never buy the first observation. Real information can instead be substitutable, antagonistic or costly, so one complementary pair does not establish universal increasing returns.

[W0303, adaptive submodularity](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B12/W0303.md), **1003.3967v5, §§3/5/App. 15**, supports greedy guarantees under conditional diminishing-return and other specified assumptions. Ordinary set submodularity or unconditional signal independence does not supply them; nonuniform-cost truncation also matters for hard budgets. The reported entropy-sign problem in W0483's proof remains an inherited source-page/proof clarification, not grounds to declare its intended theorem false. The relevant operational question is whether the selected loss, observation process and each supported history satisfy the needed assumptions.

[W0304, SAGE](../03_reading/V16/W0304.md), **2004.00668v2, §§2–3/Supps. D–E**, allocates coalition benefit with Shapley values. An additive allocation can account for a nonadditive coalition game without becoming an additive reward model for future actions. ### J.2 Recent exposure evidence makes support observable—and exposes missing arms

[BR-A08-V18, Causal Memory Policy](../03_reading/V09/BR-A08-V18.md), **2610.02070v1, §§3.1–3.2/Apps. C.2/H.1**, reserves randomized retrieval slots to expose otherwise unobserved memory contributions. This is valuable measurement design. However, union with deterministic top-ranked retrieval means **random assignment differs from realized inclusion**. An item can remain in every realized context; Appendix H.1 reports 90 inclusions and no exclusion arm. Constant propensity weights cancel within nonempty self-normalized arms, but that algebra does not supply missing support, correct denominators or comparable co-exposure backgrounds.

Its conditional utility is relative to displaced items. The oracle-enriched candidate pools improve required-memory discrimination while some answer outcomes worsen and deployable pool selection remains weak. A required fact can have negative marginal value without its companion. These observations change the next study: randomize the actual inclusion/exclusion treatment, log displaced content and other exposures, retain positive and negative utilities, and test future-query continuation separately. They do not invalidate every reported measurement or the estimator family.

[W0339, DynaKRAG](../03_reading/V15/W0339.md), **2607.06507v2, Method/Eqs. 1–2/Algorithm 1/Tables 1–2**, ranks valid actions using immediate supporting-document recall proxies and a separate continuation gate. That is not terminal rollout labeling. Its four-hop MuSiQue subgroup loses despite overall gains, and exact sufficiency targets, calibration and teacher-cost fields remain in an unavailable cited supplement. The complete available body is read; the work stays PARTIAL for that particular missing material.

### J.3 Logged feedback and budgets preserve their assumptions

[W0315, doubly robust evaluation](../03_reading/V16/W0315.md), **1103.4601v2, §§2–4**, combines reward prediction and propensity-weighted residuals under support and nuisance-estimation conditions. Its bias analysis permits history-adaptive logging; a stationary-logger simplification belongs to the variance result, not every claim. [W0317, counterfactual risk minimization](../03_reading/V16/W0317.md), **1502.02362v2, §§3–4**, trades clipping bias against variance under its signed-loss and logging contract. Neither recovers unobserved outcomes for unsupported actions, nor turns logged one-step rewards into values under a new continuation policy.

[W0319, *Bandits with Knapsacks*](../03_reading/V16/W0319.md), **1305.2545v8, §2/Theorem 5.3**, compares against a distribution-aware dynamic optimum, stronger than a best fixed arm. Yet fixed per-arm distributions and additive accumulated reward are premises. History-dependent complementary facts need additional state and assumptions. Budget awareness alone does not make that model a source-navigation theorem.

Extra internal computation remains an eligible alternative. [W0302, *Rational Metareasoning for Large Language Models*](../03_reading/V16/W0302.md), **2410.05563v3, §3/Tables 1–5**, trains chain selection using known-answer likelihood gain minus output-token cost. A valid policy comparison therefore includes strong fixed and myopic baselines, complementary zero-immediate-gain pairs, harmful combinations, supported randomized continuations and actual terminal outcomes. All teacher, search, selection, failed-call and stopping costs count. Unsupported actions remain unknown, not zero-valued. No threshold or policy is fixed by this synthesis.

### K.1 Fresh computation is not more persistent memory

The latest [BIW-0076, Scratchpad Patching](../03_reading/V08/BIW-0076.md), **2605.09630v2, §§3–5/Appendix C.2 Table 4/Appendix D**, now has a FULL v2 card, superseding the source audit's earlier selected-v2 receipt. It adds transient partial-patch computation while retaining the same distinction between committed patch states and temporary scratchpads. It is not recurrence through earlier scratchpads, nor an external source index. A byte-encoder residual also bypasses the trunk.

At 500M/100B bytes, fixed-p8 plus scratchpad improves over fixed-p8 but remains worse than the flat byte baseline (**0.7425 versus 0.7283 BPB**). At 2B/400B, the corresponding values favor scratchpad (**0.6345 versus 0.6366**). Each cell is one run. Fixed-data training work increases; the H-Net variant regresses under matched training FLOPs despite improving at fixed data (**§5.1/Fig. 7, p. 9**). Serving latency/throughput are unmeasured, and persistent trunk-KV savings are not whole-model peak-memory savings.

This changes the causal comparison: hold boundaries and committed states fixed; compare entropy, periodic and random fresh-compute triggers at matched total training work; separately vary local bypass and aggregation. Score local byte prediction and remote-content dependence independently. A gain from fresh prefix computation need not imply better segmentation, more retained facts or useful source rereading.

### K.4 Four units and the scope of the classic alternatives

The input unit determines which primitive distinctions the model initially receives. An internal position determines when substantial computation and persistent state are allocated. The output unit determines the distribution that is normalized and sampled. The supervision unit determines what training targets and teachers expose. Changing any one can affect the others without making them identical. A character-composed word model, a Unicode-codepoint encoder, a byte denoiser, a merged-byte-token generator, and a causal byte generator therefore answer different questions.

### K.5 Output laws, sharing, and remote-content tests

A serial local decoder defines a joint probability through conditional factors. Parallel future-byte heads instead define separate predictions unless the method supplies a joint model or a verification procedure. Speculative candidates may be useful, but proposals, rejected branches, verifier work, EOS handling, arbitrary partial prefixes, and final incomplete blocks belong in the cost and probability contract. A canonical token-path score is not a marginal probability of the underlying string when multiple tokenizations are possible. An implementation that changes tokenization during scoring must define which event it measures before comparing perplexities.

Output geometry likewise requires actual tensor identities. Input–output tying shares a lookup table with a projection; encoder–decoder input sharing ties two lookup paths; tying both is a third treatment. A normalized cosine head changes direction and temperature separately from tying. The classic word/subword evidence in [BIW-0035, 1608.05859v3](../../byte_language_interface_survey/03_reading/R08/BIW-0035.md) is useful scoped reuse, not a theorem for a 257-symbol alphabet. nGPT's bundled normalized recipe and PIT's large-vocabulary feasibility condition do not resolve a small-byte tying × linear/cosine factorial. Match trainable count where possible, record nonidentical initial functions where not, and distinguish raw-gradient contributions from actual optimizer updates. A tied parameter receiving two gradients does not imply that either path supplies a fixed share of an Adam update.

The target outcome cannot be legality alone. ASCII, UTF-8 leading bytes, and continuation bytes have different local predictability and byte-count denominators. Report validity, intended character identity, exact identifier retrieval, and semantic answers separately. Include role swaps with the same entity/value bag, paraphrases, and joins whose answers require remote content. A local decoder can complete a multibyte character from nearby bytes while ignoring the old fact that determines which character should appear. Conversely, a correct remote fact can be spelled incorrectly by an inadequate output interface. Separate these failures rather than crediting or blaming the trunk from aggregate BPB.

For multilingual evaluation, parallel semantic content and script-dependent byte lengths make equal positions and equal text different controls. Lossy normalization may remove the very distinction being tested; reversible preprocessing needs an offset map. Record raw bytes, normalized bytes, token positions, missing or truncated outputs, scorer/version, EOS treatment, and alignment checks. A successful offset check on one dataset or one model configuration does not certify every scorer. These are artifact obligations as well as scientific comparisons; no target code has been inspected or executed by this synthesis.

### L.2 T02: actual full public reading changes the interface comparison

[BR-R2-T02, *Stochasticity in Tokenisation Improves Robustness*, **2604.16037v1**, 37 pages including Appendices A–G](../03_reading/V17/BR-R2-T02.md) supersedes the drafts' acquired-but-unread wording. The public PDF was posted 17 April 2026 and has the staged hash recorded in the card; the American-spelling official association remains a candidate relationship, not manuscript equality. The reading is FULL at that public edition only and is not a reproduction.

**Problem:** many valid token-ID sequences decode to the same question text, while a canonically trained model can be brittle to alternative boundaries. **One-sentence solution:** augment pretraining, fine-tuning, or frozen in-context demonstrations with alternative valid segmentations, including samplers with explicit support and uniformity properties. **AI techniques:** stochastic data augmentation, idealized Dirichlet–multinomial split allocation, segmentation-tree sampling, and multi-valued decision diagrams for whole-string tokenization sampling. This is not arbitrary text corruption, a learned-byte-memory system, a paraphrase test, or long-range retrieval. Candidate answers remain canonically tokenized in the principal random-query tests, so output-tokenization invariance is not established.

STOCHASTOK begins with canonical tokens and repeatedly attempts a valid two-way split. Unsplittable selected tokens do not change, so nominal α is not realized expansion. STOCHASTOK-UNI samples split counts and is uniform **conditional on a per-token split count** inside canonical-token supports, not uniform over all whole-string segmentations. UNIFORM-K samples over the whole string at specified token-edit distance and can cross old boundaries; UNIFORM samples the declared entire valid set. These are different supports as well as different sampling laws (v1 §5.1, Algorithm 1 p.18, Appendix F). BPE-dropout probability and α cannot be equated without actual split budgets. Appendix F’s exact Pólya/Dirichlet–multinomial correspondence does not unconditionally describe Algorithm 1’s skipped unsplittable attempts. Even renormalizing onto feasible counts need not recover its realized law; successful-split assumptions and skip/cap/feasibility handling require reconciliation before sampler-equivalence or reproduction claims. This source-scope issue does not overturn the reported task cells. [Targeted review, §2.1](../05_review/RECENT_ORIGINAL_CLAIM_RULES.md).

The positive result is substantial within its scope. On Language Game with Llama-1b, canonical fine-tuning yields **94.0%** canonical accuracy and a **29.8-percentage-point** decline under uniform query segmentation. UNIFORM-K α=1 yields **94.8%** clean accuracy and a uniform-query score 2.8 points above its own clean score (Table 2 p.7). Under the tested greedy local adversary, canonical training falls **94.0%→6.1%**, while UNIFORM-K falls **94.8%→69.6%** (Fig.5 p.8). The latter is better tested robustness, not complete protection or a worst-case certificate. The attacker uses model scores and token-ID access; a deployment accepting only text through a fixed canonical tokenizer has a different attack surface.

Reversing controls matter. Random-start attacks leave canonically pretrained Tiny-LLM at **0.00–0.04** accuracy across listed stochastic settings, and UNIFORM clean fine-tuning can score **.47 versus .58** for canonical training (Fig.11 p.27). A same-two-way-support comparison finds no significant uniformity improvement in the specified settings, while expanded support helps under stronger/random-start search (Fig.15 p.30). This narrows a uniformity-only account without proving a universal support law. Canonical ARC-E declines **.378→.330**, COPA **.660→.610**, and the TokSuite canonical average **.3746→.3623** (Tables 8–9 p.25 and Appendix G). Per-token perplexities under different segmentations do not constitute byte-normalized likelihood comparisons. Frozen in-context augmentation improves selected spelling tasks but reduces clean CSQA **68.3%→67.1%** (Table 3 p.8).

The one-layer output-change bound in Theorem 6.2/Appendix E pp.19–20 uses aligned substitution columns, bounded embeddings, and Lipschitz assumptions. The threat set also includes variable-length splits and insertion/deletion shifts; extension to those edits, deep networks, and classification margins is not established by the displayed argument. The bound does not itself prove stochastic training lowers risk. This is a bounded applicability concern, not a refutation of all theory in the paper.

The source's no-additional-inference-cost claim applies when the same deterministic tokenizer/model execution is retained after training. It does not price longer stochastic demonstrations, changed training token/byte exposure, segmentation construction, attack search, or output length. Full GPU-hours/FLOPs and effective sequence distributions are unreported (§5.1, Appendix A). The next scientific comparison is sampling support × realized splits × clean/attacked task outcomes with raw-text, context, and compute controls—not an immediate recommendation to randomize a learned boundary compressor. Its implications touch A's target law, E's training incentives, F's interface adaptation, and K's units; it does not close any remote-content-use gap.

<a id="priorities"></a>
## M. Conditional priorities and minimal discriminating comparisons

### M.1 Choose the unresolved fork before the mechanism

The practical recommendation is an order of evidential dependence, not a fixed experiment queue. First establish whether a competent host and sensitive instrument exist. If exact replay changes behavior or a known-positive content swap is undetectable, stop interpreting null effects and repair the assay. Next use unchanged saved state, when available, to distinguish tested-content recoverability, legal native return, final content dependence, and task correctness. Only then choose a writing, readout, objective, schedule, interface, or source-access comparison predicted to separate the leading explanations.

If a legal q-only repair recovers held-out bindings from the same state, prioritize a reader/integration comparison before claiming erased content. If damage follows near-key writes but not transition-only delay, prioritize editing/geometry rather than generic memory expansion. If higher carry precision rescues long-delay answers, numerical accumulation deserves priority over a rank diagnosis. If content-use trajectories rise and later fall while ordinary loss improves, preserve replacement, redundancy, and authority changes before claiming never-formed memory. If companion-aware repair removes a transplant gap, study the package interaction rather than rank update equations in isolation. None of these conditional observations has been measured in the target project by this report.

Source-enabled work is conditional on permission and workload. Where retained summaries cannot distinguish two source worlds with different answers, another observation may be useful; strong flat/fixed-fine, parallel, serial, and hierarchical policies should compete on the same source pool. Lifecycle and actual full costs then become part of correctness, not optional serving details. Where the deployment promises a bounded query family and prohibits original-source return, task-aligned objectives, covariance-aware writes, precision, partitioning, legal iterative reading, predictive-state learning, and timed adaptation remain eligible.

### M.2 Evidence actions and stopping conditions

The [detailed decision matrix](EXPERIMENT_DECISION_MATRIX_EN.md) specifies query, current evidence, alternative explanation, control, possible outcomes, cost class, existing-checkpoint versus new-training type, and a stop condition. The minimum sequence is:

1. Inventory artifact availability without assuming checkpoints contain optimizer, scaler, RNG, stage, and data-cursor history.
2. Calibrate replay, semantic/grouped positive controls, negatives, authority policy, and task competence on separate analysis/evaluation sets.
3. Examine the smallest same-information contrast that can reverse the leading explanation.
4. Separate fitting an external reader from forward-only analysis and from native unmodified use; separate main-model continuation from either.
5. Add training only if a formation or learning-cause claim is essential and separately authorized. Return opportunity to unchanged/self-interface controls and report failed seeds.
6. Stop an arm when its instrument fails, its information contract cannot be matched, its required artifact is absent, or its result already discriminates the narrow question. State remaining ambiguity rather than expanding the claim or silently extending the budget.

Numerical tolerances, seed counts, exposure, effect margins, and cost weights must be calibrated to the task, training stage, same exam stream, and environment. A criterion transferred across frozen versus adaptable hosts, t=0 versus trained states, seeds, or hardware requires a new reference. This report neither freezes thresholds nor authorizes a training run. More papers are justified only when a specific missing primary can change a retained claim; more literature cannot reconstruct missing optimizer history, provide target-host outcomes, or manufacture off-support policy data.

The resulting research program is positive and plural. The literature identifies concrete mechanisms, viable native alternatives, and controls capable of favoring or rejecting them locally. It does not identify one cause of all FLUED failures, guarantee unrestricted memory, certify every body or theorem, or select an architecture from absent evidence. The appropriate deliverable is a broad, source-linked, decision-ready comparison with honest unresolved boundaries.

## 1. Main conclusion and scope

The literature supports a broad research program with several viable explanations and remedies, not a single architecture selected from an endpoint failure. A system can lose a distinction during compression, corrupt it through later writes, retain it numerically but fail to address it, return it correctly but bypass it, learn it temporarily and later replace it, or expose it through an interface a recipient cannot use. These possibilities imply different interventions. None is established as the cause of a FLUED failure by literature alone.

The report is organized around six independent axes: **A compression/task sufficiency; B native writing/retention/capacity; C addressing/native readout; D deployed content use/causal measurement; E learning dynamics; F component composition/host transfer**. Relations, source lifecycle, closed-loop utility, byte interfaces, and source-enabled access cross those axes without replacing them. Learned multigranularity—using several information resolutions—is one candidate alongside native-state reform, objective changes, query learning, bounded adaptation, and additional computation on unchanged retained information.

All **167 original directions** remain explicit: A20/B22/C20/D20/E22/F19/I28/R16. The [report's source-linked crosswalk](REPORT_EN.md#direction-crosswalk) gives each an individual disposition and unresolved discriminator. It does not claim 167 solved questions. The practical recommendation is to identify the leading explanatory fork and run the cheapest **valid** comparison that could reverse it, only under separate authorization where needed. More names, more FULL labels, or inaccessible competitors cannot substitute for that comparison.

## 3. A — Compression and task sufficiency

**Problem:** reconstruction can preserve distinctions irrelevant to a downstream task, while continuation or retrieval supervision can omit details later required by another task. **One-sentence solution:** specify the promised task/query family, realized rate, loss, and permitted reader, then train and evaluate the representation against those distinctions. Information bottlenecks, rate–distortion analysis, continuous/discrete coding, soft summaries, and teacher-trained bridges offer different implementations.

The evidence does not support a universal objective ranking. ICAE's mixed reconstruction/continuation pretraining improves its comparison, while SAC's LM-only arm wins reported QA but loses a summarization comparison. W0067 makes priorities rate-dependent under a specified task suite; its nominal Gaussian rate, hard-code payload, empirical entropy, and fitted-reader risk are different quantities. Reconstruction can preserve useful private signal or nuisance. The useful comparison is **objective × achieved rate × consumer adaptation**, not a contest between paper headlines. [ICAE, 2307.06945v4, Table 5](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B24/W0038.md); [SAC, 2510.08907v4, Tables 5/20 pp.7/23](../03_reading/V01/BR-A01-R02.md); [W0067, 2610.30493v1, §§2–6](../03_reading/V01/W0067.md).

Finite-bit random-copy restrictions coexist with useful compact state for bounded tasks. A maximal invariant or predictive statistic preserves its stipulated task family, not every later detail. Query-first filtering is easier than writing before an unknown query. Exact identifiers, rare exceptions, relation bindings, and within-segment order therefore need separate tests: vary rarity, entropy, output frequency, and importance independently, and preserve value bags while changing roles/order. A stronger diagnostic reader recovering a target rules out complete loss for that target under its permissions; failed finite readers do not prove information-theoretic erasure.

## 4. B — Native writing, retention, interference, and capacity

**Problem:** storing new information can reinforce, overwrite, decay, or interfere with useful old information. **One-sentence solution:** treat the update as an editing policy and match it to first-binding, latest-binding, or context-tagged truth. Addition, residual correction, directional erase, independent strengths, covariance-aware filtering, partitioning, nonlinear fast weights, and predictive-state learning remain serious no-original-return alternatives.

Delta writing corrects the association currently predicted at the write key; correlated addresses can cause collateral changes. Global decay is not selective erase, independent erase strength is not an independent erase address, and extra value-side correction is not an invertible relation store. GDN's passkey and essay-number comparisons reverse rankings. Bayesian covariance can improve constructed correlated-key tasks while retaining extra statistics and supplied read keys. TTT/Titans/MIRAS expand the memory function and learning rule but also introduce fast weights, optimizer state, buffers, or local attention. [W0199, 2102.11174v3, §4.2/App.B](../03_reading/V02/W0199.md); [GDN, 2412.06464v3, Table 2](../03_reading/V02/W0201.md); [Bayesian Layers, 2605.31163v5, §2](../03_reading/V03/W0226.md).

Load, elapsed transitions, write count, distractor similarity, precision, rank, and training reachability should vary independently. Retuning/curriculum successes reject some claims of intrinsic incapacity, not finite-bit limits. Quantization buffers and low-rank bases count; low-energy directions can carry rare critical facts. Learned predictive-state identification now has direct primary evidence: **0912.2385v1** estimates state/operators under rank, coverage, and conditioning assumptions, then plans. Its 78/100 successes leave 22 failures; 18.2 actions is success-conditional. Large feature/moment/planning structures accompany five deployed coordinates. Its printed initial-state estimator also requires dimensional reconciliation before literal reproduction, without implying empirical failure. This foundational completion does not establish semantic-language transfer or arbitrary-query memory. [PSR learning, §§2–5 pp.3–9](../03_reading/V18/BR-PSR-0912.2385.md).

## 5. C — Addressing and native readout

**Problem:** adequate retained content can remain inaccessible to the legal deployed query. **One-sentence solution:** hold the bank fixed and compare native q, bounded equal-permission query/readout repair, and a stronger diagnostic reader. Learned addresses, regression conditioning, query diversity, normalization/temperature, post-read gating, and adaptive iterations address different causes.

D-NTM demonstrates learned addressing but also a shortcut through static address weights. MemN2N learns adaptive hops from answer supervision while repeatedly accessing an explicit bank. Thin Keys separates address width from full-width values, but adaptation changes its apparent penalty. Count-correct softmax merging is exact only relative to already-equalized keys; it does not match repeated residual-write transitions. QKNorm's learned-temperature ablation shows that normalized direction alone can produce poor competition. Output gates act after attention weights and need not repair matching. [D-NTM, 1607.00036v2, §§4–9](../03_reading/V04/BR-A03-D02.md); [MemN2N, 1503.08895v5, §2/Table 1](../03_reading/V11/BR-A03-D11.md); [QKNorm, formal EMNLP 2020, Table 6](../03_reading/V04/BR-A03-D13.md).

Distinct queries, distinct KV projections, resident states, and independent semantic contents are not synonymous. Compare K-only/V-only/both replacements with legal bindings and replay, then independent queries at fixed bank/return width. Compare adaptive rereading with fixed-q repeats and equally costly nonread computation. Exact cues, noisy cues, paraphrases, and relational joins need separate outcomes; oracle output rescue does not isolate q formation.

## 6. D — Deployed content use and causal measurement

**Problem:** probes, retrieval hits, answer mentions, and ablation scores can diverge from correct evidence use. **One-sentence solution:** distinguish recoverability, native return, content-specific dependence, and task-correct benefit under a declared authority policy. Natural counterfactuals, target/non-target controls, grouped interventions, and calibrated statistical tests make those distinctions operational.

Natural donors do not ensure natural hybrid states, zeroing and shuffling define different interventions, and a donor invariant in the target feature can make resampling insensitive. IOI/Hydra show compensation that defeats individual ablations. Four-cell interactions measure background-specific nonadditivity, not unique information shares or global complementarity. FaithEval's stipulated-context answers and ClashEval's fallible-source hazards have different authority contracts; answer-mention scoring is not whole-response endorsement. [Patching, 2309.16042v2, §§3–6](../03_reading/V05/W0533.md); [IOI, 2211.00593v1, Apps.J–M](../03_reading/V12/BR-A04-N09.md); [FaithEval, 2410.03727v3, App.A](../03_reading/V12/BR-A04-N07.md); [ClashEval, 2404.10198v3, Tables 2–4](../03_reading/V12/BR-A04-N08.md).

Before interpreting null use, establish competent task performance, exact replay, detectable semantic/grouped positives, screened negatives, and non-target integrity. Statistical non-rejection is not equality; the circuit-test source does not study Type II error. Follow fixed held-out content across checkpoints while accounting for selection and sparse observations. Literature instruments do not establish target-host validity automatically.

## 7. E — Learning history and changing routes

**Problem:** an endpoint cannot distinguish never-formed content use from temporary use, substitution, repurposing, or compensation. **One-sentence solution:** measure mechanism-sensitive trajectories and intervene in formation timing, not only the final component. Inference-update allocation, auxiliary supervision, curricula, initialization/rebalancing, timed bypass changes, and actual optimizer diagnostics are competing techniques.

Lagging-inference gains require substantial extra encoder work and concern stochastic variational models, not generic deterministic bypass. Strategy Coopetition finds intermediate competitor-trained components helpful while early/late ones are not; its later strategy still needs correct contextual labels. Other work permits simultaneous core/shortcut learning and persistent context-sensitive phases. Positive endpoint gradient cosine therefore cannot reject earlier starvation or harmful scale. Momentum, preconditioning, clipping, and skipped steps separate actual updates from raw gradients. Missing optimizer/RNG history cannot be recovered from citations. [Lagging Inference, 1901.05534v2, Algorithm 1/Tables 1–4](../03_reading/V06/BIW-0096.md); [Coopetition, 2503.05631v2, Fig.4](../03_reading/V06/BR-LD-N05.md); [ForkMerge, 2301.12618v3, Fig.2](../03_reading/V06/BR-LD-N04.md).

Reconstruction targets and deep supervision are different treatments. Delaying quantization can help representation formation; GenFirst gives positive generation results while doubling headline epoch exposure against some controls. Those recipes do not establish a universal stage order. Direct large-causal-LM intermediate supervision, writer–reader coformation, and complete optimizer histories remain precise primary/experimental gaps.

## 8. F — Composition and transfer

**Problem:** transplant failure can reflect recipient mismatch or missing companions rather than deficient content. **One-sentence solution:** separate intact replay, self-stitch, reset/relearning, constrained repair, and full-host adaptation, then test informative combinations within each host. Feature matching, teacher-fed local fitting, low-rank adaptation, staged migration, and companion-aware initialization are practical techniques with different permissions.

A successful supervised stitch can learn class cues rather than preserve original semantics. A failed bounded connector rejects only that class and budget. LoLCATs' two-transfer-plus-two-adaptation versus four-adaptation epochs is a meaningful total-envelope strategy comparison, not an equal-stage-dose or teacher-FLOP factorial. Convolution nearly removes one strict recurrence gap. RAM reverses rankings across stages and metrics; retain its **nominal 500M / displayed-dose 500.040M** correction without discarding quality cells. [Smith, ICML 2025, §§4–5](../03_reading/V07/BR-A06-X04.md); [LoLCATs, 2410.10254v3, Table 2](../03_reading/V07/BR-N06-07.md); [RAM, 2610.11214v1, Tables 4/6/12](../03_reading/V07/BR-N06-10.md); [audit R1 §3](../05_review/READING_AUDIT_R1.md).

Use original/A/B/AB packages, unchanged-interface continuation, wrong/constant/random content, held-out bindings, and complete adaptation costs. Repeat informative cells across hosts only after within-host validity. Acute disruption, restoration, formation, and final frontier selection remain different outcomes.

### 9.1 What the remaining uncertainty means for a research choice

Source gaps and empirical gaps have different consequences. The unread Incentive, Routing Lock-In/Farsighted Correction, Attention Placement, Predictive Scope, and two transfer bodies could change particular recent mechanism comparisons. Their absence prevents adopting their results, not preserving their hypotheses. Conversely, another exact-key title cannot supply semantic-query-family transfer, a missing optimizer checkpoint, a valid grouped causal assay, or a matched physical-bit comparison. The open-evidence document therefore names the required body, artifact, or experiment rather than recommending undirected expansion.

The mathematical boundaries are similarly local. A finite-state counting argument concerns its specified random-copy task; a useful predictive-state construction concerns its future-test family. An unlocated proof, notation conflict, or unproved extension is not universal theorem refutation. In utility theory, one complementary pair establishes a counterexample to an inappropriate diminishing-returns assumption, not global increasing returns. Shapley allocations can sum while the underlying coalition value remains nonadditive. Supported logged estimates can be useful without identifying a new continuation or future-retention policy. [Reading audit R2, §§3–4](../05_review/READING_AUDIT_R2.md).

Practical selection should retain complete recipes and honest frontiers. A staged migration can be preferable after its actual training opportunity even when its early score is worse; a source-enabled policy can be worthwhile despite larger retained storage; a local byte decoder can improve prediction without demonstrating remote trunk use. Count teacher construction/scoring, search, fitting, covariance/buffers, raw source, transfers, failed actions, and tail service alongside achieved quality. Unknown costs remain unknown. Equal steps, active state, or nominal epochs cannot certify an all-resource win. This report makes those comparisons actionable without converting a bounded positive or a missing competitor into a winner.

## 10. Conditional next decisions and stopping rule

First inventory actual artifacts and calibrate the assay. Then test same-state legal accessibility before erased-content claims; separate load/delay/precision before changing memory size; inspect fixed-content histories before a formation narrative; and compare companion-aware repair before intrinsic transplant claims. Source-policy, relation, interface, and utility experiments follow only when their contract is relevant. The decision matrix separates existing-checkpoint forward analysis, fitted diagnostics, artifact work, and new main-model training. None has been run here.

Stop when replay/positives fail, required history or treatment support is absent, the information contract cannot be matched, or the narrow fork is resolved. Preserve the remaining ambiguity instead of extending a claim. Numerical gates must be calibrated to host, task, stage, seed/exam stream, and environment; no thresholds or training authorization are frozen. The contribution is a coherent, source-linked set of competing explanations and minimal discriminators, with unresolved official bodies and local experiments still explicit.

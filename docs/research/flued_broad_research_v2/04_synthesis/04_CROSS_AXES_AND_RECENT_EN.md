# Cross-axis synthesis and recent evidence: what changes the research comparison

**Evidence snapshot: 9 November 2026.** This chapter connects the six governing axes: **A**, compression and task sufficiency; **B**, state writing, retention and capacity; **C**, addressing and native readout; **D**, deployed content use and causal measurement; **E**, learning dynamics; and **F**, component composition and cross-host transfer. Source-enabled memory and byte interfaces remain independent alternatives, not explanations into which the other axes must converge.

The [canonical 167-direction list](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/07_scope_recovery/FOLLOWUP_DIRECTIONS.md) contains 123 six-axis questions, 28 interface questions, and 16 alternative-route questions. Its [individual dispositions](../05_review/COVERAGE_RECHECK_R2.md#4-complete-original-direction-disposition--167-explicit-members), [return-aware coverage R3](../05_review/COVERAGE_RECHECK_R3.md) and [ownership packets](../02_catalog/CROSS_AXIS_OWNERSHIP.md) supply the index and current limits. This revision incorporates actual V17/V18 cards and the [official-source audit R3](../05_review/ICLR2027_SOURCE_AUDIT_R3.md), distinguishing completed public editions from unavailable anonymous manuscripts and T02's acquired-but-unread edition. It develops the intersections, especially R.01–R.16, rather than claiming 167 resolved findings or representing the 772-entry catalog as 772 read originals. **FULL describes the carrying card's source-edition reading scope, not replication or theorem certification.** Particular body, artifact, and experiment gaps are distinguished below.

## 1. The central comparison: change the cause, not just the container

A poor delayed answer is compatible with several causes: the relevant distinction was discarded; later writes corrupted it; numerical error accumulated; the native query did not address it; the returned information was bypassed; another path compensated; or the transplanted consumer did not understand the interface. Different causes require different changes. A larger state, a better objective, an adapted query, a different output head, and source rereading therefore belong in one decision space without being interchangeable treatments.

Three contracts make comparisons interpretable. First, specify **information access**: what the writer, query, reader and evaluator see, when they see it, and which original or derived objects survive. Second, specify **learning opportunity**: labels, teachers, trainable components, optimizer history, stage doses and selection. Third, specify **resources and outcomes**: actual retained bytes and precision, active accesses, compute, costs of unsuccessful actions, and the promised task family. These contracts connect all six axes; they do not prescribe a preferred architecture.

### 1.1 A × B × C: sufficiency is task-relative, and accessibility is another variable

[W0059, *Lossy Compression for Lossless Prediction*](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B04/W0059.md), **2106.10800v5, §§3.2–3.3/App. B.2–B.3**, defines sufficiency relative to a chosen equivalence relation and its invariant task family. A detail removed by that equivalence relation can later become the answer to a question outside the family. This is a change of promise, not a contradiction of the conditional result. The source also separates an optimal probabilistic consumer from what a finite trained network can learn to use. Its log-loss result should not inherit the broader appendix claims without the card's additional restrictions.

Recent [W0067, *Optimal Multitask Representations Under a Rate Constraint*](../03_reading/V01/W0067.md), **2610.30493v1, §§3–6/Apps. B–F**, makes objective choice explicitly rate-dependent. A specialist pretext can win at low rate and lose at higher rate under the stated task and Gaussian assumptions. The empirical hard-bit experiment uses separately trained bottlenecks and newly fitted probes. Its Gaussian nominal rate, fixed-length binary payload, sample code entropy and fitted-probe risk are different quantities. This changes the test from “which objective wins?” to **objective × realized rate × permitted consumer**, with task-family shift evaluated separately.

The favorable reconstruction evidence matters equally. [W0038, ICAE](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B24/W0038.md), **2307.06945v4, Table 5**, favors mixed reconstruction/continuation over either alone in its comparison. Yet **Table 3** shows very poor random-text reconstruction, and **§3.3.3** requires extra training for independently compressed segments to compose. Reconstruction is neither universally useless nor a certificate of rare-detail or binding preservation. The discriminating evaluation should vary rarity, identifier entropy, output frequency, role binding and within-segment order independently, rather than use fluent reconstruction as their common proxy.

Predictive state is a substantive no-revisit alternative. [BR-A08-R07](../03_reading/V08/BR-A08-R07.md), **institutional *Predictive Representations of State* manuscript, §§1–3**, constructs finite core-test predictions from known dynamics, preserving future-test distributions rather than every historical detail. The returned [BR-PSR-0912.2385, *Closing the Learning-Planning Loop with Predictive State Representations*](../03_reading/V18/BR-PSR-0912.2385.md), **0912.2385v1, §§2–4, pp. 3–6**, now extends this existence-only account: empirical moments, singular-value decomposition and pseudoinverses identify transformed state/operators under finite-rank, coverage and nonsingularity conditions; reward regression and planning then use the learned state. This is a **2009 foundational completion**, not an ICLR 2027 result.

Its closed-loop example reports **18.2 actions conditional on 78 successes out of 100**, with 22 failures; A* receives privileged geometry (**§5.4/Fig. 2D, pp. 8–9**). A five-dimensional deployed coordinate vector accompanies large kernel banks, moment matrices, operators and planner state. The missing comparison is no longer whether predictive-state learning has any inspected primary example. It is whether adequate exploration, conditioning, finite-data reliability and complete resource costs support the required test family, then whether that result transfers to semantic/query-family-shift language tasks. Neither this learned example nor the known-dynamics construction establishes arbitrary future-query sufficiency.

### 1.2 B × C × E: an update rule is also a learning and addressing system

[W0199, *Linear Transformers Are Secretly Fast Weight Programmers*](../03_reading/V02/W0199.md), **2102.11174v3, §4.2/Eqs. 20–28**, corrects the value currently predicted at a key before writing the residual. The learned write strength, feature map and normalization are parts of the actual operator. **Appendix B** preserves another association under an orthonormal-key example; correlated keys need not enjoy the same protection. Rebinding success should therefore be evaluated beside collateral damage, with first/latest/context-tagged truth declared in advance.

A useful cross-axis contrast holds projected keys, values and physical state fixed, then varies additive versus residual writing, key/value overlap, number of later writes and no-write delay. This distinguishes capacity allocation, interference and temporal decay without assuming that a better-trained bundled model identifies one recurrence term. Query-last relevance is particularly important: target locations supplied by an oracle or relevance available early in depth do not solve unknown importance when the content was written.

Two recent originals sharpen the resource comparison. [W0086, *On State Reduction in Linear Attention*](../03_reading/V09/W0086.md), **2602.04852v3, Tables 1/4 and App. G.1**, uses calibration and structured channel selection, followed optionally by recovery. A 50%-reduced Gated DeltaNet improves WikiText perplexity after recovery while worsening other tasks; its 4k RULER score falls **38.8→27.5** and needle score **81.3→61.9**. Batch-64 and batch-1 speedups differ sharply. Low spectral concentration is consequently not a license to discard low-energy content, and stable average loss is not evidence that removed state was unused.

[BR-A08-V23, LinQuant](../03_reading/V09/BR-A08-V23.md), **2610.39418v1, §3/Apps. C–F**, changes recurrence coordinates and factor placement before quantization. Exact-arithmetic equivalence depends on the relevant gates, transforms and noncommuting operations. Controlled experiments retain FP32 carry and a low-bit read copy; production measurements concern prefill. The appropriate follow-up is repeated low-precision state carry, long delay and actual recall at fully counted bytes—not an extrapolation from prefill quality to long-lived low-bit memory.

### 1.3 C × D: successful return and useful consumption are different observations

[BR-A03-D02, Dynamic Neural Turing Machine](../03_reading/V04/BR-A03-D02.md), **1607.00036v2, §4.3**, reports a shortcut in which trainable address vectors act as additional weights while episode content is bypassed. A read/write overlap penalty encourages alignment but does not guarantee correct content use. Its inconsistent state indexing also leaves the exact read-before/write-after convention unresolved. The useful experimental distinction is between fixed address parameters, mutable contents and recurrent controller state, with valid positive content substitutions before interpreting null ablations.

[BR-A03-D11, End-To-End Memory Networks](../03_reading/V11/BR-A03-D11.md), **1503.08895v5, §2.2/Table 1**, provides a positive example: answer supervision trains adaptive queries over multiple hops. However, each hop rereads an explicit retained bank, and the reported improvements do not isolate rereading from equal extra nonread computation. Path-finding remains difficult. The comparison should retain the same bank and distinguish one read, repeated fixed-query reads, adaptive queries and equally deep computation without another read. Extra inference depth can help without proving new information was acquired.

The recent [BR-A03-D20, *How Linear Attention Remembers*](../03_reading/V09/BR-A03-D20.md), **2609.33093v1, §§3–4/App. F**, supplies donor-write, boundary-state and output interventions in pretrained models. On its controlled four-choice task, increasing fact load hurts much more than the tested filler-delay increase. That makes load and delay separate experimental axes, not interchangeable definitions of “long memory.” Its output restoration is not a query-formation intervention. Moreover, its prose pilot fails the stated competence gate, so the precise recall intervention cannot simply be transported to semantic prose.

### 1.4 D × E: a measurement must survive both redundancy and training history

[BR-A04-N11, *Hypothesis Testing the Circuit Hypothesis in LLMs*](../03_reading/V12/BR-A04-N11.md), **2410.13032v1, §3/App. B**, turns preservation, necessity and minimality into specified statistical comparisons. Its strongest lesson is operational: the data distribution, score, ablation and random reference define the question. The authors do not study Type II error, and their known-program positives do not establish sensitivity to nonlinear conditional redundancy. **Table 1/App. E** also contain interpretation conflicts. A non-rejected null therefore cannot become evidence that content or an alternative route is absent.

History adds another dimension. [BR-LD-N05, *Strategy Coopetition*](../03_reading/V06/BR-LD-N05.md), **2503.05631v2, §§4–5/Fig. 4**, finds that intermediate competitor-trained components can facilitate formation of a context-dependent skill although early and late components do not. Later replacement and even resurgence occur in specified settings. Its later strategy still needs the correct label in context; declining exemplar mapping is not universal context non-use.

[BR-A05-N05, *Competition Dynamics*](../03_reading/V13/BR-A05-N05.md), **2412.01003v4, Figs. 7/33 and Apps. C/I**, shows improved training-distribution behavior alongside deteriorating novel-process behavior, while a context-generalizing phase persists at another diversity within the observed budget. These findings motivate fixed held-out content interventions across checkpoints and task diversity. Endpoint loss, terminal gradient cosine and fitted behavioral mixtures cannot reconstruct the mechanism's whole history. Optimizer state, noise/preconditioning and direct large-causal-model intermediate supervision remain important primary-evidence gaps.

### 1.5 E × F × C: transfer success can be practical without proving shared semantics

[BR-N06-07, LoLCATs](../03_reading/V07/BR-N06-07.md), **2410.10254v3, §§3.1–3.2/Table 2**, first fits attention outputs using teacher-fed later layers, then adapts the assembled student. Its two-transfer-plus-two-adaptation epochs versus four adaptation epochs is a meaningful equal-total-epoch strategy comparison, not a same-stage-dose factorial or equal teacher-FLOP comparison. **Appendix B.5.2/Table 21** is especially informative: ordinary adaptation yields zero passkey retrieval in the tested 8192-token cells; task-format training raises it to 100%, but longer extrapolation fails. Trainability and general retention are different outcomes.

[BR-A06-X04, *Functional Alignment Can Mislead*](../03_reading/V07/BR-A06-X04.md), **ICML 2025, §§4–5/App. A**, shows that trained stitches can succeed using cue-disjoint or class-prototype representations. The prototypes are shared across train/test; this is not fresh arbitrary noise conveying unseen labels. It invalidates stitch accuracy as sufficient evidence of identical original information, without negating connector usefulness.

Accordingly, exact replay, intact same-host stitching, reset/relearning and cross-host adaptation should remain distinct controls. Within each host, compare original/A/B/AB components and adaptation budgets, including unchanged-interface continuation. Measure correct-content versus constant, wrong and random-content dependence on held-out bindings. A successful connector may learn an answer mapping; an unsuccessful finite-budget repair rejects that repair class, not every possible interface or the component's intrinsic capacity.

## 2. Source-enabled alternatives: what the additional permission buys

**Original directions R.01–R.08/R.13; consult A.06–A.08, C.18–C.20 and D.03–D.04.** Retaining an original source changes the information contract. It is useful precisely because later queries can reach distinctions not represented in a compressed state. A fair scientific account first compares methods with the same access permissions, then compares the resulting quality–cost frontiers across contracts. It should neither credit source retention as free capacity nor exclude it to protect closed-state alternatives.

### 2.1 Flat, parallel and hierarchical access are real competitors

[W0138, ReadAgent](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B02/W0138.md), **2402.09727v3, §§3–4/Tables 1–4/Apps. H–I**, stores page gists and permits rereading original pages. On QuALITY, gist-only accuracy is **77.52%**, parallel rereading up to two pages **86.16%**, and sequential up to six **87.17%**, with strong flat retrievers included. The experiment supports valuable selective rereading under those permissions. Sequential access is not a multilevel tree, and its extra calls matter: QMSum's sequential policy can use up to six times as many requests. The reported word savings are amortized over many questions per document, not a workload-independent latency gain.

[W0162, SSA/H-SSA](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B01/W0162.md), **2604.20920v2, §§3.2–3.6/Table 3**, separates gist-based selection from original token-KV payload. Hierarchy changes ranking with training stage: at the stated 32× condition flat SSA leads after continued pretraining, whereas H-SSA leads after fine-tuning. Original KV remains necessary for expansion. The implementation also performs an all-position metadata scan during decode, so ideal hierarchical routing complexity is not the whole implementation cost.

These results motivate matched source pools, original-value fixed-fine baselines, strong flat retrieval and parallel versus serial access. Learned boundaries, aggregation, routing depth, selected payload and read frequency should be independently varied. More levels can improve selection or merely add parameters, accesses and preprocessing; more original pages can improve accuracy or add distracting context.

### 2.2 An uncertainty trigger is not observation of an omitted fact

ReadAgent explicitly reports premature decisions not to reread and failures caused by missing gist details. A policy cannot infer an arbitrary hidden distinction solely from observations that are identical in two differently answered source worlds. This is an observation-contract issue, not a new impossibility theorem about every practical omission detector: an expanded view, verification query or exploratory read may supply a separating observation.

[W0450, DRAGIN](../03_reading/V15/W0450.md), **2403.10081v3, §3/Tables 2–5/App. D**, combines entropy and attention to detect an information need after generating provisional tokens, rewinds, retrieves and regenerates. Those provisional tokens are not future ground truth, but discarded generation is real work. It improves several multi-hop cells while losing to single-round retrieval on 13B IIRC and to no retrieval on 7B StrategyQA. Its relevant-passage-but-wrong-answer example also separates retrieval success from deployed use. **Tables 5/9** leave a threshold/task-label discrepancy unresolved, not a known corrected setting.

For R.06–R.08, retain distinct denominators: confident wrong stops, missing-source cases, irrelevant extra reads, attempted but failed searches, truncations and hard-budget failures. Calibrate on held-out queries with source authority and query shift declared. Compare always/no retrieval, a strong flat policy and alternative triggers under total calls and latency. Fallback improves reliability only if the trigger detects useful cases and the fallback itself finds and uses the missing information. A search button is a mechanism, not evidence of either condition.

[W0112, MemGPT](../03_reading/V15/W0112.md), **2310.08560v2, §3/App. 6.1.4/6.1.6**, illustrates why task instructions matter: document and nested-lookup prompts explicitly state that the answer is in archival memory. Large gains over summary-only access remain useful, but they do not establish no-gold omission detection when source coverage is unknown. Neither mean accuracy nor successful examples supply the missing false-stop and full-call ledger.

## 3. Source lifecycle, placement and selective updating

**R.09–R.10/R.12/R.16; consult B.04–B.06 and F.17–F.19.** Placement concerns where an object lives; lifecycle concerns when that object remains valid. These are different from recurrent forgetting. A serving cache may retain exact old computation while a semantic state deliberately changes its contents. Both need explicit treatment, but one should not replace the other in the six-axis analysis.

### 3.1 Exact reuse, learned memory and recomputation retain different objects

[W0435, Pensieve](../03_reading/V15/W0435.md), **2312.05516v3, §§3–4/§6**, places conversation KV across GPU and CPU and recomputes evicted fragments from retained raw tokens. Its gains depend on session reuse, think times and contention; CPU cache is material. It is not evidence that a smaller latent state retained the same information. Source/model-version changes and deletion are not demonstrated protocols.

[W0438, Mooncake](../03_reading/V15/W0438.md), **2407.00079v5, §§4/6/8**, separates prefill/decode and distributes prefix-block KV. Its trace replay measures scheduling, throughput and rejection with a **LLaMA2-70B-shaped dummy model**. It does not measure semantic answer equivalence. Over half the sampled blocks are never reused, and overload still rejects requests. Cold retrieval, replication, network, CPU/SSD capacity and wasted work therefore belong in a placement comparison alongside completed-request latency.

Learned memory has additional drift. [W0143, LONGMEM](../03_reading/V15/W0143.md), **2306.07174v1, §§2–3**, freezes the encoder while training a side reader, reducing same-encoder training drift. Its exact NeurIPS target remains body-blocked; the public v1 preprint is separately FULL, with edition equality unknown. [W0144, Memorizing Transformers](../03_reading/V15/W0144.md), **2203.08913v1, §3/App. A**, retrieves old KV while weights change; normalization mitigates rather than eliminates staleness. Larger caches have flat or adverse task effects. Neither mechanism automatically handles a later model release or changed source document.

### 3.2 Maintenance requires declared dependencies and validity rules

A useful ledger connects **source version → encoded/summary object → address/index → model-layer KV → answer cache → replicas/backups**. Each edge needs its derivation rule, relevant model/configuration version, refresh permission and failure behavior. A source identifier alone does not determine whether a cached representation is valid. A model update can invalidate a derived object even if text is unchanged; a source edit can invalidate only some outputs if a sound dependency relation is available.

[W0420, *Provenance as Dependency Analysis*](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B19/W0420.md), **0708.2173v2, §§3–5**, supplies conservative dependencies for a specified query language. A marked dependency can be a false positive; unmarked independence is guaranteed only within the formal semantics. Neural summaries do not inherit this property from a provenance label. [W0425, Datalog materialization maintenance](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B19/W0425.md), **1711.03987v2, §§3–6/App. A**, shows why alternate support and recursive cycles complicate deletion and why a small source change can induce large maintenance work. Its fixed-rule result is not a general neural-cache invalidation guarantee.

[W0429, DELF](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B19/W0429.md), **USENIX Security 2020, pp. 1057–1074, §§4–6**, distinguishes synchronous root deletion, read-time descendant masking, asynchronous deletion and backup recovery. Declared edges can still miss undeclared references. Its completion-time statistics concern completed deletions, not every initiated request; recoverability is not physical erasure. These distinctions should become explicit outcomes in memory systems, not an assumed consequence of removing a source from current search results.

Selective updating also has two meanings: update selected persistent content, or skip selected computation. Reactivating skipped state requires a freshness/recomputation rule; it does not follow from sparse writes or cached prefix identity. Before claiming a selective-update speed advantage, test stale replay after source edits, model changes, partial failure and backup restoration, and measure update fan-out against full rebuild. No universal resident/external/recompute crossover is supplied: repeated-query count, update rate, reuse and hardware determine it.

## 4. Relations: separate arity, reusable results, identity and depth

**R.11; A.11–A.14/C.20/F.10.** A binary relation has two arguments; a relation between two relation results is a different composition. A persistent relation instance additionally requires a stable identity, argument roles and possibly provenance or revocation. Network depth, derivation steps, number of objects and tensor order are further independent coordinates. Collapsing them makes both positive and negative evidence misleading.

The ordinary binary baseline is strong. [W0542, Relation Networks](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/FINAL_RELATION/arxiv_1706.01427.md), **1706.01427v1, §2/§4/Supp. A–F**, evaluates shared functions over object pairs, pools their outputs and learns a readout. Pooling removes independently addressable edge records, but it does not prove that the resulting function cannot compose useful relations. [W0541, Multi-layer Relation Networks](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/FINAL_RELATION/arxiv_1811.01838.md), **1811.01838v1, §2/Eqs. 2–5**, feeds per-object pooled relational results into later pair computations. Improvements co-vary with training duration, regularization and parameters; they are not isolated benefits of persistent relation identity.

[W0538, Abstractors](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/FINAL_RELATION/arxiv_2304.00195.md), **2304.00195v4, §§2.2–2.4/§3 Fig. 2(e)**, distinguishes repeated layers still querying original objects from a second abstractor receiving the first relation state as input. The latter is a real design, but no dedicated Figure 2(e) net-effect experiment is established by the card. [W0537, relational convolutions](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/FINAL_RELATION/arxiv_2310.03240.md), **2310.03240v3, §3.3/App. B Fig. 13/App. D**, directly compares group-relation patterns; its deeper-baseline sweep is not an arity × depth × matched-state experiment. [W0539, Slot Abstractors](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/FINAL_RELATION/arxiv_2403.03458.md), **2403.03458v2, §2/App. A**, contributes object discovery and compositional rule tests with pretraining and candidate-processing costs, not a durable relation-record lifecycle.

[W0540, Neural Logic Machines](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/FINAL_RELATION/arxiv_1904.11694.md), **1904.11694v1, §2/App. B Table 4/App. D**, makes maximum predicate arity, derivation depth and object count explicit. Task-varying configurations are not a same-task factorial; the constructive finite-rule argument does not guarantee training finds the program. Graduation rates and extra action-effect supervision must accompany successful test results.

New [W0543, Dual Attention](../03_reading/V16/W0543.md), **2405.16727v3, §§2–3/App. B.1/App. C**, emits learned pair-comparison content separately from sensory values, permits nested processing and reports positive language/relational results. Relation vectors and sender symbols are pooled, not retained as independently identified relation instances. Its language model also changes grouped-query/symbolic mechanisms and is not a fixed-state memory test. Meanwhile [BR-A02-N03, Triadic Linear Attention](../03_reading/V09/BR-A02-N03.md), **2609.36529v1, §2/Table 1**, uses a third-order state that can be flattened into a joint-key matrix and costs E times the state. Its second learned key has no prescribed semantic role. Tensor order is consequently not evidence of relation objectification.

The decision-bearing experiment holds the fact pool and source permissions fixed, then varies relation-result reuse, persistent instance identity, arity and computation depth separately, with a strong flat binary baseline and matched training/state costs. Role-swapped bindings, relation-of-relations queries, identity collisions and revocation distinguish the proposed functions. Comparable performance from ordinary deeper processing would weaken the need for explicit relation objects; an isolated identity/provenance benefit would support that narrower feature. Existing evidence makes these choices testable, not settled.

## 5. Utility: local contributions do not define a closed-loop policy

**R.14–R.16, with D.11/E/F consultation.** A useful action score must state its continuation. For history h, action a and subsequent policy π, compare expected terminal task quality minus complete cost after taking a and following π against the specified alternative. Stopping immediately, following the current policy and following an optimal policy are different counterfactuals. A current-query content effect is also different from keeping an item for unknown future queries.

### 5.1 Complementarity changes which labels are worth collecting

[W0301, *Learning to Select Computations*](../03_reading/V16/W0301.md), **1711.06892v3, §2.2/§§4–5**, explicitly distinguishes myopic stop-after-one value from continuation-aware computation value. Its learned policy performs well in known-model simulations, with blinkered policies winning some cells and offline fitting amortized/excluded in the application ledger. Selection overhead can eliminate gains when it exceeds the useful computation's cost.

The elementary example retained in [W0483, *Informational Substitutes*](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B17/W0483.md), **1703.08636v1, Example 2.2.2**, explains the risk: two independent fair bits reveal an XOR target jointly, although neither alone improves optimal guessing. Under 0/1 reward, the joint gain is one-half; under information gain it is one bit. A policy trained only on immediate gain may never buy the first observation. Real information can instead be substitutable, antagonistic or costly, so one complementary pair does not establish universal increasing returns.

[W0303, adaptive submodularity](../../flued_multigranularity_survey_20261108/03_reading/B12/W0303.md), **1003.3967v5, §§3/5/App. 15**, supports greedy guarantees under conditional diminishing-return and other specified assumptions. Ordinary set submodularity or unconditional signal independence does not supply them; nonuniform-cost truncation also matters for hard budgets. The reported entropy-sign problem in W0483's proof remains an inherited source-page/proof clarification, not grounds to declare its intended theorem false. The relevant operational question is whether the selected loss, observation process and each supported history satisfy the needed assumptions.

[W0304, SAGE](../03_reading/V16/W0304.md), **2004.00668v2, §§2–3/Supps. D–E**, allocates coalition benefit with Shapley values. An additive allocation can account for a nonadditive coalition game without becoming an additive reward model for future actions. Practical marginal replacement also changes the theoretical conditional game. More Monte Carlo samples cannot repair that estimand change. Attribution, policy learning and causal intervention need separate evidence.

### 5.2 Recent exposure evidence makes support observable—and exposes missing arms

[BR-A08-V18, Causal Memory Policy](../03_reading/V09/BR-A08-V18.md), **2610.02070v1, §§3.1–3.2/Apps. C.2/H.1**, reserves randomized retrieval slots to expose otherwise unobserved memory contributions. This is valuable measurement design. However, union with deterministic top-ranked retrieval means **random assignment differs from realized inclusion**. An item can remain in every realized context; Appendix H.1 reports 90 inclusions and no exclusion arm. Constant propensity weights cancel within nonempty self-normalized arms, but that algebra does not supply missing support, correct denominators or comparable co-exposure backgrounds.

Its conditional utility is relative to displaced items. The oracle-enriched candidate pools improve required-memory discrimination while some answer outcomes worsen and deployable pool selection remains weak. A required fact can have negative marginal value without its companion. These observations change the next study: randomize the actual inclusion/exclusion treatment, log displaced content and other exposures, retain positive and negative utilities, and test future-query continuation separately. They do not invalidate every reported measurement or the estimator family.

[W0339, DynaKRAG](../03_reading/V15/W0339.md), **2607.06507v2, Method/Eqs. 1–2/Algorithm 1/Tables 1–2**, ranks valid actions using immediate supporting-document recall proxies and a separate continuation gate. That is not terminal rollout labeling. Its four-hop MuSiQue subgroup loses despite overall gains, and exact sufficiency targets, calibration and teacher-cost fields remain in an unavailable cited supplement. The complete available body is read; the work stays PARTIAL for that particular missing material.

### 5.3 Logged feedback and budgets preserve their assumptions

[W0315, doubly robust evaluation](../03_reading/V16/W0315.md), **1103.4601v2, §§2–4**, combines reward prediction and propensity-weighted residuals under support and nuisance-estimation conditions. Its bias analysis permits history-adaptive logging; a stationary-logger simplification belongs to the variance result, not every claim. [W0317, counterfactual risk minimization](../03_reading/V16/W0317.md), **1502.02362v2, §§3–4**, trades clipping bias against variance under its signed-loss and logging contract. Neither recovers unobserved outcomes for unsupported actions, nor turns logged one-step rewards into values under a new continuation policy.

[W0319, *Bandits with Knapsacks*](../03_reading/V16/W0319.md), **1305.2545v8, §2/Theorem 5.3**, compares against a distribution-aware dynamic optimum, stronger than a best fixed arm. Yet fixed per-arm distributions and additive accumulated reward are premises. History-dependent complementary facts need additional state and assumptions. Budget awareness alone does not make that model a source-navigation theorem.

Extra internal computation remains an eligible alternative. [W0302, *Rational Metareasoning for Large Language Models*](../03_reading/V16/W0302.md), **2410.05563v3, §3/Tables 1–5**, trains chain selection using known-answer likelihood gain minus output-token cost. Its mean token savings accompany numerical task reversals: 8B MMLU-CF accuracy is **36.4 versus 40.2** for STaR, with overlapping intervals. Gold labels, candidate chains and scoring belong to training costs; shorter answers are not automatically equal-quality or a calibrated online trigger.

A valid policy comparison therefore includes strong fixed and myopic baselines, complementary zero-immediate-gain pairs, harmful combinations, supported randomized continuations and actual terminal outcomes. All teacher, search, selection, failed-call and stopping costs count. Unsupported actions remain unknown, not zero-valued. No threshold or policy is fixed by this synthesis.

## 6. Byte interfaces cross the axes without resolving them

**I.01–I.28; consult A.17–A.20/B.11/C.12/D.05/E.02/F.17–F.18.** The independent [byte-interface synthesis](../../byte_language_interface_survey/04_synthesis/EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_EN.md) and its [final rigor recheck](../../byte_language_interface_survey/05_review/FINAL_SURVEY_RIGOR_RECHECK.md) preserve a field-level conclusion: input alphabet, internal positions/state, output distribution and supervision are four different interfaces. Character-composed word prediction, codepoint encoders, byte-output denoisers, merged-byte tokens and causal byte generators are not one family. Their scoped existing FULL cards remain reusable rather than newly counted readings.

### 6.1 Fresh computation is not more persistent memory

The latest [BIW-0076, Scratchpad Patching](../03_reading/V08/BIW-0076.md), **2605.09630v2, §§3–5/Appendix C.2 Table 4/Appendix D**, now has a FULL v2 card, superseding the source audit's earlier selected-v2 receipt. It adds transient partial-patch computation while retaining the same distinction between committed patch states and temporary scratchpads. It is not recurrence through earlier scratchpads, nor an external source index. A byte-encoder residual also bypasses the trunk.

At 500M/100B bytes, fixed-p8 plus scratchpad improves over fixed-p8 but remains worse than the flat byte baseline (**0.7425 versus 0.7283 BPB**). At 2B/400B, the corresponding values favor scratchpad (**0.6345 versus 0.6366**). Each cell is one run. Fixed-data training work increases; the H-Net variant regresses under matched training FLOPs despite improving at fixed data (**§5.1/Fig. 7, p. 9**). Serving latency/throughput are unmeasured, and persistent trunk-KV savings are not whole-model peak-memory savings.

This changes the causal comparison: hold boundaries and committed states fixed; compare entropy, periodic and random fresh-compute triggers at matched total training work; separately vary local bypass and aggregation. Score local byte prediction and remote-content dependence independently. A gain from fresh prefix computation need not imply better segmentation, more retained facts or useful source rereading.

### 6.2 Geometry and multilingual access need their own controls

Input/output tying, encoder/decoder input sharing and cosine versus linear output heads are different factors. [W0367/BIW-0038, Pseudo-Inverse Tying](../03_reading/V14/W0367.md), **2602.04556v2, §§3–4/App. B**, uses a column-orthonormal vocabulary matrix requiring vocabulary size at least hidden width. It is not directly feasible as written for a 257-symbol interface when hidden width exceeds 257. Its random-initialization and 590M continuation negatives also prevent treating geometric consistency as a universal improvement. Classic word/subword tying positives remain relevant controls, not small-byte theorems.

[BR-A07-N07, cross-lingual knowledge sharing](../03_reading/V08/BR-A07-N07.md), **2609.19291v1, §§5–6/Apps. D/G/H**, shows that separated lexical codes can impede measured synthetic-fact transfer and that semantically meaningful mapping helps. Its reported score is a slope ratio, not raw factual accuracy; nonlinear and linear specifications produce very different absolute estimates. Dictionary supervision and changed token counts alter both information and exposure. Shared byte inventory therefore cannot be assumed either to solve semantic alignment or to cause the observed failure.

For I.08–I.11, test identical prefixes under different future suffixes, partial blocks/UTF-8 sequences, commit/flush behavior, cache reuse, EOS and the actual output probability law. Legal UTF-8, intended characters, exact identifiers and remote semantic answers require separate endpoints, including ASCII/leading/continuation strata. For I.22–I.28, compare equal raw spans and equal compute as distinct frontiers, retain normalization/scorer versions, missing-output denominators and reversible offsets. The independent review's MtPC stochastic-wrapper, TokSuite plotting and MYTE aggregation gaps remain specific artifact questions, not blanket rejection of their other results.

### 6.3 Returned public editions sharpen streaming and supervision contracts

[BR-R2-T03, *Online Tokenization with Optimal Delay in Linear Time*](../03_reading/V17/BR-R2-T03.md), **2610.19139v1, Definitions 4.7–4.9/§§4–6**, supplies a stable-prefix comparator: emitted tokens must remain compatible with offline tokenization under every continuation, and delay is measured against that comparator, not wall time. Its worst-case conditional lower bounds concern growing tokenizer/regex families, not every input of a fixed tokenizer. Crucially, the measured implementation waits for a completed pretoken, unlike the strongest low-delay construction (**§6.2, printed pp. 40–41**). Unfinished pretokens can grow without bound; observed near-flat memory and first-token speedups therefore do not inherit worst-case delay or end-to-end serving guarantees. This converts prefix legality from a slogan into a comparator plus an implementation-specific test, including long whitespace, flush and partial-byte transport.

[BR-R2-T05, byte-prefix distillation](../03_reading/V17/BR-R2-T05.md), **2607.22334v1, pp. 3–6/App. C, pp. 16–17**, maps teacher-token mass through byte-prefix partitions with a residual cell. Conservation is distinct from conditional exactness under lossless decoding/greedy-longest-prefix assumptions; multi-teacher-token spans use a realized-path lower bound, not unrestricted byte-string probability. The whitespace ablation is decision-bearing: unmasked code pass falls **49%→9%**, while random non-whitespace masking does not recover the same behavior (**Fig. 3, pp. 8–9/App. H.2**). Headline aggregate gains coexist with task/divergence negatives, masked alignment rows and substantial generation-cap rates. Teacher full-vocabulary scoring, stopping treatment, masking and checkpoint selection belong in transfer costs. The next test is functional whole-output preservation under explicit alignment assumptions, not merely normalized local targets. These two FULL public editions do not establish equality with their still-blocked anonymous submissions.

The unresolved mixed byte-input/nonbyte-output inventory, dedicated trunk/decoder budget allocation, byte-native long-delay semantic/exact-content assay, small-vocabulary head factorial and gradient/optimizer coupling remain explicit. T05's supervision bridge does not close those architecture questions. T02's independent **2604.16037v1** has been acquired in source R3 but awaits terra reading; no robustness result or cost-neutrality claim is adopted from its title, abstract or successful download.

## 8. Decision sequence and remaining scientific gaps

The following sequence is proposed, not a preregistration, numerical gate or experiment authorization. It prioritizes distinctions that can reverse an explanation before expensive architecture changes.

| Decision | Minimum informative comparison | What remains undecided afterward |
|---|---|---|
| Is the assay meaningful? | Original task competence, exact replay/sham, known-positive content swaps, screened negatives, non-target integrity and grouped redundancy | A calibrated assay remains task/operator-specific; null effects require power, not a declaration of no memory. |
| Was tested content lost or inaccessible? | Same frozen state; native, equal-permission adapted and stronger diagnostic readers; held-out bindings | Diagnostic success bounds loss for tested targets; universal sufficiency and legal native access remain separate. |
| Is the failure writing, load, time or precision? | Fixed K/V and truth policy; independent load/no-write-delay/similarity; recurrence and physical-precision controls | A better bundled recipe does not identify a single component or solve shifted semantic queries. |
| Did formation or later substitution matter? | Fixed-item trajectories, stage boundaries, timed bypass/auxiliary suppression and restoration, unchanged/self-interface adaptation | Missing checkpoint/optimizer/RNG history remains censored; endpoint evidence cannot recreate it. |
| Does source policy add net value? | Same source: fixed-fine/strong-flat/hierarchical, parallel/serial, same-source stronger reader and equal-compute no-read | Omission calibration, hard-budget reliability and cross-workload placement still need their own outcomes. |
| Is relational structure useful beyond depth? | Same fact pool; flat binary baseline; independent arity/result-reuse/identity/depth and resource controls | Persistent identity/provenance benefit is narrower than general reasoning superiority. |
| Does an adaptive policy beat its labels? | Supported action/continuation trials, complementary and harmful pairs, fixed/myopic baselines, actual terminal utility | Current-query contribution does not decide future retention, off-support actions or new continuation policies. |
| Does the interface change semantic use? | Four explicit units; local legality versus remote facts; head/tying and raw-exposure/compute controls | Byte benefits, decoder masking, multilingual alignment and host transport remain separable. |

Three kinds of work remain. **Primary-evidence gaps** include the 17 selected official bodies, terra's reading of acquired T02 v1, shifted semantic/query-last preservation, optimizer-history mechanisms and direct large-causal-model intermediate supervision. Learned predictive-state identification now has a completed foundational source; its exploration/conditioning, finite-data, full-resource and language-transfer limits—not absence of any learning original—remain open. **Artifact gaps** include the cited construction/sufficiency supplements, official/public-edition comparisons, source-dependent scorer/offset implementations and full teacher/lifecycle ledgers. **New experiments** must identify the target host's cause, establish same-bit or same-permission quality–cost advantages, calibrate omission/fallback and test transport across hosts, histories and workloads. More titles or FULL labels cannot perform those comparisons.

The positive conclusion is a broader and more discriminating research program. Closed-state writing, task-aligned objectives, native query/readout learning, consumer adaptation, ordinary relational computation, efficient internal deliberation and source-enabled access all have credible mechanisms and reversing controls. The literature now explains which comparison could favor each. It does not yet justify choosing one from absence of contrary evidence, freezing suggested thresholds, or treating a single failure as the diagnosis of every axis.

## 3. Required extraction schema for each returned original card

A future full card must contain the following, not just a prose summary:

1. Exact stable ID/title, source version/date and official venue status with uncertainty; old W/BIW alias retained; anonymous submission identity kept anonymous.
2. Previous concrete problem; one-sentence solution; borrowed mechanisms and their role. Distinguish a mathematical framework, fixed operator, trained policy and inference-only heuristic.
3. **Query contract:** inputs at its formation time; local/prefix/bidirectional/target/task/position visibility; learned fixed vector vs input-generated vs iteratively updated; extra labels/adapters; route-q vs state-read-q.
4. **Write contract:** key/value source, temporal unit, independent writers/destinations, update order, overwrite/erase/decay, collisions and delayed future importance.
5. **Read contract:** selected slots, aggregation order, per-head independence, gate/normalization placement, repeated multiplicity; source revisit permissions explicit.
6. Rank object, condition number, key/value geometry, bits and precision; theorem assumptions including exact-key, stationary map, orthogonality, separation and sample limits.
7. Author claim vs measured result vs our inference; actual original locations, matched and missing controls, failure tasks/hosts, causal interventions, observed collateral.
8. Cost ledger: total persistent state, active accesses, factors/projections, train/calibration/teacher/labeling cost, iterative compute and communications; speed measurements tied to hardware/workload.
9. Reading scope and unavailable pages/figures/appendices. No automatic FULL because a large HTML response exists.
10. Which A03 experiment it changes, and whether it closes literature synthesis, original-reading, artifact or empirical gaps. “More literature” is not a project-local measurement.

| Layer writers vs reuse/mixing | W0287/W0289; D04 | Write ordering and source lifetime explicit; shared writers not inferred from fewer cache copies |
| Adaptive iterations vs equal compute | D09/D11/D02 | Same bank/content; repeat-same-q and no-extra-read compute control |
| Replacement × adaptation × granularity | D12/D19 plus causal content controls | Equal budget and self-interface control; compare candidate gain minus control gain; no imported thresholds |

Stop targeted expansion for a subquestion once its operator, assumptions, available negative evidence and experiment discriminator can be stated accurately. This is not a field-exhaustion claim.

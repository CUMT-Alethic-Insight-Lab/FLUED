### Evidence required from each full read

1. Exact original problem, one-sentence solution, borrowed AI mechanism and role—not merely a title summary.
2. Data/label source, causal visibility, objective, optimizer, initialization, stage boundaries and frozen/unfrozen parameters.
3. Trajectory observations versus actual formation-time interventions; native output versus fitted probes; tests of persistence after restoring an easier path.
4. Equal-step, equal-data and equal-compute controls distinguished; additional branches/probes/teachers/adaptation and optimizer resets charged explicitly.
5. Conditions of each theorem, counterexamples, failed arms, seeds, censored learning times, version differences, and which proposed local hypothesis survives.
6. A conclusion at three levels: enough to synthesize, enough to nominate a cheap discriminating test, or enough to select a training design. The third level will normally remain unestablished.

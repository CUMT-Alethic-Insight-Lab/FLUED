## 5. Minimum extraction schema for every returned card

1. Stable title, source URL, exact version/date; official forum and venue ID/state separately from arXiv; version relation and alias, anonymous status preserved.
2. Original prior problem, one-sentence actual solution, borrowed AI mechanisms and their role. Use “unknown” instead of deriving an optimizer or architecture from the title.
3. Four units: input, internal positions/state, output, supervision. Separately record codepoints, bytes, graphemes, tokenizer pieces, markers and structured action IDs.
4. Learning source: task labels, self-supervision, entropy teacher, external segmentation, morphology, distillation or offline behavioral search; estimator/bias, training stages, regularizers and what gets frozen.
5. Visibility and causality: full source vs prefix, partial UTF-8, complete/partial segment, delayed commits, future label hints, prefix revisions, EOS/flush and caching. No blanket causality proof from an attention mask.
6. Comparison controls: same host, same-host self-connection, same source text vs same positions vs same FLOPs, vocabulary/output changes and adaptation budget; positive and negative tasks/languages/checkpoints.
7. Findings at precise page/section/table/figure; author claim vs inspected observation vs reader inference; uncertainty, seed count, missing cells and unverified code/proof explicit.
8. Costs: input preprocessing, teacher and offline search, training, local encoder/decoder, persistent vs transient memory, prefill, drafting/verification, rejected work, output length, EOS and serving hardware/batch/concurrency.
9. Hypothesis distinguished and what remains an experiment. Full reading does not answer which mechanism causes this project's behavior without a matched causal test.

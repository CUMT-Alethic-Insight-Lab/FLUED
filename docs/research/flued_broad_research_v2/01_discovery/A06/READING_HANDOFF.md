## 6. Minimal reporting template for the queued readers

For each primary paper record: stable work identity and exact version; formal/submission status separately verified; source and recipient/model/task/input units; source/target/connector freeze permissions; training signal and label/teacher access; local/whole-chain measurements; same-host identity/self/reset controls; random/constant/wrong-content controls; component×companion×adaptation cells actually tested; budget (tokens, steps, teacher/student compute, search, state/precision); failure cases; original locations; and an explicit claim/observation/analyst-inference distinction.

Return an answer to a decision question, not a list of interesting mechanisms. Examples:

- Self-stitch succeeds and cross-stitch fails after restricted fitting → evidence about the tested cross-interface, not impossibility under all adaptation.
- Local fit succeeds but natural downstream behavior fails; final-feature repair rescues it → reader/chain sensitivity is a live explanation.
- Additional source branch helps over random-source same-capacity branch → useful transferred information under that contract, not free capacity.
- Damage to a proven positive host is rescued by an information-limited intervention → actual restoration evidence; contrast with a separate formation arm.

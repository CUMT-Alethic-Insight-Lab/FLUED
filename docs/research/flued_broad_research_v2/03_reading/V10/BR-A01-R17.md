# BR-A01-R17 — The Usual Suspects? Reassessing Blame for VAE Posterior Collapse

Read all main sections and supplement §§1–4, including proof steps; visually checked main Figs. 1–2 and supplement Figs. 1–4. Reading is not independent proof validation or execution.

## Problem and contribution

KL regularization, an overly powerful decoder and inference lag are often invoked for VAE collapse, yet deep autoencoder optimization itself may be difficult. **Solution:** analyze continuous Gaussian VAE loss geometry and use deterministic autoencoders as an additional collapse control, rather than claiming one universal culprit. Borrowed tools are affine probabilistic PCA, soft-threshold nonlinearities, autoencoder reconstruction, KL annealing and Lipschitz analysis. Encoder maps a complete image into Gaussian latent content; decoder maps z to image mean with isotropic variance γ. This is not a query-addressed state or a causal byte-sequence memory.

## Claims and proof scope

The authors distinguish unused extra latent dimensions, overly large fixed γ, learned heteroscedastic outlier degeneracy, expressive-decoder non-use, and poor local minima (§3). In affine mean, isotropic γ and stipulated rank assumptions, they summarize earlier benign local-minimum results; fixed excessively large γ can suppress meaningful dimensions. **Proposition 1** constructs, for any α>0, a soft-threshold decoder πα(Wz)+b and suitable data with a collapsed bad local minimum even with learnable γ (§4). Supplement §2 specializes to n=2, d=2, one latent, points ±(1,1); it bounds Gaussian tails, exhibits a sequence of noncollapsed solutions with energy tending to −∞, and argues local behavior via Hessian and a flat W subspace. The main proposition's wording says a *global minimum* perfectly reconstructs, whereas the supplement's exhibited comparison tends to −∞ as variance tends to zero; existence of a finite attained global minimum is not established by that displayed construction. The supplement's gradient argument along the W-only slice after a degenerate Hessian is not independently verified here as a full joint-neighborhood proof. These are source/proof-scope cautions, not proof that its local-minimum claim is false.

**Proposition 2** assumes Gaussian model, Lipschitz gradients and a nondegenerate decoder; supplement §3 introduces an adjustable latent scaling, derives a locally relevant surrogate and finds sufficiently large finite γ favors a collapsed comparator, with capacity to output a constant. This does not say every trained deep VAE must collapse. The AE-error lower-bound relationship used for the practical argument is described as typical and is not an unconditional theorem for arbitrary optimization (§5 and footnote 4). Supplement §4 gives collapsed stationary-coordinate examples; a stationary point need not be a minimum.

## Empirical scope

Fashion-MNIST fully connected widths 512 and 1–10 layers; CIFAR100 convolutions; residual models across SVHN/CIFAR10/CIFAR100/CelebA. Main Fig.1 shows worsening reconstruction at larger depths, including some residual cases; Fig.2 reports deeper CelebA VAE encoder variances concentrating near 1, not direct task utility. KL annealing improves some curves but does not eliminate deeper degradation. Supplement §1: Adam, batch 64, **250k updates**, LR .0002 halved every 100k, 64 latent dimensions; 5 repeated Fashion-MNIST trials shown. At depths 2→10, IWAE MSE **4.58→12.98**, flow VAE **4.79→11.15**. Budget is equal iterations, not equal FLOPs, optimization convergence, or matched parameter counts; errors can also reflect plateaus, not proven bad minima at every run.

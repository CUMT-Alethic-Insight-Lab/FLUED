## Problem and solution

**Problem:** full KV projections per attention head give a large growing cache, but completely shared KV may constrain specialization. **Solution:** train a shared full-rank K and V base plus per-head low-rank residuals, retaining independent per-head queries (§3 Eq. 2–3). Borrowed low-rank factorization is an architectural parameterization optimized jointly from scratch, not a post-hoc frozen-model adapter. Per token/head, shared K,V plus low-rank features are written; queries read all prior causal positions, and outputs mix with the ordinary residual and FFN pathways. Content remains represented at each token position; no temporal merging or source revisit is proposed.

## Objective, actual resources, theory

Decoder-only next-token pretraining on FineWeb-Edu: 100B tokens for 128M, 50B for 1.2B/2.5B/6.3B, 2048 context, eight H200, Muon plus AdamW, cosine warmup; subsequent one-epoch 568K-example supervised midtraining includes SmolTalk, MMLU auxiliary train and GSM8K (§4, Appendix D). Training materializes full per-head K/V; the proposed decoder cache stores shared head-width features plus H rank-r features, 2L(dh+Hr) elements per layer (§3 Eq. 4–9). Model parameters include both factors and may increase or decrease; Eq.10 gives 2d dh + 2Hr(d+dh). Cache allocation is not measured full serving or temporary memory.

Associative query and value algebra §3 Eq. 6–7 is exact for the *trained factorized operator*, not a guarantee of approximation equality to separately trained MHA. The v3 RoPE statement in §3 distributes rotation through head-specific B as written; without compatible dimensions/commutation or a special implementation, equality and exact cache savings require verification, not an inferred universal guarantee. Appendix F both recognizes absorbed-query MLA and describes T-dependent latent expansion unless fused; compare actual matched kernels before ranking latency. Its cache-shape `numel × element_size` measurements (Appendix G) do not measure end-to-end bandwidth or achieved speed.

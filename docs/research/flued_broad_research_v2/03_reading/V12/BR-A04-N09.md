# BR-A04-N09 — Interpretability in the Wild: a Circuit for Indirect Object Identification in GPT-2 Small

**Status: FULL**, [arXiv:2211.00593v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.00593v1), 2022-11-01; this is an early preprint, no later version/formal-edition equivalence asserted. Local [PDF](sources/BR-A04-N09.pdf), [text](sources/BR-A04-N09.txt). Main body and Apps.A–M read; PDF pp.10–11 and 24 checked visually. Figures are interpreted at the level required by the claims below, not digitized exhaustively.

**Problem → solution.** A behavioral score alone does not expose the path by which GPT-2-small identifies the indirect object in templated sentences. The authors trace paths backward by patching head outputs from counterfactual inputs, organize 26 head/position nodes into seven groups, then test fidelity, completeness and group-conditioned minimality. Borrowed techniques: causal mediation/path patching, activation replacement, mean knockouts, group ablations, greedy search, induction-head and embedding projections. No training of a new language model or supervised probe is involved.

**Method and permissions (§§2–3, App.B).** Inputs are 15 hand-written templates plus ABBA/BABA swaps, sampled from 100 single-token names and 20 single-token places/objects (App.E); baseline 100,000 items yields logit difference 3.56, IO-over-subject 99.3%, correct-token probability .49. A head/position is a node; content (which name) and position signals are distinct. Original IOI sentences have two names, donor distribution `pABC` uses three distinct random names, preserving grammar templates but removing the duplicate-name structure. Knockouts replace activation with template-matched pABC mean, not zero. Path patching freezes other attention heads at original activations, patches a donor sender, recomputes MLPs/layer norms and saves selected receiver activations, then recomputes forward using those receivers; it is a hybrid internal intervention, not natural inference. Path averaging uses >200 input pairs (App.B).

**Results and countercontrols (§§3.4–4; Apps.F,J,K,M).** Three name movers ablated together reduce logit difference only ~5% as other heads compensate; eight backup heads selected after knockout using >2% effect threshold. Single-head null effects therefore do not establish irrelevance; compensation is an observed property of this host and assay, not universal repair. For average logit difference F, fidelity is `|F(M)−F(C)|=.46`, about 87% of original 3.56 retained. Completeness asks `|F(C\\K)−F(M\\K)|` small for *every* subset K of circuit; since exponential search is infeasible they sample random sets, whole functional groups and greedy sets. Random/groups appeared near-complete, but greedy mixtures expose gap up to 3.09 (87% of baseline), so the full circuit **fails a stronger test**. Minimality asks for each node v some K excluding v where `|F(C\\(K∪{v}))−F(C\\K)|` is nontrivial; selected class/other sets yield at least 1% original logit difference, not unconditional individual necessity. App.K gives the actual sets, but Fig.20's class labels appear shifted relative to the head identities in Fig.2 (e.g. head 5.5); use the head IDs and sets rather than silently trusting that class column. Greedy search uses 10 runs with k=10 candidates for full and k=5 for naive, keeping top five sets (App.M). These are searched interventions; no independent held-out names/templates for circuit selection versus evaluation is established in this paper. Natural-appearing duplicate-IO attack raises wrong-name preference to 23.4%, while adding a subject occurrence gives .4%; these are templated controls, not diverse in-the-wild semantic proof. All later MLPs knocked out jointly yield −1.1 logit difference even though individual knockouts often appear harmless (App.J).

**Limits and directions:** D.11–D.15/E.21, D.16–D.17/D.20. Circuit explanations concern specific attention positions with MLPs/layer norms mostly held outside the circuit, logit difference rather than full semantic outputs, and a fixed counterfactual reference distribution. Preserve later robustness critiques as qualification, not as proof the original protocol never controlled backups. Pair individual with justified grouped/null/random and held-out natural-input interventions; compare native readout improvement or objective/state reforms without source reaccess. Hypothesis changes if preregistered independent templates/names and task-valid semantic outcomes confirm group contributions with matched perturbations, or if alternative groups reproduce effects.

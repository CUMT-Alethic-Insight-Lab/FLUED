# BR-A08-V18 — Causal Memory Policy

**Reading:** FULL public [arXiv 2610.02070v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02070v1), 2026-10-01, §§1–5 and Appendices A–K including proof, design, costs and disclosed discrepancies; [PDF](sources/2610.02070v1.pdf), [text](sources/2610.02070v1.txt). Anonymous [ICLR 2027 submission](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Gj3NCR6Hge) is separately audited, not established accepted or equivalent. Not independently replicated or a proof certification.

**Problem → solution.** A memory absent from retrieved context can appear to have zero utility because its contribution was never exposed. Reserve retrieval slots for randomized known-propensity exposure and compare outcomes with and without a memory (§§2–3). Borrowed structural causal models, randomized experimental design and self-normalized inverse-propensity estimation. Generator is frozen; this is measurement, not a learned recurrent-state readout or verified deployable retention policy.

**Estimand and constraints.** Memory is a stored conversational turn, address is a ranked text item, context budget B=6. Authors' store-utility factorization requires their fixed-size substitution convention: with/without the item differs by replacing a marginal ranked memory and generator sees retrieved texts plus question. Conditional value is therefore relative to the displaced item and other context, not intrinsic importance. In a balanced exposure schedule nm is fixed, so the Hájek ratio collapses to the difference of sample arm means; App. C.2's claimed exact variance requires independent draws with a fixed store. Evolving sequential stores cause autocorrelation; do not export the formula unconditionally.

**Important internal design caveat.** Eq. 5 unions randomized E with deterministic top-(B−k), so exposure can overlap top slots and produce fewer than B distinct items; more importantly Z is defined as *retrieved*, not merely assigned. A highly ranked item can occur in every realized context even when not assigned. App. H.1 explicitly gives 90/90 inclusion and no exclusion arm. Thus the claim that assignment alone guarantees both realized treatment arms and nm=ΣZ, and the unconditional unbiasedness proof based on that equality, do not hold as written for the union design. This is a source-level tension, not a proof that every reported estimate is wrong. The per-query LoCoMo design uses k=B and removes deterministic slots; even there assignment independence, background co-exposure, potential-outcome stability and both arms matter. Store randomization may surface low-ranked items with positive but tiny probability (C.1 Remark 1); finite non-observation is not automatically mathematical probability zero.

**Results and limitations.** LongMemEval independent subset: 30 items, 113 required memories; 54.0% had no observed inclusion under the tested store design. Store versus exposure AUC 0.542→0.664; 90 draws, k=2, pool ≈32, oracle-includes required memories (Tables 1–2, E.4). Exposure displaces useful evidence: independent-subset mean token F1 0.223→0.197, p=.45, concentrated harm on some items (H.3). Required evidence can have negative conditional marginal value without companions (H.1). LoCoMo per-query AUC 0.784 versus pooled 0.581 on the specified subset, but transfer to held-out queries is weak; tested deployable pool selectors nearly miss required items. One-sided abstention is Bayes-optimal only for the stated normal posterior and asymmetric linear-loss/horizon model, not all retention policies. One-sided gold-loss 5.2 vs store 10.9 per 100 slots is not evidence of closed-loop answer benefit. Author reports $193.06 / 80,187 calls, with earlier uninstrumented exploration excluded; Mem0 depth 20 is not top-6 comparable.

**Directions:** D.03/R.06–R.08/R.14–R.16. Independent paths include objective/reader adaptation, robust no-eviction fixed state and non-retrieval memory; no external retrieval solution is assumed necessary. Revisit if legal non-oracle candidate selection plus assigned-vs-realized logged propensities, both-arm support, interaction-aware randomized backgrounds and end-to-end closed-loop future-query evaluation preserve the benefits at matched generation cost.

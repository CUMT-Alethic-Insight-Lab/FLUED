# BR-A08-V19 — When Errors Become Memories

**Problem → solution.** Incorrect answers can be incorporated into later memory or shape later user questions. The authors use a dynamic structural causal model and 2×2 counterfactual entry intervention to separate these two pathways and their interaction (§§3–4). Borrowed causal mediation and factorial interventions; no new model training objective. At injected turn matched correct/error answers are passed independently to the memory updater and simulated next-question generator, producing Tcc, Tec, Tce, Tee; thereafter each trajectory's states/questions/responses evolve independently. The two main effects are relative to clean-clean, not the same as interventions in the other background; the interaction is Yee−Yec−Yce+Ycc.

**What is observed.** Four outcomes: erroneous proposition in memory, in natural response, in response to a fixed cross-trajectory diagnostic question, and token-length-normalized erroneous-versus-correct log-probability in bits. Natural response absence is not proof of state erasure. Intervention uses generated matched answers, counterfactual states and simulated users, not natural longitudinal outcomes; precise construction and recomputation cannot be fully audited without the cited supplement. Outcomes Y_M/Y_A/Y_C use a proposition-level model judge; correctness labels, normal outputs and probability preferences are distinct.

**Comparisons and budget.** 144 constructed 15-turn interactions, 24 each of six categories, injected at turn 3 then 12 rounds; retain only successful corruption *and* clean-memory counterfactuals: 125 valid for Qwen2.5-7B, 120 for OLMo3-7B, limiting generalization to all attempted cases. Question simulation, pair construction and primary judge use Qwen2.5-32B; 5,000 instance bootstrap resamples and 8 H200 GPUs (§5). Total natural-answer effect at k=1→12 falls 0.584→0.040 and 0.642→0.067 respectively; memory/probe traces are more persistent. GLM rejudges 35,280 outcomes, GPT stratified 900; agreement and direction remain imperfect (Table 1). MemoryBank-style evolving store versus top-3 lexical episodic retrieval retain errors differently (Fig. 4). Repair after k=3 on Qwen uses restored clean counterfactual dialogue and/or memory: residual probe effect 0.178 no repair, 0.129 question, 0.053 memory, 0.003 joint (Table 2); these oracle restorations have access/counterfactual costs and are not ordinary deployment repairs or independent reconstruction.

**Directions:** D.11–D.14/R.10/R.14. Independent options include robust updater, read-time contradiction handling, repair of dialogue state, and a closed recurrent state without external item retrieval. Hypothesis changes if pre-registered valid-and-invalid injection cohorts, blinded evaluator validation, genuinely naturally generated interaction and independently budgeted repair preserve or reverse pathway ordering. Missing supplement remains a substantive FULL blocker, not a claim it does not exist.

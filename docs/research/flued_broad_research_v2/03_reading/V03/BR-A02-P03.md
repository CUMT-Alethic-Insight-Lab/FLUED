**Question and solution.** Whole-cell comparisons confound local mixing, recurrent transition and optimization; factorially toggle convolution, rank-one transition and decay at matched matrix-state elements, then train with gap curricula. Borrowed AI techniques: masked-token cross-entropy, delta and Mamba-style transitions, depthwise causal convolution, distance and success-gated training schedules.

**Objects, timing, supervision.** §3 pp.3–4: masked MQAR supplies a table of K=8/16/32 fresh key–single-token values followed by queries; no stable key/value mapping. Haystack uses four entries, distractors, then a masked query answer; collision variant reuses table keys with new distractor values and defines **first binding** as correct. A bidirectional masked denoiser's reverse stream sees the query before the haystack; forward-only is causal and cannot inherit that permission. Recurrent state holds written content, projected q addresses a read and output fills the mask; local convolution adds recent-input storage despite paper's 'zero recurrent state' phrasing. Rebinding permutes associations and scores the original value: correct binding should fall. Floors: 1/26≈.038 marginal, ~.068 no-binding; collision marginal 1/54≈.019 and positional no-binding ~.26 (App.C).

**Results and budgets.** §3.2: d=32, one layer, 1,024 recurrent elements per head group, ~28.9k vs ~29.3k parameters; matching elements is not complete resident bytes, local cache or read cost. §4.2–4.3/Table 1: convolution toggles .518→.983 and .151→.592 at matched LR; retuning {3e-4,1e-3,3e-3}, five seeds, changes delta no-conv to .870 versus .999 with conv, diagonal no-conv .172. Official fused Mamba vs armed GDN, state matched, tuning {1e-3,3e-3,1e-2}, five seeds: .955 vs 1.000, not .592 vs .983 as cross-implementation comparison (§4.3). Hardened rank-vs-diagonal within-cell gaps .191/.319 at K16/32 shrink to .034 with convolution, and to ~.002 tuned. Rebinding/Table 3 supports dependence on actual bindings, not calibrated long-term semantic use.

§5–7/Tables 4–6: 2-layer query-last haystack near chance at 64/128/256 despite some high-load MQAR success. L=64 1/10 dense-only versus 7/10 distance curriculum seeds exceed .8; adding dense supervision gives 6/10. At L=256 shaped ramp 4/5 vs pooled 0/9 uniform trials across budgets/widths/LRs; at 512 success-gated 6/6 vs time-ramp 0/6, one-sided test. **These longer lengths are trained at those lengths, not extrapolation.** Under collision, 3/10 bidirectional vs 1/10 causal; shaped curriculum 9/10 each. S5 guardrail improves with convolution but absolute accuracy declines to .087 at depth16 (Table 7), not cost-free universal state tracking. Model/tokenizer/data and compute differ from language-model deployment.

**Source conflicts, theoretical and causal limits.** Same v1 §4.2 p.5 reports decay .561 vs .518, p=.86, replacing the earlier −.32 estimate; §10 p.12 still claims −.32. §9 says no length extrapolation (correct), while §10's L≥256 pending-ramp sentence is stale relative to §6's actual L256/512 training results; §10's apparent directional advantage conflicts with §7 null/parity. Do not silently pick the summary. Authors' interference/sparse-supervision explanation is consistent with curriculum success, not a proof of the exact write policy or absence of early decay; floor effects can mask length slopes. State-matched across two different tasks does not prove an eightfold physical spare capacity. §4.2/W0088 no-conv discrepancy is task/cell/tuning-dependent, not reconciled by fiat.

**Discriminator.** Cross matched exact cell/generator with equal per-arm learning-rate search, load × delay × distractor similarity independently, no-write gaps, first/latest-binding truth and rebinding; count all persistent/local state, failed seeds, training exposures.

**Problem → solution.** Updates for fixed-state sequence memories obscure their assumptions; specify a linear-Gaussian auxiliary design model and derive a Kalman-filtered memory mean plus covariance, with uncertainty-directed corrective writes (§§2–3). The borrowed technique is Gaussian filtering/online regression. The design model conditions on input-generated k,v and dynamics; it is not a learned generative model of raw tokens or evidence that the model's uncertainty is calibrated to reality.

**Equations and objects.** For current token xt, learned functions produce key k, value v, dynamics A, positive observation-noise scale and nonnegative process-noise scale (Definition 1). State mean M (D×m) stores key/value content; dense P (D×D shared across value columns) is the additional uncertainty state. Prediction Pbar=A P Aᵀ+ℓ²I; u=Pbar k; beta=(r²+kᵀu)⁻¹; M=AMold+beta u(v−(AMold)ᵀk)ᵀ, P=Pbar−beta uuᵀ (§2.2, App.B). Current q reads Mᵀq after the update; no source return. The posterior and Bayes-optimal squared-error readout are exact **only under the stated model, Gaussian independent columns/shared covariance and supplied coefficients**. Resetting **predicted** covariance Pbar to λI at each step recovers a delta-rule-shaped correction but discards accumulated geometry (Prop.2/App.A.1); a separate latent-write model yields additive updates (Prop.3/App.A.2). Proof text read, not independently checked. App.C uses FP32 recurrent quantities, structured diagonal or damped-rotation transitions, chunk histories, optional replay/checkpointing; O(Dm+D²) state plus history, not free covariance. Parallelism still has position-sequential gain and mean passes.

**Controlled recall and optimization.** §4.1, Apps.D–E: constructed unit-key target/distractor pairs share overlap, different pairs orthogonal; K=8, D=16, write content labels re-permuted per sequence, queries read supplied key with explicit state-freeze mask. Training overlap .60–.80 and ≤8 distractor writes; test at .98 and 64 gives 65.0% dense covariance versus 33.9% diagonal, other compared methods near zero. AdamW 2,500 updates, batch256 and same shared LR 3e-4, three seeds; no per-model retuning. Prediction is a classifier using frozen supplied address and learned output/value, not natural semantic query formation; random-key stress is App.E.4. Covariance geometry explains interference for specified noise/dynamics/history; it does not prove greater information capacity at equal bits.

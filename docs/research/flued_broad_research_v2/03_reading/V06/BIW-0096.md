## Question, technique and contract

**Problem:** A powerful generator can fit data while disregarding its latent variable. **Solution:** accelerate the amortized inference network early while leaving the evidence lower bound (ELBO) objective unchanged, then revert to ordinary joint training. The borrowed technique is variational inference: a Gaussian encoder approximates the generator's posterior, while an autoregressive decoder provides a reconstruction likelihood; this is not a generic deterministic compressor posterior.

Input is a whole text sequence or binarized image; encoder writes one 32-dimensional Gaussian latent, decoder receives that latent plus its autoregressive output prefix and generates the original sequence/pixels. The encoder sees the full training example; decoder prefix visibility follows its autoregressive model. There is no separate future user query or address index. Latent activity, mutual information and likelihood are indirect use measurements rather than content-specific native causal interventions. ELBO combines expected log reconstruction and KL-to-prior; text uses SGD and image Adam (§§2, 6.1, B.2–B.3).

## Observations and qualifications

- §3 and Fig. 2 track 500 samples using scalar latent posterior means: the model posterior departs from its prior while approximate inference lags, then both return. Appendix A approximates the true posterior via a grid over [-20,20] at 0.01 stride. Mean agreement is not full distribution equality; the proposed explanation is a dynamics hypothesis supported by this toy case, not a universal causal theorem.
- Algorithm 1 and §4.2 repeatedly update encoder alone at fixed generator until practical near-convergence, then update generator; every epoch validation mutual information is estimated and aggressive mode ends when it stops increasing, usually within five epochs. The MI estimator is biased (an estimated upper bound), not exact. §6.5 reports typically 30–100 inner updates; its fixed-budget Yelp comparison is single-run. This allocates additional backward passes.
- §§6.1–6.4 and Tables 1–3 compare basic VAE, autoregressive model, annealing, beta-VAE and semi-amortized VAE on Yahoo, Yelp and Omniglot, five restarts; likelihood uses 500 importance samples. Table 1 reports Yahoo NLL 328.2 versus basic 329.0, Yelp 356.9 versus 358.3; Table 2 Omniglot 89.05 versus 89.41. Table 3 gives relative aggressive/basic wall times **2.20×, 3.73× and 2.19×**, respectively, despite §6.3's broader prose “2–3 times slower”; it is 3–7× faster than semi-amortized VAE. The values do not establish matched-total-compute superiority over basic training. The enlarged image decoder prevents direct comparison with earlier image results (§6.2).
- Table 4 gives Yelp 10 inner updates: NLL 357.9, MI 1.0, 11.97h; 50: 356.9, 2.8, 29.58h; convergence: 357.0, 2.6, 21.44h. These are single runs; 70 iterations takes less wall time than 50 because aggressive mode stops earlier. Appendix E Table 9 shows that sufficiently slow KL annealing avoids collapse. Its 100k-step row reports Yahoo NLL 327.5 and Yelp 356.8 versus 328.0 and 357.0 for the unannealed aggressive row; this qualifies the prose assertion that slower annealing produces no superior likelihood. The rows are single-run results, not an all-dataset or equal-compute reversal. Appendix F's learning-rate-only trials still collapse or become unstable; neither result exhausts optimizer schedules. Appendix G changes initialization and again observes rapid inference collapse in its case.

## Research mapping and discrimination

Prior directions **A.03–A.05, A.19–A.20, E.01–E.03, E.17–E.20**. Bounded synthesis: encoder–generator update lag is a tested VAE hypothesis, not evidence of deterministic information loss or natural content use. Rival explanations include ELBO landscape, inadequate inference expressivity and imbalanced compute. Minimal future test: same initialization, dataset and overall backward/FLOP budget; encoder-extra versus decoder-extra versus joint continuation, with objective, held-out latent-content intervention and full trajectories fixed. The recovered table cells close the paper-reading gap; a quantified equal-compute verdict still needs an actual matched-compute comparison.

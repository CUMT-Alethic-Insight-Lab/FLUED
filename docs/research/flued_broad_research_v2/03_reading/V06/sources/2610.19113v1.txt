O Release

We release the code, per-item results for every ta-
ble and figure, the analysis plans, and the full ap-
pendix as a supplement PDF (the ‘suppl.’ tables
cited here). Fine-tuned weights are regenerable
from pinned base revisions and seeds and are not
released. Experiments used about 320 GPU-hours
on single H100 80GB GPUs.

P Changes to the analysis plans

Three choices made after the plans were fixed
change a reported value or verdict. The block-
ing results are re-scored (Appendix D). A cor-
rected Quechua scoring line moves the synthetic-
document comparison from fail to pass (Ap-
pendix M). The attention-matched control spans
are split post hoc. No post hoc choice or descriptive
result turns an unmet criterion into a met one. The
supplement lists every departure.

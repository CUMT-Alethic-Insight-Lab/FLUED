## Cross-axis handoff

- **Relation objectification (R.11; A.11–A.14/C.20):** W0543 shows a mechanism for sending pairwise comparison content separately from selection and for nesting relations across layers. Its symbols tag senders, not guaranteed persistent independent relation identities. Strong ordinary flat/binary comparisons plus same-fact-pool variation of arity, relation-result nesting, inference depth and physical state/cost are still needed. Reuse already FULL W0537–W0542 at their own editions, without adding six to this batch.
- **Closed-loop utility (R.13–R.16; D.11; E/F):** W0301 explicitly distinguishes myopic gain from future continuation; W0304's Shapley allocation accounts for coalition interaction but is not an adaptive policy's additive action reward; W0315/W0317 need logged support and correct estimation assumptions; W0319's budget-aware optimum assumes stationary additive per-pull reward. W0302 adds a gold-answer-based chain-selection training surrogate; its tables show aggregate token savings but also task-level reversals, an 8B out-of-domain accuracy decrease, and an incompletely specified direct-SFT comparator. Do not conflate extra computation with new source information, a supervised/oracle label with deployable observation, or local query gain with future retention. Unselected unsupported actions are unknown, not zero.
- Independent no-revisit alternatives remain in scope: state writing/retention reform, native query and readout improvements, task-aligned training, direct prediction and budgeted internal computation. No work proves all memory blind, byte interfaces exonerated, or source-enabled access unnecessary. Quality, workload, observability, legality and all training/teacher/search/service costs can reverse rankings.

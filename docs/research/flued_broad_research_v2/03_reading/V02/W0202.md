**Problem / solution / technique.** Existing gated delta rules tie the strengths of overwriting and writing; introduce separate learned channel gates in the fast-weight delta residual, retaining channel-wise decay and a WY chunk algorithm (§3). Online proximal-style objective explains this particular update; it is not supervision about which human facts are stale.

**Unit, visibility and update.** For `S∈R^(dk×dv)`, form decayed `S̄=D S_prev`, erase read `r=S̄ᵀ(b⊙k)` and value target `z=w⊙v`; update `S=S̄+k(z−r)ᵀ`, then native read `Sᵀq` (§3.1 eqs.8–11). b is key-channel sigmoid gate, w value-channel sigmoid gate, both token projections; b-modulated key changes the read direction but the left/write factor remains k—this is **not a freely projected independent erase address**. Tied scalar b=w=β gives KDA; further scalar D gives GDN. Keys and queries are L2-normalized, projected from causal input through short conv+SiLU; output RMSNorm, separate SiLU gate, projection; the hybrid adds SWA KV. Explicit decoder state is fp32 (§Appendix C.5, D.3), plus convolution buffers and any hybrid window; equal matrix dimensions alone do not prove equal total bytes.

**Optimization / assumptions.** §3.2 eq.13's exact local argmin uses a fixed, prior-state-dependent linear residual and quadratic proximal penalty; it is not the exact minimizer of generic nonlinear long-history regression. The gated factor is asymmetric; eigenvalue intuition does not alone prove a Euclidean operator-norm contraction for arbitrary b and k. The implementation uses b∈[0,1] by default; a [0,2] variant permits negative eigenvalues while w stays [0,1] (§3.1, Appendix D). Appendix A derives chunk factorization, B backpropagates gates through triangular solve; these are author derivations, not independently verified numerical tests. Outer objective is language prediction, AdamW; no frozen host/transplant.

**Comparisons.** §4 and Appendix E use 1.3B models, 100B FineWeb-Edu tokens, 4K train context, 2K SWA in hybrids, and compare GDN/KDA/Mamba-2/Mamba-3 including rank-4 MIMO. Authors match main recurrent state at 16×128×128=262,144 floats/layer; params approximately matched but gates/projections and work differ. Table 5 (recurrent-only) full vs scalarized b vs scalarized w: Wiki PPL 15.90 vs 16.55 vs 16.12; multi-key 4K accuracy 37.8 vs 30.6 vs 35.2. Runtime scalarization preserves projections, not a parameter-reduced or independent-address control. Expanded erase range [0,2] gives 15.95 PPL and 37.6 multi-key, no consistent gain. Table 4 (p.8) reports recurrent GDN-2 recall average 29.88 versus KDA 28.67; DROP is 17.87 versus 21.80 and NQ 19.64 versus 19.58. Hybrid GDN-2 average 42.28 exceeds KDA 40.14, but not every task. H100 hybrid training Fig.2 reported 38.0→36.1k tokens/s with increasing length; no general inference claim follows.

**Rival and next discrimination.** Independent strengths, channel-dependent erase direction, extra projections, modified decay and optimization can all explain improvement. Cross scalar/channel/tied/independent gates and decay parameterization at matched full bytes, parameters, FLOPs and data; use unrelated vs correlated key/value, no-write delay, first/latest binding and collateral old-fact accuracy.

Optimization. All models are trained on FineWeb-Edu
with a batch of 256 sequences and a sequence length of
2,048. All models share the same LLaMA tokenizer with
a vocabulary of 32,000 tokens for training and evaluation.
We utilize AdamW with β1 = 0.9, β2 = 0.95, ϵ = 10−8,
weight decay 0.01, and gradient clipping at 1.0. The learning
rate follows a cosine schedule that decays to 10% of its peak
value. Table 8 summarizes the training steps, peak learning
rates, and warmup steps for all model scales.

Table 8. Training steps, peak learning rates, and warmup steps of
pretraining.

Scale               130M 340M   1B

Evaluation protocol. All downstream and retrieval
evaluations are zero-shot and are conducted with
lm-evaluation-harness (Gao et al., 2021). Re-
sponses are generated via greedy decoding, with a max-
imum of 48 new tokens for real-world retrieval and 128 for
NIAH.

**Problem → solution.** Isolated multi-hop retrieval pipelines cannot jointly choose whether and how to get more evidence. Authors compose state-valid retrieve-more, rewrite, gap, bridge, sufficiency and stop actions, and train a lightweight state-conditioned controller plus optional final snippet compressor. Borrowed tools are random-forest regression, dense retrieval and LLM-generated diagnostics, not an end-to-end policy-gradient or no-source state.

**Learning and deployment (Method, Eqs. 1–2, Algorithm 1).** State contains question, retrieved documents, frontier, generated query, missing-information feedback, history and accumulated cost. Runtime sees no gold labels; training obtains gold supporting-document recall U(s). Every valid action is executed **once from each logged prefix** and scored for immediate change, execution cost β, and opportunity-cost λ relative to stopping. Sufficiency adds no evidence; it is credited U(s)+ρ(s)(1−U(s)) for a putative gap. Exact ρ construction is cited to missing supplement. A separate continuation gate learns from recorded prefix versus completed-trajectory answer gains; it does not make action-ranking targets unbiased terminal rollout values. The observed action×prefix execution is not the full history×action×continuation teacher ledger. 1,000 train questions per dataset split 80/20 by question for fit/calibration, 300-tree random forests trained in three independent runs; prefix counts, teacher calls and compute are unknown. Inference caps four control steps, at most three new docs per retrieval and 12 final docs; BGE dense index, no model retraining for transfer to two answer backbones. Content unit is document/snippet; addresses are generated queries and bridge entities, not model-native memory slots. The controller can stop erroneously after positive sufficiency.

**Results and countercontrols (Tables 1–2, Figs. 3–5).** Visual table confirms Qwen F1 .5998/.5340/.3061 Hotpot/2Wiki/MuSiQue, full vs uniform-valid .5722/.5127/.2937. Authors report matched-backbone S2G-RAG 10.1–34.3% fewer total prompt/completion tokens and 15.1–43.4% fewer retrieval calls, but these exclude model training, teacher labels, index construction and storage. Fixed-K reports prompt tokens only and is excluded from total-token ranking. Removing sufficiency also removes gap querying, so no isolated sufficiency coefficient. MuSiQue four-hop F1 .1798 versus S2G .2060 reverses the overall ranking. The reported 2×2 uniform-valid/fixed-horizon ablation is useful but not same-action one-step-greedy or full continuation optimum; no evidence that complementary actions with zero immediate support are learned.

**Gaps and hypothesis test.** Need exact v2 ρ, thresholds, calibration, prefix samples, all teacher/search/generator calls, execution cost accounting and source lifecycle. Lack of this material does not erase the observed quality cells, nor justify claiming v2 completed. Compare equal-source flat parallel retrieval, a same-observation myopic policy and terminal-rollout-supported policy, plus independently source-free recurrent/state-read adaptations at matched total cost. Reverse complementarity claim if zero-immediate-gain action pairs are systematically skipped, or actual no-gold false-stop and hard-budget failures exceed simpler alternatives. Directions R.06–R.08, R.14–R.16.

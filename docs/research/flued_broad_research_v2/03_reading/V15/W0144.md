# W0144 — Memorizing Transformers

**Status: FULL, assigned arXiv v1, 16 March 2022**, paper header says ICLR 2022 conference paper; not a certified comparison to another version. [Original](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.08913v1); local `sources/W0144__2203.08913v1.pdf`, SHA-256 `757462f39eb2b305a30738c6e96c5c3b8fb94a77f67284d900419607f14ae065`. Read main text and Apps. A–B, including ablation and qualitative examples.

**Problem → solution.** To use distant past tokens without dense global attention, the authors add approximate nearest-neighbor lookup over a nondifferentiable cache of previous transformer attention keys and values, then gate retrieved and local attention. Borrowed techniques are kNN search, Transformer-XL segment cache and per-head learned gates; learning is causal next-token likelihood, not explicit supervision of evidence dependence.

**Exact contract (§3).** Documents are sequential 512-token segments; per document/head, one layer caches previous K/V, appends after each training step, discards oldest when capacity fills and clears for each new document. The same current-token query searches local and external K, approximate top-k (k=32) feeds attention over V; gate is learned head-wise scalar, not per-token content-dependent. No position bias on retrieved memory. Approximate TPU kNN has reported ~90% top-k recall. Normalizing Q/K reduces magnitude shift of stale representations as weights change, but authors explicitly do not claim it eliminates staleness. Precision and exact ANN index storage representation are not specified in the paper passages read; do not invent a bit count. Source/raw input, cache K/V, index and transport are additional resources, not a fixed-size closed state.

**Evidence and budget (§4, Tables 4–5, Apps. A–B).** 12-layer 1024-hidden/8-head models, 32 TPU cores, generally 500k steps (100k Isabelle); 1B and 8B scaling are separately reported. On C4 long documents, 512-local vanilla PPL 17.20 versus 14.42 with 8192 memory; 512+XL cache 15.38 versus 14.04 with 8192. The 65K-memory 512+XL C4 cell remains 14.04, not an additional gain. 131K/262K results additionally fine-tune 20k steps; training with large cache can be unstable. On TPUv3 200M model step time .2s without, .25s at 8K, .6s at 65K; ANN/storage costs and full energy are not decomposed. App. A.1 shows two kNN layers do not further help, layer choice matters, and 32 versus 256 neighbors only 2.38 versus 2.37 PPL. The qualitative Isabelle analysis manually checks ten lemma predictions, eight retrievals find the body; it is selected qualitative evidence, not a general causal necessity test. Figure 7 says some tokens worsen with larger memory.

**Contamination and boundaries.** PG19, C4, arXiv/math, GitHub and Isabelle construction are described in §4.1; the paper does not supply a complete repeated-text/train-test contamination audit in the retrieved text. No per-record correction propagation, model-version compatibility, delete semantics, causal answer replacement or full storage/physical-erasure evidence is established. Theory claim is not a finite-bit no-revisit bound. Contrasting source-enabled retrieval with independently redesigned non-revisit recurrent state and a matched native query, count total cache/index/source plus compute and adaptation. Reversal: gains disappear with controlled duplicates, an equal-budget flat cache, stronger same-state native reader, or intentional content swaps fail to flip outputs. Directions R.02–R.03, R.09–R.10, R.16; B.04–B.06.

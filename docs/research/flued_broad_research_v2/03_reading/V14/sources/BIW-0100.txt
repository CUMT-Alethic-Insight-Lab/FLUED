                                                                  I. INTRODUCTION

                                           Subword vocabulary size is fixed at tokenizer construc-
                                        tion and inherited by every model trained on it. A coarse
                                        vocabulary yields short sequences and cheap inference but
                                        obscures character-level structure; a fine vocabulary exposes
                                        orthography at the cost of longer sequences. Systems that need
                                        both typically train two models.

**Problem / solution / borrowed technique.** Two augmented views may share nuisance-invariant information but not all information needed by future tasks. Add a representation-to-own-view information incentive to the contrastive objective, implemented through image reconstruction or a stochastic InfoNCE lower bound. This borrows self-supervised contrastive learning, autoencoding and variational information estimation to train visual features; no language-model memory or address is involved.

**Theoretical and training contract.** §3.1–3.3 define representation z1=f(v1), view sufficiency for v2, and idealized minimality. Downstream task T is not available during representation training. Their illustrative nonshared information I(v1;T|v2)>0 includes e.g. tracking details in video absent from corresponding audio (§3.1). Theorem 1's claimed equality relies on the extra assumption that the minimal representation discards all view-private information, I(zmin;v1|v2)=0; source supplement A invokes it in Lemma 1. This is stronger than merely having a minimum information sufficient statistic in arbitrary distributions, and text extraction is not a proof certification. Theorem 2 provides a Bayes-error **upper bound**, not strict observed ordering; Theorem 3 additionally assumes uniform, Laplace or Gaussian conditional errors. More own-input information may include noise; when relevant task information is shared and invariance is desirable, minimality can remain appropriate (§5).

**Implementation and observations.** Equation 12 reconstructs the *augmented view* using squared error and an added decoder; Eq. 15 uses sampled Gaussian feature noise and representation-level InfoNCE; neither directly optimizes unknown task labels. Random crop/flip/color distortions; CIFAR/STL ResNet18, 200 epochs, batch 256, Adam 3e−4; ImageNet ResNet50, 200 epochs, batch 1024, LARS (§4.1). Frozen-feature linear probes are separately trained 100 epochs; detection/segmentation fine-tune heads/backbone with different task permissions. SimCLR, BYOL and Barlow Twins are compared (§4 and supplement C). Main §4.2 reports a reverse-U in regularizer strength and source-CIFAR10 not benefiting from extra information; more epochs can hurt transfer. Equal epochs are not equal compute: reconstruction adds a decoder, InfoNCE adds computation, effective information/physical bits not matched. The source's statement that near-minimality follows from shared supervision is an empirical interpretation, not measured proof. Similarity regularizer controls use a fixed stochastic variance rather than unrestricted original formulation (supplement D); augmentation held to listed recipe, not an exhaustive augmentation factorial.

**Problem / one-sentence solution / technique.** A high evidence lower bound (ELBO) or likelihood can coexist with an input-ignoring latent. Decompose the variational autoencoder objective into rate and reconstruction distortion, and explore/target points on that frontier rather than judging ELBO alone. The borrowed tools are variational mutual-information bounds and rate–distortion geometry, not a new addressed memory.

**Learning/contract.** §2 defines stochastic encoder e(z|x), decoder d(x|z), approximate marginal m(z), data entropy H, reconstruction negative log likelihood D and average encoder-to-marginal KL rate R, with H−D ≤ I_e(X;Z) ≤ R. R includes aggregate-posterior/marginal mismatch and is not necessarily true mutual information or physically encoded bits. Decoder reconstructs x conditionally on z; autoregressive decoder also sees preceding target pixels during training. No future query, external address, persistent sequence state, or deployed task-use test appears. β-VAE minimizes D+βR; β=1 is negative ELBO. Rate-targeted toy optimization is D+|σ−R|; more broadly β sweep/annealing with Adam and normalized gradients, 200 epochs (§4, supplement F.4).

**Theory and evidence.** In the infinite-family discrete-data ideal (§2; supplement D.1–D.4), diagonal D=H−R yields the same ELBO over rates from autodecoding (R=0,D=H) to autoencoding (R=H,D=0); equal ELBO explicitly does **not** mean equal reconstruction, mutual information, downstream utility, or finite-model equality. Approximate prior/decoder slack lifts the finite-model frontier. The paper derives representational and distinct generative MI bounds, not a semantic guarantee. §4 toy latent has known true MI 0.5 nats: fitted ELBO rate 0.0002 vs target-rate 0.4999 and closer latent clusters; the target value uses ground-truth generative knowledge, and this is not a general identification theorem. MNIST uses 12 encoder/decoder/prior families and 209 runs; PixelCNN++ powerful decoder can ignore z near β=1, but β<1 induces use. §4 Figure 4 compares qualitatively different near-equal ELBO models. Supplement A notes a high-rate strong-decoder/weak-encoder modeling failure; B reports Omniglot best −ELBO 90.37 nats with rate 0.77 and other similar ELBOs over different rates. No direct downstream task probe establishes representation usefulness.

**Coverage and discriminating test.** A.03–A.05, A.16 and E.01: objective degeneracy is distinct from inference-network lag and from deterministic latent non-use. Minimal next test matches data, decoder and budget, compares actual content replacement/likelihood under targeted rates and stronger readers, then compares extra encoder steps at fixed training compute. The qualitative visual evidence and stated sample values do not prove an optimizer mechanism or a target-host failure; no independent numerical replication was attempted.

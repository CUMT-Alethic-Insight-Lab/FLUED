**Problem / solution / borrowed technique.** Are expensive reconstruction-pretrained special compression tokens necessary? Semantic-Anchor Compression selects original context tokens, adds a learned anchor embedding, and uses bidirectional encoding to produce per-layer key–value (KV) states. It borrows low-rank adaptation and KV-cache prompting; the learned anchors are both selected content positions and the persistent interface, not an external retrieval address.

**Learning and visibility.** §3.2/§4.1: 510-token segments; middle token of each uniform chunk by default, floor(L/r) anchors; all context tokens can mutually attend at compression time, so this is not online causal prefix encoding. The selected anchors' KVs at *every layer* are concatenated across segments and made available to a causal target decoder; state grows with document length and per-layer KV count, not one vector per chunk. Llama-3.2-1B encoder has rank-128 LoRA (α=256), decoder frozen. Pretraining uses next-token continuation, then supervised MRQA answer generation; the question is available to the decoder after context compression. 20k steps each, batch 16, eight RTX 3090; larger models use eight A100 (Appendix A/B.4). Appendix A says split 510–2040-token examples in half for AE and LM and discard AE half when absent: equal steps do not automatically equal token exposure or training FLOPs.

**Evidence and limits.** §§4.2–4.6 compare ICAE, 500x, Beacon, DAST, EPL, and fine-tuned uncompressed and hard-selection baselines across 5×/15×/51×. §4.3 says AE-only worsens SAC and AE+LM remains worse than LM-only, while 500x AE+LM has mixed effects and ICAE previously benefited; Table 5 values are transcribed above. Removing anchor embedding or bidirectional attention hurts; random selection loses against uniform/Lingua-2, but both selection and position coverage change. On 3B average F1/EM 50.48/34.73 vs EPL 47.46/31.82, on 8B 52.31/35.93 vs 50.82/34.42 (§4.4; original prose). Appendix B.5 expressly reports SAC AE+LM slightly better on QMSum whereas LM-only better on GovReport: retain this counterexample. §5.1 gradient cosine near zero is an observation, not proof of destructive optimization; §5.2 attention maps and §5.3 t-SNE do not establish correct bindings or causal use.

**Problem and one-sentence solution.** Is linear replacement of pretrained softmax attention limited by the update rule or by missing companion paths? Compare frozen-backbone replacement rules, then add disjoint local/sink attention and short convolutions or low-rank adaptation (§§3–4).

**Mechanism and objective.** Borrow gated delta-rule updates as a key-dependent rank-one correction; compare normalized Hedgehog features, gated linear attention (GLA), key-gated GLA and gated delta network (GDN). Strict arm freezes pretrained Q/K/V, normalization and backbone; only new mechanism parameters train, with no teacher distillation, no sliding window, 10M DCLM-Edu tokens, 4096-token sequences and 2,500 batch-one next-token cross-entropy iterations (§3.3). LoRA or short convolution is a distinct relaxed regime, not strict frozen QKV; LoRA learning rate is reduced to 5e-4 for instability. Appendix C says three seeds 1,2,3, same hyperparameters, no sweep; LoRA rate is the exception.

**Native data contract.** Causal token sequence supplies query/key/value; matrix recurrent state accumulates past writes and is read by current q. In companion variants, separate exact softmax attends to an explicitly retained recent window and prefix sink tokens while the linear state excludes both. The nominal fixed 64-token cache splits 56 local + 8 prefix (§4, Eqs.13–14); it is extra retained KV, not a pure fixed-state model without a local path. Output is next-token prediction; content is contextual token value, not independent semantic fact slots. Common-reasoning tasks, 5-shot MMLU and LAMBADA are downstream task scores, not direct provenance of native content use.

**Theoretical and empirical limits.** §3.2 and A.1–A.3 derive local first-order softmax expansion around zero, approximate mean subtraction by projection assuming normalized, concentrated keys, then relate products of rank-one operators to the projection. Uniform 1/t term is absent in practical GDN; residual and concentration are measured correlations, not global equivalence or proof of learned mechanism. A.3's stated product-error bound versus its series derivation warrants independent mathematical review; do not resolve from extraction. §3.3/Figs.2–3 and accompanying prose report better GDN approximation; key-gated GLA's initialization advantage largely disappears after training. §4 says short convolutions make delta/non-delta gaps almost disappear; LoRA may be disadvantaged by the reduced rate. Appendix B.4 says 128-total-token cache is not viable for retrieval-heavy subsets and 896 tokens still weak on multi-query/multi-value and variable tracking. A within-host rule × companion factorial, separately tuned at equal tokens, measured FLOPs/state/cache and teacher costs, with identical seeds and held-out long/multi-item tasks distinguishes update-rule advantage from companion and optimization advantages.

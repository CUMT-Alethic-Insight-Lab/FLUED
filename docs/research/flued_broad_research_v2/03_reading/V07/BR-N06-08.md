**Question and solution.** How to transfer a pretrained Transformer's expensive sequence mixing into an efficient state-space architecture? MOHAWK aligns teacher/student mixing matrices, then local block outputs, then full-model output distributions (§4, Fig.2).

**Borrowed techniques / optimizer.** Teacher–student distillation and Mamba-2 structured state-space duality supply the mixer and three supervision levels: teacher-fed per-layer matrix Frobenius loss, teacher-fed block L2 loss, then whole-model teacher-logit cross-entropy (§4.1–4.3). Stage 1 initializes convolution to identity; Stage 2 opens gate at one and removes incompatible pre-output normalization; post-convolution activation is removed. Student has multi-head rather than multi-value structure and discrete-time parameterization. Embeddings, output head, MLPs and input/final normalization are copied for Stage 3; full model can be tuned, and a frozen-non-mixer variant is also tested. Hybrid preserves four original full-attention layers and their growing KV cache, not constant total memory (§4.4, §5.6).

**Inputs and state.** Causal input/prediction units are text tokens; teacher attention at each position mixes earlier value vectors and student recurrent state writes and reads input-dependent projections. Stages 1–2 see teacher hidden states, not the student's naturally accumulated upstream errors; stage 3 sees the assembled student. Output LM/benchmark scores measure behavioral recovery, not direct proof of natural use of a particular fact.

**Results and budgets.** §5.1/5.5: final Phi-Mamba uses C4, length 2048, 80M + 160M + 2.76B = 3B tokens; hybrid uses 5B. Teacher Phi-1.5 is 1.3B, student 1.5B. Hybrid mean 66.0 vs teacher 67.2 and pure student 65.1 on specified tasks; pure student WinoGrande 71.7 vs Mamba-2 60.9, ARC-C 44.1 vs 33.3 (intro/§5.6). Appendix A.4 warns Samba metrics are unnormalized for ARC-C/HellaSwag while theirs are normalized. Table 3 explicitly uses 5B tokens for each stage strategy; this is token-matched, not equal training FLOPs or teacher-pass cost, and its 5B-token Phi-Mamba averages are 53.3 for stage 2, 54.5 for stage 3, 62.3 for stages 2–3 and 62.7 for stages 1–3 (Table 3, p.9). Phi→Phi control resets attention and relearns it rather than replaying intact: §5.4 says stage 2+3 alone does not fully recover teacher while all three do. Fig.3 compares stage-2 allocations against direct stage-3 at matched cumulative tokens; optimization/hyperparameter and teacher-cost equality remain unproven. Section 5.7 and Appendix C compare fitted matrices per sample: 1000 samples × 512 tokens, one sampled head/layer, 10,000 optimization steps and tuned LR for some families; this is an offline expressivity diagnostic, not the deployed causal reader.

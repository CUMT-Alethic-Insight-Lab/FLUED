## 1. Decision: broad rebuilding can proceed; universal closure cannot

**Yes: the evidence now supports rebuilding a genuinely broad report without making multigranularity, source revisiting, or bytes its governing explanation.** The main structure must remain A compression/task sufficiency, B native state writing/retention/capacity, C addressing/native readout, D deployed content use/causal measurement, E learning dynamics, and F composition/host transfer. Byte interfaces and source-enabled alternatives are independent, explicit comparisons across that structure, not the destination to which every failure is redirected.

This is a stronger go-ahead than the [original scope audit](ORIGINAL_SCOPE_AUDIT.md), but a narrower statement than “all directions answered.” Three material changes justify it:

1. **Missing originals now change competing explanations.** V01/V10 supply objective and decoder countercontrols; V02/V03/V09/V11 supply update, capacity, query, precision and iteration evidence; V05/V12 strengthen the measuring instruments; V06/V13 retain formation, replacement and plasticity explanations; V07/V13 distinguish practical transfer from common semantics. These are not merely more recent architecture names.
2. **The previously endangered cross-cutting questions have explicit routes.** [Ownership](../02_catalog/CROSS_AXIS_OWNERSHIP.md) and V15/V16 preserve source placement/lifecycle, omission/fallback, relation representations and sequential utility. They are no longer wholly unowned omissions. Routing is not evidence closure or proof that a synthesis owner has accepted and delivered a packet.
3. **Negative results survive alongside positives.** Higher latent KL need not improve likelihood; tensor order need not mean relation objectification; multiple hops need not isolate rereading; a naturalistic intervention can be wrong; a useful early competitor can later displace a skill; a successful stitch can learn class cues. These reversals prevent one preferred architecture from organizing the conclusions.

**Release position:** proceed with bounded broad synthesis and explicit unresolved entries for all original directions. Do not release a report claiming universal unknown-query retention, calibrated omission detection, a uniquely diagnosed target-host failure, equal-resource architectural superiority, or complete ICLR 2027 coverage. None follows from the reading program.

The most consequential remaining **primary-evidence** gaps are learned predictive-state identification beyond known dynamics (B), shifted semantic/query-last accessibility and exact binding preservation (A/B/C), and optimizer-history/large-causal-LM intermediate-supervision evidence (E). D has strong conceptual instruments, including a newly completed statistical-test original, but unresolved test-power/reference assumptions and unvalidated target-host semantics. F has substantial positive and reversing evidence; its largest residual is controlled host transport, not a need for another generic stitching title. Details and claim-dependent exceptions follow.

### 2.3 Meaning of “decisive” in this review

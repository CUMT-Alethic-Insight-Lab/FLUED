## 6. Claims the final report must not make

- **Coverage/novelty:** complete ICLR 2027 coverage, acceptance, an exhaustive recent-state-of-the-art comparison, absence of a competing method, or scientific novelty inferred from catalog absence. Neither the original 25-record union nor first-page keyword searches are a venue census.
- **Source equivalence:** an official body was read because metadata were verified; note v2 means PDF v2; a matching preprint is the anonymous manuscript; a missing supplement does not exist; a 403 proves no lawful public original exists.
- **Compression/state:** universal superiority of reconstruction/continuation/discrete codes; unrestricted exact future-query retention; universal “capacity, not optimization” or the reverse; rank/energy/parameter count as a complete usable-bit budget; higher-order tensor state as persistent relation identity.
- **Reading/use:** probe, oracle, teacher-fed local fit or learned stitch success as spontaneous native access; answer mention as semantic use; output patch rescue as q-formation proof; an insensitive null ablation as no memory; source hit or context dependence as task-correct benefit.
- **Dynamics/transfer:** endpoint cosine diagnoses all history; a helpful bypass must always help or always harm; finite-horizon failed recovery proves irreversibility; failed bounded repair proves an intrinsically defective component; adapted compatibility guarantees transported original semantics.
- **Resources/policy/interfaces:** equal tokens/epochs/active state implies equal raw exposure/FLOPs/total bytes; fallback calibrates omission; local randomized assignment ensures actual treatment support; one-step utility determines future retention; tokenizer TTFT is end-to-end serving latency; mass conservation is exact unrestricted byte probability; valid UTF-8 or lower BPB proves remote content use.
- **Target host:** literature identifies the cause of a FLUED failure, authorizes an experiment, selects the architecture, or freezes suggested budgets/thresholds. None follows from this review.

These exclusions do not require stripping positive findings. Conditional PSR identification, useful selective rereading, learned adaptive queries, reconstructive pretraining, retuned recurrent recall, staged migration and useful supervised connectors can all be reported at their actual permissions and tested scope.

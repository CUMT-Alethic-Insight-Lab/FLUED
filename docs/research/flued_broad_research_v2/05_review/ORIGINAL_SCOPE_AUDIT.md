**Candidate preference finding:** I find no explicit retrieval-first inclusion rule in the current eight findings/queues. They repeatedly offer independent state, objective, reader and adaptation alternatives. It would be inaccurate to say the original architecture preference still governs them unchanged.

There are nevertheless three selection pressures:

- **Legacy experimental anchoring:** A03's detailed block-replacement/broadcast designs and A06's transplant designs understandably follow known project failures. Unless balanced with unknown-query retention, delayed semantic tasks and training-history studies, they can narrow the governing question to repairing one surgery.
- **Recent recurrent-recall salience:** A08's top queue heavily favors contemporary linear-memory/recall mechanisms. “Not capacity” titles are hypotheses, not findings that abolish finite-bit, task-family or precision constraints. Toy MQAR, vision readout and exact-lookup controls cannot silently stand in for semantic, relation, rare-detail and language-interface coverage.
- **Overcorrection against the old proposal:** repeated “no source revisit required” statements correctly restore eligibility. They are not a reason to drop source-enabled alternatives, relation processing, maintenance, or full cost accounting. The current missing cross-cutting handoffs create this reverse-bias risk. Source-enabled methods should compete under an explicitly larger information-access contract, not be excluded or rewarded automatically.

## 8. Minimum source coverage before stronger v2 writing

### 8.1 A claim-dependent source contract, not a numerical target

For each original direction that receives a substantive conclusion, the writer needs an evidence packet containing:

- **A direct primary mechanism/problem source**, already available at sufficient scoped reading depth, with immutable edition and exact location.
- **The most discriminating control or counterexample**, preferably within that primary or an independently relevant original; not merely a second paper agreeing with it. Positive and negative evidence may come from one work and must not be counted as independent replication.
- **Actual permissions and learning contract:** writer/query timing, visible targets/teachers, input/internal/output/supervision units, saved source/state, update/read order, frozen/trainable components and adaptation regime.
